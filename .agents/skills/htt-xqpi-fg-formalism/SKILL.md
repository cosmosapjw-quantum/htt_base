# htt-xqpi-fg-formalism


Bind the actual carrier before choosing a scalar summary: retain full tensor or
field-valued departure data, component conventions, joint covariance/law and sky,
depth or parameter support. A selected signed projection x_C is one image of that
carrier; it does not replace its tensor content or joint dependence. Q is a declared
policy-normalized score, with denominator/domain and covariance assumptions.

Distinguish deterministic image/pushforward from probabilistic interpretation.
A deterministic tensor/scalar map can be computed and reported honestly without
null calibration; a probability, exceedance or calibrated-significance claim needs
the corresponding joint law, support and null ensemble. HTT owns model-dependent
posterior pushforwards; MIO may describe a family-independent diagnostic image.
Never manufacture a law, covariance or occupancy from a point estimate.

F is occupancy under an explicit measure/support and admissible sign-clean ceiling;
state the certified or conditional status and preserve denominators/filling fractions.
Pi is an exceedance under its declared law, not truth probability. G_F is the
declared depth-gap functional of supported F reports, retaining bin measures,
selection and cross-bin joint dependence. Null calibration limits claims of
significance/coverage; it does not prohibit computing an honest deterministic image.
Scalar x/Q/Pi/F/G or tensor morphology alone cannot identify a Bianchi family.
