## Mathematical result and boundary

The four-axis contract verifies only the registered conditional statements:

- a product-of-block-balls gauge is the maximum block norm/radius;
- the registered ellipsoid and symmetric H-polytope unit sublevels equal their
  declared bodies;
- `H_lin subset A` implies `rho_A <= 1`;
- invertible right scaling preserves response rank and the exact transformed
  response;
- the registered exact J1/J2 counterexamples have the declared values.

The parent-owned runner observed all four executables under contract SHA-256
`4f7092860fde327e0029154e3c4ca4c7e60f7095c4026c7fb00d1899ca8c3322`
and returned `CAS_4AXIS_PASS`. This satisfies the CAS component only.

The strong conjectures are not promoted:

- `J1-EXACT` is `REFUTED_OR_RESTRICTED` in its general outer-envelope form:
  the same physical set lies inside two unequal anchors with gauge values
  `1/2` and `1/4`.
- `J2-UNIFORM` is `REFUTED_OR_RESTRICTED` for joint-law-free or marginal-only
  calibration: equal numerator and anchor marginals give raw-ratio exceedance
  probabilities `0` and `1/2` under different couplings.

Neither counterexample rules out a narrower theorem with additional registered
physical assumptions or a valid test calibrated from a registered joint law.

## CAS remediation history

The first blind wave found that `POLYTOPE-FIXTURE` registered bounds but omitted
the ordered normals. SymPy failed on assumption alignment, and Lean supplied a
kernel-checked counterexample in which admissible normal sets with the same
bounds produce gauges `1/2` and `1`. All first-wave results were invalidated
after the contract was corrected.

The second wave was abandoned before acceptance when main-writer review found
additional runtime fail-closed and benchmark-calibration gaps. It is not
evidence.

assignment envelopes passed strict validation. The first parent execution then

preserved as
`CAS_ADJUDICATION_PR254_ANCHOR_GEOMETRY_BOOTSTRAP_FAILURE.json`.
The parent-owned clean-tree runner now prepares the pinned cache and invokes the
unchanged Lean proof source and compile-gated wrapper. A full four-axis rerun
then returned `CAS_4AXIS_PASS`.

Same-model-family program authorship is disclosed in the axis manifest. Four
engines are not four independent human derivations.

A later frozen review found three public-constructor/claim-scope defects. The
candidate was invalidated, all three defects were remediated, and contract
version 2 was rebound to the new source hash. Because the mathematical

SymPy, SageMath+Singular, and Lean were nevertheless all re-executed; the
