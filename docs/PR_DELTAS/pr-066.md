## Chosen plan

- Added `htt.departure.posterior_pushforward` at the live package root
  `htt/htt/htt/departure/posterior_pushforward.py`.
- Added:
  - `PosteriorPushforwardSample`;
  - `PosteriorPushforwardPrerequisites`;
  - `PosteriorPushforwardReport`;
  - `build_posterior_pushforward_report()`;
  - `reject_mio_pushforward_inputs()`.
- The builder accepts HTT posterior samples already mapped to diagnostic
  scalar functionals and records weighted summaries for `x_C`, `Q`, `F`, and
  `G_F`.
- The builder emits a `Pi` exceedance curve over a predeclared `Q`, `F`, or
  `G_F` source using the rule `sample_value > threshold`.
- The builder carries transfer source/spec/metadata and rejects external
  transfer metadata that claims native validation.
- The builder requires PR-063 local/global and PR-065 prior/PPC/LOOCV
  adequacy statuses by reference. Failed prerequisites downgrade claim tier to
  blocked and production status to blocked provenance mismatch.
- The pushforward rejects `MioCertificate`, MIO report-card shaped mappings,
  and `mio_*` evidence/posterior/score-style keys as inputs.
- Export and package-import probes now include
  `htt.departure.posterior_pushforward`.
- Backlog mirrors now point PR-066 at the live
  `htt/htt/htt/departure/posterior_pushforward.py` path.

Rejected alternatives: wrapping `mio.reports.departure_report` as an HTT
evidence input, reviving legacy `htt.core.departure_posteriors`, accepting raw
MIO certificate/report-card payloads, allowing native transfer relabeling, or
using scalar pushforward summaries as geometry/family claims.

## Claim-tier impact

PR-066 adds an HTT-owned posterior-pushforward artifact that maps HTT posterior
samples onto diagnostic scalar functionals with explicit transfer provenance.
It is transfer-conditional and pre-solver. Passing gates yield at most
conditional HTT diagnostic pushforward status with diagnostic-only production
status; failed prerequisites downgrade to blocked. PR-066 does not consume MIO
certificate/report-card outputs, validate transfer or native solver output,
establish morphology compatibility, or support geometry/family claims.

- owner: HTT
- implementation_scope: htt
- claim_tier: conditional when prerequisite statuses and transfer provenance
  pass; blocked otherwise
- production_status: diagnostic_only when gates pass; blocked otherwise
- transfer_source: `none`, `AniCLASS_external`, `external_transfer`, or
  `empirical_proxy`; native transfer sources rejected by PR-066
- sky_support_status: inherited from sample metadata; defaults to
  `not_directional`
- null_mock_status: inherited from sample metadata and linked to PR-065
  adequacy status
- mio_input_status: not_consumed
- scalar_functionals: `Q`, `F`, `Pi`, `G_F`
