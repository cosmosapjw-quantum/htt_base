## Outcome

PR-281 adds a dimension-aware, fail-closed orbit-acceptance value object and a
V2 `AnisotropyTypeReport` projection of replayed anchored-response null
directions. The result is a diagnostic-only C2 contract. It does not identify
a geometry or family, establish global orbit completeness, or turn numerical
response rank into data identifiability.

## Governing assumptions and conventions

- The group action is the registered `SO(3)` action on the declared state
  representation.
- The 12-, 15-, and 18-dimensional counts apply only on the principal stratum,
  where generic isotropy is finite and the group orbit is three-dimensional.
- The velocity-frame split counts `beta_RM` and `beta_MO`; the redundant
  `beta_RO` view is not counted again.
- The single-vector counterexample is a nonzero vector. Its stabilizer is
  `U(1)`, so its orbit is two-dimensional.
- `uncovered_directions` means the right-null basis of the replayed anchored
  response on its declared covariance support. It is distinct from
  `covariance_null_response`, which describes unsupported data-space channels.
- The null-basis tuples are exact within the registered same-environment replay.
  Cross-LAPACK comparisons should compare their spanned subspace/projector, not
  assume a globally canonical basis orientation.

## Dimension and limit audit

| Registered case | State dimension | Orbit dimension | Quotient rank | Boundary |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| principal base | 12 | 3 | 9 | finite generic isotropy only |
| principal plus acceleration | 15 | 3 | 12 | finite generic isotropy only |
| principal plus acceleration and velocity split | 18 | 3 | 15 | finite generic isotropy only |
| nonzero single vector | 3 | 2 | 1 | off-stratum `U(1)` stabilizer |

For each registered case, `quotient_rank = state_dimension - orbit_dimension`.
The formula is not exported as an unconditional rule: applying `3 - 3 = 0` to
the single-vector case is explicitly rejected. All quantities here are counts,
so there is no physical-unit conversion or sign convention to alter. The zero
vector limit has larger isotropy and is outside this registered acceptance
table.
