  - passing prior-sweep report;

  - passing LOOCV report;

- Missing or failed gates downgrade claim tier to blocked and production status
  to blocked provenance mismatch.

  production status.
- The new adequacy bundle rejects decisive-language requests when prior, PPC,
  LOOCV, or matched-null reports are missing.
- Exports and package-import tests now include `htt.infer.prior_sweep`,
  `htt.infer.posterior_predictive`, and `htt.infer.loocv`.
- Backlog mirrors now point PR-065 at the live `htt/htt/htt/infer/...` paths.

Rejected alternatives: modifying legacy `htt.core.advanced_diagnostics` into a
claim gate, treating old directional shell booleans as PR-065 adequacy, routing
PPC/LOOCV status through MIO or COMMON, merging MIO certificate status, or
allowing native/family/morphology claims from adequacy checks.

## Claim-tier impact

This PR adds HTT-owned inference-adequacy gate reports for prior sensitivity,
posterior-predictive checks, LOOCV, and the composite all-gates status. The
outputs are pre-solver, manifest-backed, and diagnostic-only in production
status. A passing adequacy report can support only conditional HTT adequacy
status; missing or failed gates are blocked. PR-065 does not create MIO
certificate content, validate transfer or native solver output, establish
morphology compatibility, or support geometry/family claims.

- owner: HTT
- implementation_scope: htt
- claim_tier: conditional when all adequacy gates pass; blocked otherwise
- production_status: diagnostic_only when all adequacy gates pass; blocked
  otherwise
- transfer_source: none
- sky_support_status: not_directional
- null_mock_status: prior/PPC/LOOCV adequacy status only
- matched_null_status: required from PR-064 for composite readiness
