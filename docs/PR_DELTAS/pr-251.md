# PR-251 — Equivariant orbit response and model discrepancy

Status: implementation candidate. This is an internal DAG card, not a GitHub
pull request.

## Implemented scope

- Added an exact Cartesian STF5 action for the signed departure state.  Shear
  transforms as a rank-two tensor, beta as a polar vector, omega as an axial
  vector, and the signed curvature coordinate as a scalar.
- Bound every state action to an explicit coordinate frame and active O(3)
  convention.  Proper rotations preserve the preregistered even and odd
  invariants; reflections preserve the five even invariants and reverse only
  `det[beta,sigma beta,sigma^2 beta]`.
- Added a fixed six-invariant catalogue whose report records the multiplicity
  method, alignment-null identity, preregistration, frame, congruence, window,
  scale, basis, units, parity and perturbative order.  States with a different
  unit or polar/axial contract are rejected before an invariant is emitted.
- Added a response-rank report computed only from an explicitly supplied
  post-transfer/post-mask response and covariance.  Missing transfer, mask or
  covariance identities return `MISSING_INPUT`; they never default to rank
  eight or full rank.
- Added supported-quotient whitening for singular PSD covariance.  Parameter
  null directions and covariance-null residuals remain visible.
- Fixed the historical wide-response ladder defect: when
  `rank < parameter_dimension`, `min_singular` is now exactly zero even if the
  economy SVD returns only non-zero observed singular values.
- Added tangent/perpendicular residual statistics and an optional explicit
  nonlinear-manifold distance.  `NONLINEAR_COMPATIBLE` can be constructed only
  when the same registered candidate beats linear, systematics,
  frame-mismatch and derivative-failure alternatives on both held-out and
  matched-injection scores by a positive declared margin.  The two data sources
  carry distinct SHA-256 content identities, and a rank-deficient response
  returns `NON_IDENTIFIED_RESPONSE` before attribution.
- Bound the OBSSTAT low-ell pole definition, ell set, numerical gap tolerance,
  coordinate frame and harmonic convention into a stable analysis identity.
  Every estimate now carries the typed analysis spec, constructor gaps are
  recomputed from the eigenvalues, and cross-frame/cross-convention alignment
  is rejected.  Added explicit scalar-alm inversion parity and O(3)
  tensor/antipodal-axis actions.

failed both independent reviews.  That verdict remains preserved in
`.agent-harness/runs/stat-foundations-pr251-review-20260727/`; it is not

- missing or rank-deficient response reports cannot be relabelled as positive
  nonlinear attribution;
- held-out and matched-injection evidence are separately identity-bound;
- tied low-ell eigenvalues cannot be made identified by a forged gap;
- analysis frame and harmonic convention reach every pole and alignment;

Any review receipt bound to the first candidate is invalid for the repaired
candidate.  A new seal and a new blind review round are required.

## Second sealed review and repair

The second frozen candidate
`270295867d766c54205a835d178f566cffb72b72` also failed both blind reviews.
Those verdicts and executable oracles remain preserved in
`.agent-harness/runs/stat-foundations-pr251-rereview-20260727/`.  They exposed
additional boundary failures that the first repair did not cover:

- rank support tolerance could also admit a materially negative covariance
  eigenvalue;
- public dataclass construction or `dataclasses.replace` could manufacture a
  full-rank or positive-attribution report without the measured arrays;
- response/mask/covariance labels were free text and did not bind the actual
  response and covariance bytes;
- object-dtype NumPy arrays could conceal booleans or complex values;
- a near-threshold forged pole gap could cross the abstention boundary;
- a pole axis was not bound to the power tensor from which it was claimed to
  arise, and the kernel accepted an unsupported harmonic-convention label.

The present repair separates a machine-scale PSD-validity tolerance from the
user-declared rank-support tolerance.  Rank and nonlinearity reports are now
factory-authorized frozen values; their semantic evidence labels must be
lowercase SHA-256 identities, and measured reports carry deterministic
float64-shape-and-byte identities for both response and covariance.  Rank,
singular spectrum, support dimension and nullspace are cross-validated.

Low-ell estimates now carry the complete symmetric trace-one power tensor,
validate its spectrum and selected eigendirection, and expose a deterministic
tensor content identity.  The selection gap is classified from the exact
derived value, object-array numeric smuggling is rejected recursively, and
the analysis spec accepts only the convention implemented by this kernel.
All review and integration evidence for the second candidate is invalid for
this repair; another seal and blind review are required.

## Third sealed review and repair

The third frozen candidate
`cb0d78b97cd86af74e5a1c2048ffda99d607863e` failed two fresh blind reviews.
The two first replacement assignments were aborted after accidental sibling
result exposure and remain administrative error receipts only.  The clean
replacement reviews and their executable counterexamples are preserved under
`.agent-harness/runs/stat-foundations-pr251-rereview3-20260727/`; they are not
acceptance evidence.

The clean reviews found that an invariant report could be rewritten through
`dataclasses.replace`, a low-ell report could be forged from a tied or
near-tied tensor and caller-supplied eigensystem fields, extreme but finite
common whitening scales could underflow a non-zero response to rank zero, and
public numeric paths still accepted booleans or numeric text.  They also
showed that data-receipt labels alone did not bind caller-supplied held-out
scores to candidate predictions or a scoring rule.

This repair makes invariant and low-ell reports factory-only.  Every low-ell
field is derived from one canonical power-tensor eigendecomposition, with
exact spectrum binding and a selected-axis tolerance strictly below the
accepted eigengap.  Response rank removes irrelevant common response and
covariance scales before its relative SVD, while residual statistics retain
their physical whitening.  Numeric arrays and scalar controls reject boolean
and text coercions.

Candidate evaluations are now factory-derived from exact held-out and
matched-injection predictions and targets under a registered scoring rule.
The model configuration, targets, predictions, scores and rule are bound into
a content-addressed evaluation identity; free-standing score rows and report
rewrites are rejected.  All third-candidate review evidence is invalid for
the repaired candidate, which must be resealed and independently reviewed.

## Claim boundary

All outputs are diagnostic-only pre-solver contracts.  `FOUND-EQUIV` remains
`ACTIVE_CONDITIONAL` pending `EGS3-B1`.  The invariant catalogue is not an
inverse morphology atlas; rank is not a family label; and an off-manifold
residual is unattributed unless the complete held-out competition succeeds.
No output identifies a Bianchi family, detects geometry, supplies an HTT
posterior/evidence term, or uses PR-151 partial acquisition.

Anchor stress and nonlinearity remain independent report types.  A state can be
within or above an anchor while having the same off-manifold classification;
neither report authorizes a conversion into the other.

## Kill conditions

The candidate fails if a hard-coded rank is emitted without the exact
response/covariance identities, a structural parameter null has positive
`min_singular`, a covariance-null residual receives zero cost, a reflection
does not flip exactly the parity-odd invariant, relative orientation collapses
to a norm, an incomplete or in-sample candidate competition produces
`NONLINEAR_COMPATIBLE`, or a diagnostic is described as family/native-solver
evidence.
