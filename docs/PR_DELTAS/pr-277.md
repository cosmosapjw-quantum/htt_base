## Reproduced defect

The former status projection accepted caller-authored artifact-gate fields for
claim tier, readiness, artifact mode, allowed use, production validation, and
manuscript use. A completed DAG row could therefore acquire science-facing
semantics without an evidence closure or adjudication receipt. Its fallback
also attached a blanket native-solver blocker to unrelated work.

The first registered blind pre-freeze review then reproduced two additional
authority defects in the initial mutable implementation:

1. a generic positive claim could be relabelled as another same-owner
   capability because the requested capability/action/outcome was not bound to
   the adjudicated graph; and
2. lifecycle invalidations affected issuance only when the caller repeated
   them in an optional blocker tuple.

Both findings remain preserved in
`.agent-harness/runs/pr277-prefreeze-review-20260803/`; they are not relabelled
as passes.

## Implemented contract

- Added the exact `ClaimCapability`, `CapabilityAction`, and
  `CapabilityOutcome` vocabularies and independent readiness, evidence-branch,
  scientific-semantics, identification, provenance, allowed-use, and ceiling
  axes.
- Made `VersionedClaimIdentity` root/successor-only across theorem, data, mask,
  covariance, transfer, and estimand identities. A changed identity creates a
  new content address and never reopens its predecessor.
- Made `ClaimCapabilityDecision` factory-only. `granted` is a read-only
  projection of action/outcome, not caller input.
- Removed the raw `_build_claim_capability_decision` bridge and its importable
  token after immutable review reproduced a direct-construction defect. A
  later exact review showed that `object.__new__` plus `object.__setattr__`
  could still fabricate a valid-looking record. The public constructor now
  refuses, the public issuer has no closure marker, and direct calls to the
  private registrar outside the exact validated issuer frame refuse. Identity
  registration is only an unissued-object misuse guard, not scientific
  authority. Every public decision use reissues from its exact graph,
  evidence receipt, adjudication receipt, versioned identity, owner, blocker,
  native-family, and ceiling context and compares every trust-bearing field.
  A relabelled record therefore fails even if the registration layer is
  deliberately perturbed; a bare object or serialized record grants nothing.
- Bound capability, action, outcome, and claim ceiling to an exact
  `claim_capability_binding_v1` payload in the content-addressed claim node.
  The evidence factory rejects any requested relabelling.
- Added capability-scoped supersession, theorem/data/mask/covariance/transfer/
  estimand invalidation, recalibration, and reexecution edges. Active
  invalidation/reexecution edges automatically become typed blockers; caller
  omission cannot hide them.
- Reused the existing `AuthorityRegistry`, `EvidenceReceipt`, and
  `AdjudicationReceipt`; no parallel principal or claim authority was added.
- Enforced owner firewalls, exact positive terminal adjudication for grants,
  affected-capability-only blocker propagation, and unconditional pre-native
  refusal of `FAMILY_IDENTIFICATION`.
- Replaced artifact-gate overrides with compatibility-only annotations.
  Without an independently revalidated capability decision, generated status
  rows remain internal-only, non-production, and blocked from science and
  publication use.
- Registered PR-277 as `in_progress` in the canonical status and synchronized
  compatibility mirrors. PR-278 and later nodes remain dependency-blocked.
- Regenerated the status snapshot, generated claim ledger, status matrix, and
  current handoff/decision/validation surfaces from the PR-277 authority; no
  manual count or scientific readiness override was introduced.
- Registered the capability decision and versioned-identity symbols plus
  `D-EVIDENCE-CONDITIONED-CAPABILITY` in shared context and rebuilt context
  version `e9b385d7...` within the configured injection budget.
