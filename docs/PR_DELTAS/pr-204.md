## Question tested

PR-204 tests whether the already-frozen PR-177 observable extractor has
adequate response and held-out coverage for paired quadrupolar
variance-modulation injections. The selected route uses the authenticated ACT
DR6 released baseline reconstructed-kappa alms on the strict support
`40 < L < 763`, integer multipoles 41 through 762 inclusive.

The injection begins **after** the ACT quadratic estimator:

`x_mod(n) = x(n) * sqrt(1 + a*T_j(n))`

Each modified map is transformed back to alms, hard-zeroed outside integer
41..762, synthesized again, and passed through the unchanged PR-177 raw and
mask-controlled feature extractor. This is not a pre-QE injection, raw-QE or
RDN0 reproduction, reconstruction-transfer validation, or a sky-anisotropy
result. PR-152 records that the exact raw-QE inputs are not present locally and
retains
`DEFER_RAW_QE_RDN0_PUBLIC_INPUTS_LARGE_RECONSTRUCTION`.

## Prospective split and gates

The assignment was frozen before the 400-unit result:

- five real-`Y_2` axes, 80 released simulations per axis;
- signed amplitude `+/-0.08`, balanced within every split and axis;
- 20 response-fit units per axis;
- 20 disjoint coverage-calibration units per axis;
- 40 disjoint evaluation units per axis.

The response fit uses paired injected-minus-baseline controlled features and no
intercept. Its registered gates require diagonal response in `[0.8, 1.2]`,
maximum absolute off-diagonal response at most `0.2`, and condition number at
most `2.0`. The per-axis 90% split threshold is the 19th ordered absolute
coverage-calibration residual out of 20,
`ceil((20+1)*0.9)`. Evaluation coverage must be at least `0.90` and absolute
evaluation bias at most `0.01` for every axis. The evaluation mask-sensitivity
ratio has a registered p95 ceiling of `0.5`.

No split, amplitude, axis, threshold, or gate was changed after reading the
result.
