## Reproduced capability gap

The PR-262 result evaluates one typed functional and carries an exact
PR-254 anchor stress, but it does not aggregate samples, preregister tensor
scalarization, expose a directional identified-set/anchor support profile, or
provide an explicit scalar-compatibility report. The historical scalar
`Q/F/Pi/G_F` modules are legacy-reproduction surfaces and cannot be reused as
active tensor semantics.

## Implemented change

1. Added `CertifiedFunctionalPushforward`. It retains raw tensor/vector values,
   requires one explicit scalarization policy per functional, preserves state,
   functional, anchor, and transfer identities, and derives summaries only
   after sample-wise evaluation.
   The builder now requires the exact `JointAnisotropyState` for every row and
   the exact PR-254 anchor registration for every functional. It replays every
   `TensorFunctionalResult` from those inputs before reading a value, stress,
   anchor identity, or transfer field. Exact transfer metadata receives its
   own retained provenance identity.
2. Kept `x` point values, inherited `Q` point values, and conditional `Q`
   bounds in different matrices. A conditional or unavailable anchor never
   produces one imputed point.
3. Added `SupportUtilizationProfile` over typed `AnchorVector` points and an
   exact PR-254 `AnchorBodySpec`. It reports signed and antipodal directional
   support ratios, a finite-catalogue maximum, the exact finite-hull/anchor
   gauge maximum, witness directions, and active anchor blocks/constraints.
4. A zero registered direction produces typed `DENOMINATOR_COLLAPSE`.
   A zero identified point produces valid zero utilization. A missing set,
   unavailable anchor, or channel mismatch produces no numeric utilization.
5. Added `OccupancyMeasure` only as
   `EMPIRICAL_DIAGNOSTIC_LEVEL_SET_MASS`. It requires a sign-definite,
   anchor-eligible functional, is monotone over registered thresholds, and
   refuses the complete measure when any cell is missing or conditional.
6. Added `LegacyXQPiFGView`, preserving the exact historical values and
   supplied tolerance. Available scalar `x/Q/F` successors are compared;
   `Pi` and `G_F` remain explicitly pending PR-265 and PR-266.
7. Added the MIO diagnostic bridge, governing specification, portable
   focused/adjacent/smoke runner, publication-review policy, and randomized
   support/gauge, scale, and O(3) regressions.

## Scientific and ownership boundary

- `Q` is the PR-254/PR-262 premise-anchor stress. It is not a distance,
  probability, posterior, or new denominator authority.
- Directional `F` is support utilization, not scalar cancellation, physical
  occupancy, or an anisotropic volume fraction.
- `OccupancyMeasure` is an empirical diagnostic sample mass, not spatial
  occupancy.
- MIO exposes no likelihood, posterior, Bayes factor, or evidence builder.
- All transfer provenance is replayed from the exact source states; result
  fields cannot self-attest it.
- No diagnostic identifies a source, geometry, or Bianchi family.

  certified pushforward.

- A diagnostic sample mass may not be described as physical occupancy.
- `Pi` and depth-path `G_F` remain pending their named successor PRs.

  permitted.
