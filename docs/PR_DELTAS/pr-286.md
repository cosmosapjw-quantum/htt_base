## Statistical and ownership boundaries

Every row now carries typed units/normalization/frame, sign orientation, prior
applicability, rank scope, and spectral/principal-angle status. The prior field
is explicitly not applicable to the 72 registered rows; the separate PR-272
SBC auxiliary cell remains the only prior-dependent calculation.

Expected failed controls are recorded as
`EXPECTED_NEGATIVE_CONTROL_KILLED`. A surviving negative control or a failed
registered positive cell would fail G8. In particular, covariance
misspecification, naive partial-identification coverage, unadjusted depth-path
multiplicity, weak response, rank loss, finite-library sensitivity, and the
proportional local/global design remain visible.

HTT retains model-dependent calibration and comparison ownership. OBSSTAT owns
observable estimands, nulls, and covariance inputs. MIO receives diagnostic
residual/coherence summaries only and produces no likelihood, posterior,
Bayes factor, or evidence.
