## Conjugate Gaussian group random-intercept toy

`htt/src/common/dependency_holdout.py`: `y_g = X_g beta + 1 b_g + eps_g`
with `b_g ~ N(0, tau_b2)`, `eps_g ~ N(0, sig2 I)`, prior
`beta ~ N(0, tau2 I)`. Marginalizing the group intercept gives the group
covariance `V_g = sig2 I + tau_b2 J`; the conjugate GLS posterior and the
group-joint predictive are closed form. The exchangeable unit is an
entire group — rows in a group share `b_g` and are NOT exchangeable at
the row level.

## Train-only refit, behaviorally verified

Every preprocessing step (standardization and feature/axis selection) is
re-run on the TRAINING partition of each fold only; the held-out group
never enters any transform, and the train-only transform is
content-addressed per fold. The exclusion is BEHAVIORALLY verified — the
transform raises if it is handed a held-out row — not merely self-labeled
`train_only` (the P1 the review lane raised). The held-out score is the
JOINT log predictive density of the held-out group's rows (a
multivariate density), summed over the 20 group folds — a dependency-bound
ELPD (Vehtari-Gelman-Gabry 2017, arXiv:1507.04544). Exact per-observation
ELPD -1.5909.

## PSIS verified against the exact refit

The PSIS approximation (Vehtari et al. 2015, arXiv:1507.02646; the
generalized-Pareto fit follows Zhang-Stephens 2009 as implemented in
arviz/loo) is computed from a single full-data fit and compared to the
exact per-fold refit: max Pareto k 0.4799 (< 0.7, reliable) and the two
ELPDs agree within tolerance. If any Pareto k exceeds 0.7, or the
approximate and exact ELPD disagree beyond tolerance, the PSIS estimate
is refused and the exact refit is required. A tail too small to fit fails
toward exact (never a silent "reliable" k = 0 — the P2 the review lane
raised).

## Demonstrated leakage and dependency optimism

Leaky full-data feature selection (a null-signal config with 30 noise
features) is DEMONSTRATED to inflate the held-out ELPD by 8.29 nats
(optimism from double-dipping); leaky standardization is honestly tiny
(0.0039) and labeled non-material. A full-data transform provenance is
refused. Row-level holdout that retains a held-out row's group-mates is
DEMONSTRATED optimistic (a higher per-observation lpd, gap 0.1799 at
tau_b2 = 2) because the retained mates leak the shared random intercept;
a fold that splits a dependency cluster is refused. A single
all-encompassing cluster yields `LOO_not_identified`.

## Adversarial review lane (pre-commit)

The review independently reproduced, from first principles and against the
real arviz reference, the closed-form GLS posterior (matched a brute-force
refit to 1e-16), the group-joint predictive (matched scipy MVN and the
independent marginal-conditional route to 1.5e-14), `_gpdfit`/`psis_smooth`
(matched arviz to ~1e-15, and confirmed the diagnostic flags k = 13.3 /
3.4 on genuinely heavy-tailed inputs), the selection-leakage gap (positive
across 5 seeds, +3.7 to +9.6), and the dependency optimism (monotone
dose-response in tau_b2 from ~0 to +0.30) — NO P0. It filed two P1 (guards
trusted the label instead of verifying behavior; a test named
`..._matches_conditional` never checked the conditional equivalence) and
one substantive P2 (the too-small-tail PSIS branch silently reported a
reliable k = 0). All fixed pre-commit: the train-only transforms now
behaviorally reject held-out rows, the equivalence is cross-checked in the
suite via `test_exact_elpd_matches_marginal_conditional` (the two
derivation routes agree to 1e-9 with standardization off), and the small-
tail branch raises toward exact. Two remaining P2 (caption lint evadable
by paraphrase; `refuse_as_loo` has no live caller in this PR) are
acknowledged: the lint is a backstop, and `refuse_as_loo` is a
preregistered forward-defense guard for the later data PRs.
