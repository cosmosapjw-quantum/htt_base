This unit implements a legal observer-space bridge from a genuinely
direction-indexed, dimensionless, `ell <= 2` scalar or pseudoscalar field to
typed dipole and STF2 moments. It does not manufacture direction from an MES
scalar, define physical shear or vorticity, identify local boost versus global
tilt, supply a native-solver result, or identify a Bianchi family.

## Implemented bridge

- The full-sky path implements the registered normalizations
  `V_a = 3/(4 pi) integral q(n) n_a dOmega` and
  `T_ab = 15/(8 pi) integral q(n) n_<a n_b> dOmega`. The supplied quadrature
  must reproduce sphere moments through degree four, and the typed field
  bandlimit is restricted to `ell <= 2`; higher-mode leakage returns
  `BLOCKED_DIRECTIONAL_LEAKAGE`.
- The masked/discrete path performs one weighted joint fit of the monopole,
  three dipole components, and five STF2 components. It refuses fewer than
  nine active samples or rank below nine. Exact selected directions, weights,
  field values, active mask, frame/convention, declared covariance and
  transfer identities, units, parity, and estimator scope enter the bound
  reproducibility identity.
- Ordinary scalar fields produce a polar vector and even STF2 representation;
  pseudoscalar fields produce an axial vector and odd STF2 representation
  under the registered active right-handed O(3) convention. Rotation and
  reflection tests cover both vector and tensor laws.
- The observer-space MES state divides genuine moments by one positive,
  verified, channel-typed active MES anchor. The anchor changes amplitudes but
  not normalized vector/tensor shapes. Zero vector or tensor amplitude yields
  a typed absent shape rather than an arbitrary axis or eigenspace.
- Public frozen types revalidate construction and `dataclasses.replace`.
  Callers cannot forge parity, physical-response status, independent
  information, claim tier, family identification, anchor conditioning,
  amplitude/shape consistency, state identity, units, bandlimit, or a
  probability-measure certificate.
- Signed directional fields remain explicitly
  `SIGNED_FIELD_NOT_A_PROBABILITY_MEASURE`. A separate constructive antipodal
  certificate accepts only zero-mean PSD trace-one spherical second moments
  and explicitly denies higher-moment and dynamical-attainability claims.
- Physical stress remains unavailable. A missing response returns
  `BLOCKED_PHYSICAL_RESPONSE_REQUIRED`, a cross-channel response returns
  `BLOCKED_CHANNEL_MATCH`, and even a matching response remains
  `RESPONSE_BOUND_NUMERATOR_REQUIRED`.

Content hashes in these types are reproducibility identities, not scientific
authority. The current weighted joint fit binds non-negative diagonal weights
and a declared covariance identity; it does not claim a full-covariance
likelihood or publication-ready masked-sky inference.
