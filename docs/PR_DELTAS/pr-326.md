This unit implements a legal observer-space bridge from a genuinely
direction-indexed, dimensionless field with a declared `ell <= 2` and parity
contract to typed dipole and STF2 moments. Raw samples cannot verify those
global semantic declarations: their status remains
`DECLARED_UNVERIFIED_BANDLIMIT_AND_PARITY`. It does not manufacture direction
from an MES scalar, define physical shear or vorticity, identify local boost
versus global tilt, supply a native-solver result, or identify a Bianchi
family.

## Implemented bridge

- The full-sky path implements the registered normalizations
  `V_a = 3/(4 pi) integral q(n) n_a dOmega` and
  `T_ab = 15/(8 pi) integral q(n) n_<a n_b> dOmega`. The supplied quadrature
  must reproduce sphere moments through degree four. The estimator supports
  only a declared bandlimit through `ell=2`; declarations above that boundary
  and detectable `ell=0/1` component contradictions return
  `BLOCKED_DIRECTIONAL_LEAKAGE`. Undetectable sample aliasing remains explicitly
  unverified rather than being represented as a leakage certificate.
- The masked/discrete path performs one weighted joint fit of the monopole,
  three dipole components, and five STF2 components. It refuses fewer than
  nine active samples, rank below nine, or a column-normalized design condition
  number above `1e8`. Exact selected directions, weights, field values, active
  mask, frame/convention, declared covariance and transfer identities, units,
  parity, condition diagnostic/threshold, and estimator scope enter the bound
  reproducibility identity and observer-state payload.
- Declared ordinary scalar fields use a polar-vector/even-STF2 transformation
  law; declared pseudoscalar fields use an axial-vector/odd-STF2 transformation
  law. Only the typed `RIGHT_HANDED_ACTIVE_O3` convention is admitted. The
  frame and convention are preserved in the state and identity; rotation and
  reflection tests cover both vector and tensor laws without promoting the raw
  declaration to verified intrinsic parity.
- The observer-space MES state divides genuine moments by one positive,
  verified, channel-typed active MES anchor. The anchor changes amplitudes but
  not normalized vector/tensor shapes. Zero vector or tensor amplitude yields
  a typed absent shape rather than an arbitrary axis or eigenspace.
- Public frozen types revalidate construction and `dataclasses.replace`.
  Callers cannot forge parity, physical-response status, independent
  information, claim tier, family identification, anchor conditioning,
  amplitude/shape consistency, estimator/state identity, frame/convention,
  units, condition diagnostics, declared semantic status, bandlimit, or a
  probability-measure certificate.
- Signed directional fields remain explicitly
  `SIGNED_FIELD_NOT_A_PROBABILITY_MEASURE`. A separate constructive antipodal
  certificate accepts only zero-mean PSD trace-one spherical second moments
  and explicitly denies higher-moment and dynamical-attainability claims.
- Physical stress remains unavailable. A missing response returns
  `BLOCKED_PHYSICAL_RESPONSE_REQUIRED`, a cross-channel response returns
  `BLOCKED_CHANNEL_MATCH`, and even a matching response remains
  `RESPONSE_BOUND_NUMERATOR_REQUIRED`.

Content hashes in these types are reproducibility identities, not scientific
authority. The current weighted joint fit binds non-negative diagonal weights
and a declared covariance identity; it does not claim a full-covariance
likelihood or publication-ready masked-sky inference.
