## What it does

`htt/obsstat/k1_e2e_calibration.py` binds the exchangeable observation-inclusive
pooled-rank global-p estimator (PR-135 `pooled_rank_p = (1+b)/(N+1)`) to the real
Planck PR3 FFP10 end-to-end SMICA null.

1. **Conservativeness self-consistency check (not a falsifier).** On a large
   correlated-GRF ensemble the shipped `(1+b)/(N+1)` pooled rank is verified
   super-uniform. Because it is a leave-one-out RANK statistic its marginal is
   super-uniform for ANY continuous exchangeable ensemble — the correlation
   `rho` is inert (removed by the max over statistics) — so this is a method
   self-consistency check, NOT a Planck falsifier. A **live anti-conservative
   `b/(N-1)` negative control** (which admits `p == 0`) is shown to over-reject
   at a sub-resolution significance level where the shipped form structurally
   cannot (its minimum p is `1/N`); this gives the check genuine, noise-free
   discriminating power rather than a rubber stamp.

2. **Real FFP10 E2E null.** The heavy end-to-end max-scan card
   (`docs/generated/k1_global_maxscan_e2e_full.json`) is produced ONCE by
   `scripts/k1_global_maxscan.py --precision --proc-nside 64` (masked, `lmax=30`)
   over **300 CMB Monte-Carlo maps drawn from the 999 available, each paired with
   a real noise Monte-Carlo map**, through the same frozen
   mask/proc-nside/statistic pipeline as the observed SMICA map (PR-149
   convention, byte-equivalent path). The module reads the card (the ACT
   pattern) — it never re-runs the 600 GB read. The look-elsewhere global p over
   the six registered statistics is **`11/301 ≈ 0.0365`**, on the exchangeable
   support grid, above the `1/301` resolution floor, HARD-validated on the actual
   reported value (not a grid-snapped surrogate). The per-statistic local
   p-values and the look-elsewhere global p (max-scan over the six statistics —
   no sky axis grid) are reported separately.

3. **PR4/NPIPE non-numeric skip.** The only PR4 output is a non-numeric skip
   receipt; no download, size probe, reduction, p-value, or PR3-and-PR4 combined
   result is produced. The PR4 systematics-sensitivity question stays BLOCKED.
