## Conjugate Gaussian evidence toy + exact reference

`htt/src/common/coherent_evidence.py`: `y_i ~ N(mu, sig2)` with `sig2`
known, normalized prior `mu ~ N(0, tau2)`. The evidence
`Z1 = integral p(y|mu) p(mu) dmu = N(y; 0, sig2 I + tau2 J)` is exact
(J = ones outer product), the point null `Z0 = p(y|mu=0)`, and
`log BF10 = log Z1 - log Z0 = 1.6125` is exact. The prior is a NORMALIZED
density (its integral is checked to be one); an unnormalized prior is
refused because it corrupts the evidence integral.

## Two independent engines cross-checked against the exact evidence

Thermodynamic integration (path sampling over a power-law `u^4`
power-posterior beta-ladder; the power posterior tempers only the
likelihood and keeps the full normalized prior) and bridge sampling
(Meng-Wong optimal iteration in log space, posterior + wider-Gaussian
proposal) each draw their OWN samples (distinct seeds and method) and are
cross-checked against the exact evidence. Both match within tolerance
(engine gap 0.0048, deviation from analytic < 0.001) and the verdict is
`coherent`. Independence is a DATA check, not a label check: each engine
records a `sample_digest` over its ACTUAL draws, and two engines with a
matching digest are refused even under different method labels. An
under-resolved thermodynamic ladder (9 linear beta points, 400 samples)
is DEMONSTRATED to disagree (gap 0.50) and yield the `indeterminate`
verdict.

## Prior-sensitivity gate (live) + fail-closed indeterminate

The log Bayes factor is evaluated over the preregistered prior-scale /
covariance grid; the swing is 1.814, within the ceiling of 4, so the
production verdict is coherent. The gate `require_within_ceiling` is
asserted on the production verdict (not wrapped in a tautological
`if not prior_sensitive`), and a deliberately wide grid (swing 4.43 > 4)
is DEMONSTRATED to be refused, downgrading the decisive claim to
`indeterminate`. The content-addressed receipt binds the normalized
prior, likelihood hash, data hash, and both engine configs; a caller
scalar is not a receipt, and a fitted score / max-likelihood ratio is
never accepted as a Bayes factor.

## Adversarial review lane (multi-lane, pre-commit)

Three refute-prompted lenses (estimator correctness; independence and
determinism; guard liveness and overclaim), each verifying against scipy
and brute-force recomputation, plus an adversarial verification pass over
every finding. The estimator lens found NO defect — the exact evidence
matches brute-force 1-D quadrature to machine precision, the
thermodynamic path identity and trapezoid weights are correct (bias
shrinks monotonically with the ladder), and the Meng-Wong bridge iteration
is unbiased and converges to the exact evidence on asymmetric data. One
CONFIRMED P1 was fixed: the prior-sensitivity gate was logically inverted
(`require_within_ceiling` was called only inside `if not prior_sensitive`,
the exact condition under which it cannot raise) — it is now asserted on
the production verdict and demonstrated on a prior-sensitive grid. Three
P2 were fixed or documented: the independence guard was label-based (now a
`sample_digest` data-independence check); two forward-defense guards
(`require_evidence_kind`, `caller_scalar_is_not_a_receipt`) were not
invoked on the production path (now wired into `build_evidence` and the
receipt build); and the thermodynamic MC standard error omits the
deterministic quadrature bias (~1e-3, harmless — the coherent verdict is
governed by the bootstrap error and the analytic tolerance; now disclosed
in an `mc_standard_error_note` field).
