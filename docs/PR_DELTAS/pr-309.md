# PR-309 — JWST-SN row/covariance closure

## Frozen contract

Capability: `JWST_SN_ROW_PROVENANCE_AND_SHARED_COVARIANCE_CLOSURE`.
The operator consumes only an exact PR-289 `JWST_SN` admission, ordered as
`source_rows`, `host_rows`, `individual_errors`, `covariance`, and
`competitor_model`. Historical PR-153/154 numbers and positional-only
`docs/generated/jwst_cf4_anchors.json` are neither inputs nor oracles.

Each row binds source/release/locator, non-positional host linkage and its
uncertainty, individual magnitude error, calibration group, CMB-frame redshift,
source-reported depth in Mpc, and IAU-1958 Galactic direction in degrees. The
observable sign is exactly `METHOD_A_MINUS_METHOD_B_MAG`. Full covariance is
`diag(individual + linkage variance) + Z C_zp Z^T + C_pv`; both shared terms
must have off-diagonal support and the result must be positive definite with
condition number at most `1e10`.

The whitened response order is monopole, Galactic xyz, centered redshift, and
centered depth. CF4 and 2MRS remain separate competitors. CF4 binds a native
CMB-frame forward model; 2MRS binds a solar-system-barycenter to CMB transform,
its named model and source release, magnitude unit, transformation identity,
and the same observable sign. Under the private, attended, single-operator
research threat model, the exact PR-289 admission identifies the selected
component bytes; this science boundary checks their typed meaning and does not
add a second cryptographic provider-attestation system. Missing admission,
wrong semantics, or an empty transformation identity fails closed. The
stability floor is a `1e-6` singular-value ratio after Cholesky-left whitening
and covariance-whitened L2 column scaling, so nonzero competitor normalization
cannot change rank; stability loss yields an explicit abstention with no point
estimate, p-value, forced source label, or family claim.
Even when a competitor response is numerically full rank, PR-309 reports only
that rank diagnostic. It does not fit a competitor amplitude, standard error,
or residual statistic. Any such model-conditioned inference requires a
separately registered HTT estimand and generative-branch composition.
Rows with an ambiguous host basis or linkage probability below one abstain
before response rank: scalar linkage variance does not marginalize host-derived
redshift, depth, direction, or competitor covariates.
