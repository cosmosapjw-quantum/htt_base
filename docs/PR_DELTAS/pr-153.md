## What it does

`htt/obsstat/jwst_anchor_manifest.py` + runner
`scripts/codex_harness/run_pr153_jwst_anchor.py`.

1. **Row-complete cited-seed manifest** of the 17 cited JWST distance anchors
   (Freedman CCHP TRGB/JAGB + Riess SH0ES Cepheids), each carrying the
   **cited-source citation** (the authoritative per-anchor DOI marked
   **unverified** — the authoritative table is not reproduced), the seed
   retrieval hash, the J2000 coordinate evidence, the published distance-modulus
   precision (the original uncertainty), the method/calibration group and the
   deterministic transform (identity, precision only).

2. **Probabilistic identity match** (Budavári-Szalay): a 2D-Gaussian signal
   likelihood vs a **local no-match background density**, so a distant CF4 group
   centre gets a low posterior — NOT a coordinate radius alone, NOT a bare
   argmin. Result: **10/17 positionally credible** (posterior > 0.5), 2 marginal
   (near-threshold, fragile to the background estimate), 2 ambiguous. **Positional
   credibility is a positional coincidence with a CF4 group centre, NEVER a
   confirmed physical identity** — the seed carries no redshift, so
   `identity_confirmed = False` on every match.

3. **Authoritative-reproduction decision (KILL)**: the CCHP MRT is a 404, and a
   fetched product is credited only when the manifest **certifies it parses as a
   data table** (a content gate, not a label match — a 200-OK paywall page would
   otherwise falsely certify). So the anchors are cited-seed; the JWST lane stays
   a cited-seed catalogue-linkage scenario; no CF4-conditioned forecast is
   authorized while `N-DATA-CF4-DOWNSTREAM` is OPEN.

4. **Exercised** by an independent row replay (identical), a **credible-count
   tolerance sensitivity** (6/17 flip credibility across the σ grid — genuinely
   tolerance-dependent), a leave-one-group report (per-anchor best match stable;
   the interacting duplicate grouping tracked), and a **derangement
   label-substitution negative test** (all 10 credible identities broken).

Guards (live + killed as mutations): refuse identity from a coordinate radius
alone; refuse renaming the synthetic fixture observed; refuse authoritative
reproduction without the table; refuse a CF4-conditioned forecast; refuse a JWST
measurement/detection; refuse a Bianchi-family claim. 6/6 mutations killed.
