## Concrete result

Four author-submitted arXiv source members are verified by full archive/member
SHA-256, exact numerical cell markers, committed CSV hashes, and per-dataset
exact-cell receipts before numerical emission.

- CCHP: 7 unique paired hosts, JAGB minus TRGB mean
  `-0.0081428571 mag`, conditional between-host SE `0.0181690849 mag`,
  two-sided Student-t `p=0.669759...`.
- SH0ES: 13 unique paired hosts, JWST minus HST mean
  `-0.0338461538 mag`, conditional between-host SE `0.0265189449 mag`,
  two-sided Student-t `p=0.225998...`.
- Registered displacement `5*log10(73/67.5)=0.170095... mag`: the SH0ES
  host result is far below that positive shift (one-sided Student-t
  `p≈3e-6`). This is a table-conditional comparison, not an H0 fit.
- Expanded source-reported replays: Li et al. 2024 gives
  `0.007 ± 0.037 mag` for 8 paired hosts in a 10-SN parent sample; Li et al.
  2025 gives `-0.003 ± 0.021 mag` for 20 paired objects in the complete 35-SN
  compilation. Their overlapping samples are not pooled. The finite registered-
  shift tail probability is retained as `8.422603573854381e-17`, not rounded
  to zero.

The host-level Student-t intervals condition on independent host deltas and do
not include unavailable shared-anchor/common-systematic covariance. They are not
total uncertainties. The inverse-variance calculation remains sensitivity-only.
Failure to reject zero is a consistency measurement, not proof of equality.
