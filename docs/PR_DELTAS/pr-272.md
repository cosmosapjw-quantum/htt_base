## Capability

PR-272 adds a preregistered synthetic validation layer for `VT-S3`, `VT-S5`,
`VT-S6`, and `VT-S9` through `VT-S14`. It keeps calibration and evaluation
splits separate, reports conservative coverage intervals, preserves
misspecified and unadjusted failure cells, abstains under weak
identification, and exposes model-dependent depth comparison only through the
HTT surface. The MIO surface contains residual/coherence diagnostics only.

This PR does not promote a source theorem status, use observed or PR-151
partial data, validate a native solver or morphology atlas, or support
geometry or Bianchi-family identification.

## Pre-execution chronology

The initial design was committed before any stochastic result was evaluated.
Two analytic design checks then completed the frozen design, still before
result inspection:

1. the matched-counterpair DGP was made explicit as a mean over sixteen
   frozen independent synthetic modes, so its per-mode noise and summary
   variance could not be conflated;
2. the local/global mean-separation noncentrality was calculated from the
   declared covariance, exposing that unit amplitude was too weak for the
   already declared selection lower bound. The final amplitude was frozen at
   four before any Monte Carlo draw was generated.

No seed, DGP, grid, threshold, tolerance, or amplitude changed after the first
generated validation artifact was inspected.
