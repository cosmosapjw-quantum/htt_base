## Physics/math audit

- Metric signature remains `(-,+,+,+)`.
- `CongruenceKinematics` is explicitly kinematics of one congruence.
  `VelocityFrameBundle` is explicitly a relation between three distinct
  physical frames; it is not fused into the congruence-dynamical layer.
- Shear uses STF5; vorticity is axial; acceleration and all beta components
  are polar. Magnetic Weyl is axial under improper O(3) transformations.
- Available acceleration is typed as `A/(c Theta)` in the explicit-c branch
  or `A/Theta` in the declared `c=1` branch. Conversion requires an explicit
  positive numerical `c`; the branches cannot mix silently.
- `delta_omega_k` remains a signed scalar curvature-budget coordinate. It is
  not reinterpreted as anisotropic spatial curvature.
- No acceleration ceiling is introduced. A non-geodesic acceleration value
  is congruence-typed state input, not a revived non-geodesic MES anchor.
- FLRW-zero, no-tilt, local-only, global-only, missing-channel,
  repeated-eigenvalue, and zero-shear fixtures remain diagnostic state
  fixtures and produce no converse, geometry, source, or family verdict.

## Reproduced and prevented failure classes

1. Legacy `DepartureState.beta_a` has no declared PR-256 semantic role.
   Calling the adapter without a binding returns typed `ABSTAIN`.
2. Acceleration previously had no state-level explicit-c versus `c=1`
   normalization. Wrong or missing normalization now fails construction.
3. A partial geometry payload could otherwise make absent Weyl or
   anisotropic-curvature information look like zero. Each absent channel now
   carries `MISSING` or `NEEDS_NATIVE`.
4. A payload could otherwise combine a kinematic frame/congruence with a
   different geometry or velocity support. Exact metadata mismatch now fails.
5. PR-256 payload claim-boundary drift could otherwise be hidden behind a
   valid closure value. The adapter now binds and revalidates its allowed and
   forbidden uses.
6. Improper transformations could otherwise treat axial and polar channels
   identically. Reflection/inversion regressions verify vorticity and magnetic
   Weyl parity separately.
