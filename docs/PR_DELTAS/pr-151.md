## Frozen measurement

The final card measures the weighted DESI DR1 `BGS_BRIGHT-21.5` number-count
dipole over `0.1 <= z <= 0.4` at NSIDE=64. The observed catalogue and every
mock use the same redshift, weight, pixelization, per-cap alpha-refit, and
nuisance-refit contract.

- Primary null: an observation-inclusive finite rank against all 1000 EZmock
  FFA amplitudes, including both one-sided tails and the central two-sided
  probability.
- Validation: the 25 AbacusSummit amplitudes and their finite rank are reported
  separately; they are never pooled into the EZmock support. A two-sample
  transport check is secondary.
- Claim: a concrete DESI-survey-and-official-mock-conditioned consistency or
  tension measurement. It is not causal attribution, a dipole detection,
  anisotropy evidence, geometry, or Bianchi-family identification.

## Why every mock needs a random, but not all 18

The primary estimator needs one authenticated NGC+SGC random window for **each
mock**, because that window represents the realization-specific survey
selection entering its refit. Accordingly random index 0 is consumed for all
1000+25 mocks.

Downloading every one of the 18 random indices for every mock is not required
by the frozen low-ell estimand. Those files are Monte-Carlo integration
replications of the same selection, not 18 independent cosmological mocks.
Random index 1 is therefore preregistered on 10 EZmocks plus 5 Abacus mocks to
measure the sensitivity of amplitude and direction to the one-random policy.
This is a statistical design choice, not an assertion that the full set is
impossible.

The estimated transfer is about 1.21 TB for the selected policy, with about
129 GB retained compactly; all 18 random indices would be about 19 TB. The
1.8 TB NVMe can support the streamed policy but not a retained all-18 copy.

## Safe acquisition and deletion contract

`dl_pipeline/scripts/download_desi_dr1_mocks.py` uses resumable `.part` files,
`aria2c`, full-file SHA256 receipts, and atomic per-realization records. Each raw
random pair is reduced to a weighted NSIDE=64 window, re-opened and hash-checked,
and only then may those two transient random FITS files be removed. Two deletion
receipts are required for each removed pair. Observed and mock clustering data
are retained. A partial or corrupt compact window cannot authorize raw-random
deletion, and a partial acquisition cannot produce the official card.
Completion also requires a final rehash of every retained clustering file.
The scientific producer rechecks each recorded size and SHA-256 before reading
it, and the terminal script reruns both producer and result-pack check modes.
The tmux wrapper automatically restarts a failed acquisition attempt after a
bounded wait while preserving `.part` files; it does not duplicate a live job.
