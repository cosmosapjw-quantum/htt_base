## Reproduced capability gap and self-review findings

The frozen v2 surface accepts only one scalar-era beta vector and deliberately
leaves generic orbit separation unproved. It cannot represent the joint
`omega`, acceleration, `beta_RM`, and `beta_MO` generator family, stratified
stabilizers, or a finite set of conditioned cyclic charts.

During implementation self-review, four concrete semantic failures in the
first draft were reproduced and repaired:

1. one stabilizer vocabulary was used for both O(3) and SO(3), although zero,
   repeated-eigenvalue, and simple-spectrum stabilizers differ by group;
2. cyclic reports contained determinant/condition diagnostics but not the
   declared 14 canonical coordinates;
3. `J_sigma` was labelled with polynomial degree three even though it is a
   degree-zero normalized rational shape coordinate; and
4. a singular chart used condition number zero, which could be mistaken for
   ideal conditioning.

The final implementation separates the group vocabularies, binds each chart
to 14 typed coordinates, records typed partiality, labels `J_sigma` exactly,
and uses an undefined condition number with zero reciprocal condition for
rank-deficient charts.

## Physics and mathematics audit

- `sigma` is an STF rank-2 tensor; `omega` is axial; acceleration and the two
  independent beta channels are polar.
- A mixed axial/polar Gram contraction is a pseudoscalar. A polar Krylov
  determinant is a pseudoscalar, while the axial Krylov determinant is an O(3)
  scalar. Triple-determinant parity follows `(# axial vectors + epsilon)`
  modulo two.
- Twelve deterministic random proper/improper actions verify every available
  signature entry and every chart determinant against its registered parity
  law. Singular spectra are invariant within `1e-10`.
- The resolved SO(3) shear stabilizers are SO(3), embedded O(2), and the
  Klein four-group. The corresponding O(3) stabilizers are O(3),
  `O(2) x Z2`, and the eight diagonal sign transformations.
- The local chart is the registered 14-coordinate construction:
  `I2`, `I3`, and the twelve reference-to-labelled-vector Gram-Krylov
  contractions. Numerical cyclicity is not a proof of generic separation.
- `VT-T5` through `VT-T9` are not promoted here. Their proof and four-axis CAS
  obligations remain assigned to PR-269/270.
