## Frozen row operator

The preregistered ten-coordinate row is, in order:

1. the positive rowwise `sigma` MES anchor;
2. the positive rowwise `omega` MES anchor;
3. parity even/odd ratio over `ell=2..5`;
4. the `ell=2` power-tensor gap;
5. the `ell=3` power-tensor gap;
6. the `ell=2` multipole-vector absolute dot product;
7. three `ell=3` multipole-vector absolute dot products; and
8. maximum `ell=2/3` multipole-plane alignment.

All ten coordinates use a two-sided tail. Raw `C_ell` channels are excluded:
`C_2` and `C_3` are replaced by the two active anchors, while `C_4` and `C_5`
are not reintroduced as duplicate amplitude coordinates. The exact same
operator, tail policy, leave-one-out row maximum, and observation-inclusive
finite-rank rule act on all 301 rows. The operator identity is
`sha256:1d0f8ac8d5ed149b1e994dd18c3bdb09fa9b14e4498e40362f2ba8f25998955d`.

The anchors are realization-conditional functions of the same source row.
They are therefore recorded as shared-data self-anchors and contribute no
independent information. The pooled 301-row, `ddof=1` covariance is exported
for diagnostic rank/conditioning inspection only; it is not used by the
finite-rank scan and is not a likelihood covariance.
