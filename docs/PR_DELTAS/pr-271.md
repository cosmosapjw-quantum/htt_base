## Capability

PR-271 adds the exact/core statistical-method successor for the registered
vector/tensor proof program. It types the estimand, sampling law, covariance
premises, and finite/asymptotic status for `VT-S1`, `VT-S2`, `VT-S4`,
`VT-S7`, `VT-S8`, `TF-09`, and the finite registered-path form of `TF-11`.
It inventories all 34 legacy Pillar-S rows while refusing premise invention
for the 29 title-only signatures.

This PR does not run observed data, move model-dependent inference outside HTT,
accept an oracle self-report, validate a native solver or morphology atlas, or
make a geometry/family-identification claim.

## Preimplementation evidence and repaired defects

The registered read-only map
`pr271-evidence-map-20260730-r1` found three blocking specification defects:

1. global sign reversal alone did not license pooled exact binomial
   calibration for TF-09;
2. nested support sets alone did not define the common-target filtration
   needed by TF-11;
3. VT-S8 named the cross-covariance omission term without fixing its signed
   direction.

The implementation spec now requires a uniform conditional sign-vector law
for pooled TF-09 tests, one common finite target and explicit positive-mass
partition refinements for TF-11, and both signed VT-S8 identities:
`C_omitted-C_actual=C_ab+C_ba` and its negative reverse.

The mapper initially used a no-op validator invocation and emitted a malformed
assignment identity. The invalid envelope was not accepted. The unique result
was corrected against the registered assignment, validated with
`validate_harness.py`, merged, summarized, and frozen as historical evidence.

## Implemented change

1. Added `vector_tensor_statistical_foundations.py` with explicit evidence
   grades and proof verdicts. Simulation-only evidence cannot instantiate an
   exact verdict.
2. Added a fail-closed PR-271 proof-author registry containing exactly:
   - 34 legacy Pillar-S rows;
   - five selected VT-S rows;
   - two selected TF rows.
3. Preserved `SIG-P18`, `SIG-T1p`, `SIG-DL1`, `SIG-L-T2-EXIST`, and `SIG-T2G`
   as source-typed references with scalar-reduction checks. The other 29
   legacy rows remain `INCONCLUSIVE_MISSING_SIGNATURE`.
4. Implemented finite deterministic scalarization checks for total variation
   and KL data processing, including zero-mass/infinite-KL branches and
   decision-specific sufficiency.
5. Unified coordinate thresholds, weighted max statistics, covariance
   ellipsoids, and the one-dimensional legacy interval through a typed
   acceptance gauge. Nonsymmetric or rank-deficient covariance refuses; no
   pseudoinverse fallback exists.
6. Revalidates the PR-265 law-typed conditional survival profile without
   promoting empirical-measure exactness to population coverage.
7. Revalidates the PR-264 sample-by-functional pushforward and exposes the
   noninterchangeability of mean sample-wise ratios and ratio of means.
8. Added the exact paired covariance identity with ordered cross blocks and
   explicit signed omission error. Marginal-only inputs cannot be relabelled
   as paired.
9. Added exact conditional sign-test arithmetic with retained ties and a hard
   uniform-sign-vector premise.
10. Added exact rational finite-partition tower verification for one common
    target. Support nesting, zero-mass cells, different targets, convergence,
    optional stopping, and the known unstable fast-oracle branch remain
    outside the verdict.
11. Added a human-readable derivation, deterministic generator, portable test
    runner, hostile mutation tests, and a non-self-rendering raw-count guard.
