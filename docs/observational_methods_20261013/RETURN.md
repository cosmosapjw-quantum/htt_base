## What was measured and what was assumed

OBS-02 reused the package's table4-only geometry pilot: full grouped sample X12/X13 rank 12/13 with normalized condition numbers 11.199/14.477; narrow 49–51% distance band 304.766/396.963. That pilot uses uniform weights, published dL point summaries and a Frobenius-orthonormal STF basis. It is neither a physical rank-13 certificate nor noisy-dA GLS or a cosmological detection. The implementation explicitly transforms Cartesian STF coefficients and covariance to that basis before comparison. Real CF4 table3/table4/ReadMe SHA-256 identities are `1c02e2b3829b0b323524a5f3671a5f530cbdccae3c10b432db0c2e2fe09672fe`, `be91d4fae6fa01552ab3bc85db695411fca3249eeae08b566a712e6ea790bd99`, and `2cfed1418147d5a626dee1fa37c47252124477c9828490508d9bbe511d34edb4`. Method leave-one-out deletes groups with that method footprint; it cannot recompute the published combined DMzp.

OBS-03's likelihood jointly integrates latent dA and ztrue using dL=(1+ztrue)²dA, one shared calibration parameter and one independent anchor, and a parameter-dependent selection denominator. Its H, distance shell law, source motion, errors, thresholds, calibration offsets and identity observer transform are *manufactured test choices*. The reported coverage is approximate marginal Wald coverage for one truth under that chosen generator, not actual CF4 coverage. The real radial/method plot is descriptive. No observed CF4 flow parameter was fitted. Vcmb/CMB-frame-corrected velocities were not counted as independent CMB dipole evidence; a real Vh observer transform remains to be bound.

OBS-04 uses stored reduced maps, not a freshly verified full-resolution matched beam/pixel/high-l/noise response. Its eQ grid is an assumed sensitivity scale, not a measured confidence interval. OBS-05 reads a matched local DESI DR1 BGS v1.2 product with window and covariance; remote release authentication was not independently observed. Its Gaussian template/polynomial design is controller-selected before this run, not ZIP-preregistered or a calibrated physical low-mode response. Embedded theory zeros are placeholders. OBS-07 varies assumed bias radii; it does not obtain CAS07 physical K, M2, L, ray/source normalization or a certified remainder bound. The new coverage wrapper has no Lean bridge verification. No unnecessary Wolfram/xAct, Sage/Singular or Lean rerun was performed for these data/operator checks.
