Equation (2): dot(sigma_ab) = -theta sigma_ab - three_R_<ab>.
The reduced scalar system assumes three_R_ab sigma^ab = 0; flat or
isotropic three-curvature is a sufficient condition in the source.
Equation (9), typed here as Q_D^BT to prevent symbol conflation:
  Q_D^BT := (2/3) Var_D(theta) - 2 Var_D(sigma_magnitude).
Equation (16) separates the mean-shear density from this residual Q.

With sigma_sq=sigma_magnitude^2,
  Q_D^BT = Q_D^B + 2 <sigma_magnitude>_D^2.
Thus Q_D^BT and Buchert's total Q_D^B are not interchangeable.
