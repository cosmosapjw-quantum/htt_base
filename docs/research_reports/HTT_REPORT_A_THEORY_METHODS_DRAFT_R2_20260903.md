## 10. Matrix-valued numerical uncertainty

\]

\[
\Delta\Delta^T
\preceq
N_{\rm fam}\sum_fr_f^2\sum_iE_{fi}E_{fi}^T.
\]

Define

\[
\boxed{

N_{\rm fam}\sum_fr_f^2\sum_iE_{fi}E_{fi}^T
+\lambda_{\rm reg}^2I

\]

This is the conditional Loewner theorem `RA-ERR-001`. The family partition,
basis, radii, calibration set, holdout set, processing settings, and coordinate
identity must be fixed before the rank result is inspected.

\[

omitted or rejected because it leaves the perturbation set unchanged while
changing `N_fam`. These registry invariances are `RA-ERR-002`.

\[

\|\Gamma_E^{-1/2}\Delta\|_2\le1.

## 11. Integrated interpretation

The report separates three questions.

1. **Representation.** Tensorization retains morphology that scalar powers do
   not. On the declared cyclic domain, the Q/O packet separates proper
   rotations, but its arbitrary decoder must respect the packet-image
   syzygies.
2. **Conditional physics.** MES quantities are scalar norm bounds under
   explicit premises. They do not reconstruct a tensor or identify a physical
   spacetime source.
3. **Identifiability.** A complete observable representation can still be
   nonidentifying after masking, transfer, fitting, unresolved source modes,
   and numerical error. The appropriate object is a response quotient or
   identified set, not a forced point estimate.

The continuum calculation demonstrates that an unrestricted deterministic
high-source band can span the retained carrier in the registered model at the
stated evidence grades. It does not assert that physical high multipoles have
unbounded amplitude. A physical covariance/prior, polarization, frequency,
additional angular modes, or independent depth information may refine the
quotient, but each requires its own response and statistical contract.

octupole tensors. On the declared nonzero cyclic chart, a signed Krylov packet

conditional route to robust rank but does not replace proof that the actual

The central conclusion is therefore limited but substantive: tensorization
preserves observable morphology, while physical attribution remains controlled
