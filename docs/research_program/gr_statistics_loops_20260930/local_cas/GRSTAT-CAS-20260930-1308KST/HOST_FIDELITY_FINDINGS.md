# Host statement-fidelity findings

Scope: focused read-only comparison of selected local CAS sources with their frozen execution contracts. This is Host inspection, not independent reviewer completion or scientific admission.

- CAS-01: `wolfram_xact/check.wl` sets `u={1,0,0,0}` before C01, C02 and C04, although C01/C02 quantify over every future unit timelike `u`. Its xAct tensor symmetry calls are real, but its positive check covers a rest-frame specialization. `sage_singular/check.py` uses Sage for the null-cone coefficient ideal; the explicit Singular call reduces the sphere ideal generator by itself, so it does not independently establish the null-cone kernel. The gate's axis PASS values are therefore narrower than the full contract.
- CAS-03-C03: the contract permits every invertible `D=H I+sigma`, but its truncated inverse expression divides by `H^2`. `D=diag(1,1,-2)` has determinant `-2` and `H=tr(D)/3=0`. This is a domain counterexample to the stated truncated-inverse clause, not to the exact inverse relation. See `CAS-03/CORRECTION.md` and the SymPy gate payload. The published contract remains unchanged.
- CAS-11: Wolfram's raw engine output ends with `TerminatedEvaluation[RecursionLimit]` and `$RecursionLimit::reclim` after two local attempts. The wrapper then emitted `execution_error: list index out of range` because no CAS JSON marker existed. `run-adjudicate` reports `CAS_FAIL`; this is an execution/implementation failure, not a mathematical counterexample.
- CAS-13-C04: the Lean source proves a universal positive-interval relative minimax theorem for `0<L<=U`, including a lower bound over every competitor. Sage and SymPy C04 checks verify endpoint equalities only. Wolfram adds an unweighted triangle inequality and candidate interval membership; its source does not show the required weighted endpoint lower bound. Those parser-positive checks therefore do not provide four statement-faithful proofs of C04. Lean C01-C03 remain false.
