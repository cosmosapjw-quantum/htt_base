## 0. Why this registry exists, and what it is for

Three registries currently coexist and none of them is a usable map of what the programme has proved. `THEOREM_REGISTRY.yaml` holds 65 legacy entries and has been byte-frozen since 2026-07-20 for the v9 report lane — the entire premise-anchor cycle PR-253…258 is invisible in it. `THEOREM_SIGNATURES_V2.yaml` mirrors the same 65 with 12 signatures `CHECKED` and 53 `MIGRATION_PENDING`. The genuinely new mathematics of the last cycle — the four `PA-THM-*` gauge theorems, the four `PR257-*` orbit theorems, the two restricted conjectures `J1-EXACT` and `J2-UNIFORM` — lives only in PR-card prose and CAS adjudication JSON.

This document consolidates all of it, adds the tensor-layer results, and splits the whole by **function** rather than by chronology:

* **Pillar I** — statements about geometry, kinematics, group representations, invariant algebra and the physical premise structure. These are what a theoretical-cosmology or mathematical-physics reader adjudicates.
* **Pillar II** — statements about estimands, identifiability, calibration, coverage, optimality and multiplicity. These are what a statistician or a data-analysis referee adjudicates.
* **Bridge** — a small set of propositions that are load-bearing in both and must not be filed in either alone, because filing them in one pillar is how a physical premise silently becomes a statistical guarantee (or the reverse).

A second organising rule follows the standing instruction that this programme's results must not read as a list of things it cannot do. Every entry carries a **Buys** field: what the proposition *enables* — a new derivable result, a new analysis, or a boundary that is itself informative. Entries whose only content is a prohibition or an open question are marked `NO-GO`, and they are deliberately a small minority: 4 of the 58 entries below (`I-2.8` ring completeness, `I-4.2` radial-vorticity blindness, `I-6.4` tensor realisability, `II-1.1` scalar-sector rank-2). Even those four are constructive in the sense that each names the exact object that would remove it.

**Status vocabulary** (unchanged from the legacy registry, extended by two):

`ACTIVE` proven as stated · `ACTIVE_CONDITIONAL` proven under stated premises · `PROVEN_CAS4` proven with a four-axis CAS contract at `CAS_4AXIS_PASS` · `ORACLE_VERIFIED` verified by an executable independent oracle (numeric/symbolic, no CAS contract yet) · `PROPOSED` stated with a derivation, awaiting CAS/independent adjudication · `RESTRICTED` a stronger conjecture cut down by an exact counterexample, with the surviving form registered · `SUPERSEDED` / `RETRACTED` / `WITHHELD` as before.
