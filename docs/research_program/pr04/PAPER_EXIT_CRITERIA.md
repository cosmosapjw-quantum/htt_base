# Paper exit criteria

## PAPER-A — ready for submission preparation when

- every theorem has complete hypotheses, branches, dimensions and sign/frame conventions;
- the radial-vorticity null and single-shell degeneracy are proved analytically;
- practical-rank thresholds are distinguished from exact rank;
- all synthetic coverage and destructive ablations reproduce from one command;
- novelty is framed as cosmological source ownership and theorem-to-estimator architecture, not as invention of SVD or GLS.

## PAPER-B — ready for submission preparation when

- the restricted Bianchi-I matter branch is stated before every theorem;
- species conservation and Gauss-Codazzi transport are independently rederived;
- the shear-memory identity and PSD moment-cone construction are proved;
- the exact FLRW fixture and anisotropic-stress ablation pass;
- prior two-fluid Bianchi-I and stability literature is explicitly separated from the new moment/observability contribution.

## PAPER-C — ready for a result freeze when

- all data/response artifacts carry release hashes and covariance ownership;
- observer boost, local flow and global stress have independent channel support;
- complete mock replay supplies global calibration;
- held-out tracer/channel prediction passes;
- restricted Bianchi-I statements are coverage-calibrated branch tests;
- legacy scalar outputs are posterior pushforwards with complete component provenance.

## PAPER-D — not started until

- the new Rust design pack exists;
- exact FLRW and independent transfer comparators pass;
- Python/Rust golden-contract parity passes;
- family mode/orientation support is truthful and fail-closed.
