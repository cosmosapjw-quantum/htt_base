### Radiation PSTF and nonlinear transport
Gebbie & Ellis; Gebbie, Dunsby & Ellis; Ellis & Dunsby; Challinor.
These establish PSTF radiation multipoles and covariant Boltzmann transport.

### Isotropic radiation / EGS
Clarkson & Barrett and the broader EGS literature establish that exactly isotropic
radiation imposes strong kinematical conditions; acceleration is a crucial premise.
Räsänen emphasizes assumption sensitivity in almost-isotropy arguments.

### General-spacetime cosmography
Heinesen and the later Maartens/Clarkson/Kalbouneh/Marinoni line establish directional
Hubble/distance multipoles and observer-dependence.

### Remote-CMB tomography
Deutsch et al. establish kSZ/pSZ reconstruction of remote dipole/quadrupole fields and
explicitly quantify accessible independent modes.

### Vorticity observability
Coulton, Akitsu & Takada show that late-time vorticity fields can be constrained by
combining kSZ and moving-lens information. Antony Lewis establishes CMB signatures of
primordial vector/vortical modes.

Therefore PAPER-A must not say:
"vorticity is unobservable", "redshift tomography is new", "PSTF radiation is new",
or "CMB anisotropy bounds on kinematics are new".
