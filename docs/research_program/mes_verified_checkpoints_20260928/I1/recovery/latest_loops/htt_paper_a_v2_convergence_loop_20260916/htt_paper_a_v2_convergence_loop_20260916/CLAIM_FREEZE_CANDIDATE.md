# PAPER-A V2 claim freeze candidate

## Allowed core claims

1. Observable low-ell temperature STF tensors and congruence kinematics are distinct typed states.
2. There is no generic instantaneous algebraic Q_ab -> sigma_ab inverse.
3. Local directional cosmography identifies theta, A_a and sigma_ab on the full sky while
   leaving omega_a in the scalar-response kernel.
4. The near-isotropic radiation–cosmography bridge residual is exactly decomposed into
   brightness streaming and collision multipoles at l=0,1,2.
5. The homogeneous collisionless bridge is a corollary, not the generic law.
6. In that strict bridge, optical and radiation-derivative lanes have the same structural
   rank and the same omega null; stacking them supplies consistency/precision, not new
   kinematic dimensions.
7. Redshift tomography lifts same-irrep degeneracies only when the sampled physical response
   kernels are linearly independent.
8. The old single-shell and temporal-STF rank results are corollaries of the redshift-lift theorem.
9. The local-observer STF Q->O response has the previously derived exact inverse/projector and
   conditioning bound, subject to final novelty audit.
10. Exact rank, practical rank, statistical significance and model-family attribution remain
    separate claim tiers.

## Forbidden core claims

- Q_ab is the local shear tensor.
- O_ab... identifies vorticity or Bianchi type without a response model.
- CMB is generically blind to vorticity.
- Multiple redshift bins automatically add independent modes.
- Remote Q(z_1)-Q(z_2) is generically a proper-time derivative.
- The paper invents PSTF radiation transport, EGS, SVD, GLS, Fisher rank, or redshift tomography.
- Current diagnostics constitute an observational detection.
