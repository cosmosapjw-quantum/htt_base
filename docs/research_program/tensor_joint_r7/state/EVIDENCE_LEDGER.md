## Primary source identities and attribution

- [Copi–Huterer–Starkman, astro-ph/0310511](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0310511), [Katz–Weeks, astro-ph/0405631](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0405631), [Land–Magueijo, astro-ph/0502574](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0502574): pre-existing multipole representations, frames and invariants. R2's last author attribution is corrected.
- [Dai–Chluba, 1403.6117](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.6117), [Chluba–Ravenni, 2505.02080](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02080), journal DOI10.1093/mnras/stag698: general boost/spectral operator precedent. This turn read the accessible v1 preprint and journal metadata; no claim of full text identity with the final journal version.
- [MES, astro-ph/9501016](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9501016), [Maartens–Gebbie–Ellis, astro-ph/9808163v2](https://arxiv.org/html/astro-ph/9808163v2): moment/commutator ancestry. T3 derives coefficients using the later hierarchy; it does not silently inherit an isolated inconsistent printed coefficient in the early integrated equation.
- [Hemerik–Goeman](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.7565), [Berger–Boos](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1994.10476836): random-reference and nuisance-confidence testing. Berger–Boos original full text was unavailable; formula/proof here was independently reconstructed, with bibliographic attribution.
- [CF4 catalogue](https://arxiv.org/html/2209.11238v2), [CF4 WF/CR](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.01340v1): actual product organization, calibration and reconstruction assumptions. These do not authenticate unknown local product headers.
- [Fleury–Pitrou–Uzan](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.8473), [Sandin–Uggla](https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.0759): optical and tilted-fluid precedents. R3 is a selected analytical model, not an observationally validated matter description.
- [Pontzen–Challinor](https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.2075), [Vicente–Pereira–Pitrou v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.07786v2), [Saadeh et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.07178): model/transfer and anisotropy constraints; the comparison must preserve modes, initial conditions and units.

SciSpace was used for semantic discovery; its duplicate dates/entries and incomplete retrieval were checked against primary sources. It did not establish novelty or provide the actual source for every formula. Wolfram connection failed with MCP404; no Wolfram/CAS output is cited as evidence.
