## Focused manuscript design

Working title: **Full low-multipole tensor morphology under uncertain observation response**.

Proposed contribution paragraph: “We construct and assess a common representation of the CMB quadrupole and octupole that retains their joint proper-rotation shape and tracks failures of coordinate charts without discarding observations. We connect this representation to physical response through explicit nuisance experiments and simultaneous feasible/confidence sets. The resulting analysis distinguishes stable morphology, assumption-dependent bounds and response nonidentification.” Observational verbs and numerical conclusions are added only after the corresponding execution results exist.

Main text: (1) research question and comparison to multipole vectors; (2) constructive joint orbit and all-strata observation; (3) source/observer/product response and four nuisance laws; (4) joint tensorized MES/physical set; (5) calibrated synthetic and available observed experiment; (6) conclusions and one limitations section. Standard linear algebra, long rational certificates, expanded derivative assumptions and environment details are appendices.

Required figure roles are fixed in DESIGN. The central figure compares identified/compatible regions across H0–H3 and added observed modes. A second figure shows the relationship between mask, leakage singular values and cancellation cost. A third shows calibration/coverage/refusal. Dataset and physical-model expansions may produce separate companion reports; they do not bloat or block the methods manuscript.
