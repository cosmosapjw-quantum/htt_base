### Heinesen 2021, arXiv:2010.06534v2

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2010.06534v2 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.06534v2 . Exa primary text and existing R60 source comparison checked.

\[

Eq. (3.10):

\[

\frac{1-\mathfrak Q_o(e)}{2\mathfrak H_o(e)}z^2+
\frac{-1+3\mathfrak Q_o^2+\mathfrak Q_o-\mathfrak J_o+\mathfrak R_o}{6\mathfrak H_o}z^3+O(z^4).

Vorticity cancels from the symmetric null contraction at this order; this is a statement about that response, not all observations or all Taylor orders.

Regularity, validity of the Taylor truncation, and nonzero directional H in the relevant redshift reparametrization are assumptions. A finite-depth fitted coefficient is an effective windowed coefficient until higher terms/remainders, smoothing/congruence and the exact observation operator connect it to a local derivative.
