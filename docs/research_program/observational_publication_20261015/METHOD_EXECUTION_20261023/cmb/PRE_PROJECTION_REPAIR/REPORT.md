# R24-CMB-ORBIT: retained PR3 processed-carrier experiment

Owner: `obsstat`. Scope: C0 diagnostic on one observed Planck PR3 sky, using the retained common-window processed E pseudoalms of delivered QU. This is a new conditional method result. Original physical and statistical acceptances remain unchanged; no matched total-field law or calibrated p-value exists here.

`orbit.py` verifies the frozen CMB input, all 15 E/B payload/metadata pairs, and the secondary cache before calculation. It records the complete real harmonic coordinates for ell 2 and 3 (5+7), plus complete rank-2 and rank-3 STF coordinates. The real basis is `(a_l0, sqrt(2) Re a_lm, -sqrt(2) Im a_lm)` in Galactic Condon–Shortley convention. The two STF Frobenius blocks use fixed `q0=o0=1e-5 K_CMB` for every product and control. The older angular-momentum `tensor2/tensor3` summaries are separate six-component descriptors and are not used as full STF carriers.

For each pair, the lower bound uses SO(3)-invariant Frobenius norm differences and the quadrupole determinant. Multilinearity gives `|det A-det B| <= 3 M² ||A-B||F`, where `M=max(||A||F,||B||F)`. The lower is the quadrature of the larger quadrupole bound and the octupole norm bound. A Powell search supplies a candidate proper rotation; its binary64 quaternion is interpreted as an exact rational quaternion, so the resulting matrix is exactly SO(3). The upper is its feasible distance, evaluated with 90-digit decimal arithmetic and a conservative numerical inflation. The bounds apply to the fixed stored projected tensors, including degenerate eigenvalue strata. They do not certify the optimizer or carry a sky-processing error envelope.

The result contains 15 pinned primary bundles and 45 separate pilot-cache bundles; the latter cover 0°, 20°, and 30° secondary windows. The 57 comparisons comprise 12 pinned split/full comparisons, 15 primary-versus-pilot-cached 0° comparisons, and 30 same-product pilot window comparisons. At primary L32, split/full lower bounds range from 0.01498 to 0.02232 and feasible upper bounds from 0.02525 to 0.03534. The scalar-equal phase control retains C2/C3 to relative `2.22e-16` and changes the STF2 determinant by `2.322e-22 K_CMB³`; its orbit interval is `[4.815e-5, 0.01725]`. This positive lower bound witnesses full-tensor information lost by the scalar-power control.

The first execution stopped because the pilot's recomputed 0° coefficients are not exactly the separately pinned primary coefficients. The maximum real-harmonic difference is `9.408e-11 K_CMB`. Both are retained as distinct source rows, and their orbit intervals are reported. `PROCESSING_SUPPORT.png` and `.pdf` show the L32 intervals and the exact finite-set support at the 24 signed coordinate directions of the 12-coordinate harmonic representation. This support describes the convex hull at sampled directions only; it does not reconstruct nonconvex processing strata or imply a continuum-direction bound.

No split product or processing view is an independent sky. The experiment has no foreground/common-mode envelope, matched covariance/null generator, calibrated p-value, native low-ell solver output, or Bianchi-family identification. The companion envelope question is a separate task.
