**Observed candidate x-B (same-provider sensitivity):** \(x_{\rm pair}=(v_{\rm peak},v_{\rm integrated})\in\mathbb R^4\), joint 4×4 source overlap covariance. Its difference x_int−x_peak is a selected sample *definition contrast*, not physical cosmic time. R72/R73 already computed this. **Latent physical x-C:** \((\beta_{O|S}^i,\beta_{M|R}^i,U_N,\sigma_{ij},T_{ab},C^k{}_{ij})\) with matter/radiation 4-velocities and general Bianchi \(C^k{}_{ij}=\epsilon_{ij\ell}N^{\ell k}+a_i\delta_j^k-a_j\delta_i^k,\ N^{ij}a_j=0\). \(\Gamma_{MR}=-g(U_M,U_R)/c^2\) is locally invariant and distinct from observer radio-count beta; no physical x-C observed here. **Q:** For a declared working Gaussian null \(x\sim N(X\vartheta,C_6)\), the projection residual \(Q_{\rm score}=\|C_6^{-1/2}(I-P_X)x\|\) is a *working statistical* gauge of conformity to the common-\(\beta_\perp\)+a subspace. Signed boost STF5 morphology remains a *conditional physical prediction* \(Q^{\rm count}_{ij}=(k^2+k+\kappa)\beta_{\langle i}\beta_{j\rangle}/2\), not a measured P4 x.

**Pi:** With fixed k, one a shared across bins and C_6 treated fixed Gaussian, \(\Pi_\mathrm{common}\) is the \(\chi^2_2\) tail of the two residual degrees of freedom after fitting four parameters. It is a **source-conditioned working null** only; Poisson overdispersion/LSS, k uncertainty, selection and post-fit choices change its calibration. A free-k collinearity test has a nonlinear/singular null and may require parametric bootstrap instead of naïve Wilks. R65 actual peak/integrated x-B has a different paired-source \(\Pi\), not independent evidence for velocity.

**F:** Four *different* referents must remain typed: (i) empirical selected-source CMB-axis hemisphere fraction under the actual masked count measure; (ii) conditional multinomial **predicted selected occupancy** \(F_{b,A}(\beta)=\sum_r (N_{br}/N_b)\,p_{br}(A;\beta)\) within fixed declination-ring totals; (iii) hypothetical ideal full-sky round solid-angle fraction of positive STF quadrupole \(F_{\Omega,2}(\chi)\), a deterministic geometric label and not a selected count fraction; (iv) confidence-ellipsoid volume \(F_{\mathcal E}\), a chosen parameter measure and not the source population. Only (i)/(ii) can be computed from the actual R65 count carrier without inventing a physical body.

**G_F:** A genuine physically transported population has a time-/redshift-identified count density ρ(n,τ), fixed or transported event A(τ), source birth/death/selection function and normal/observer ray/sky transport. Peak vs integrated or flux-bin changes do not define G_F.
