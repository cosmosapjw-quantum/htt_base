# Moving Thomson redistribution and a collisionless radiation species

The harmonic Lie operator labels rays by the outward unit vector n. Physical photon momentum in an orthonormal normal tetrad is proportional to (1,−n), with signature (−,+,+,+). Velocity β=v/c is dimensionless. In the conserved-anchor clock the direction-dependent sink is μ=1+n·β and the Doppler factor is D=γμ. The positive scalar clock is proportional to σ_T c times the number of electrons per normal-frame volume times normal proper time. Velocity and geometry are frozen at the midpoint of each e-fold cell. This is a bolometric, cold Thomson, homogeneous, linear brightness calculation on an evolving Bianchi background.

## Energy, flux and anisotropic pressure

For a massless rest-isotropic distribution with normal energy E and velocity v/c=b, the normalized normal-sky brightness is

    1+4Θ(n) = [γ(1+n·b)]⁻⁴ / [γ²(1+b²/3)].

The exact flux and tracefree pressure obey q/E=4b/(3+b²) and π/E=STF(b⊗q/E). The ℓ=1 and ℓ=2 temperature terms are −(3/4)n·q/E and (15/8)n·π·n/E. These follow by integrating −4 nΘ and 4 STF(nn)Θ on the unit sphere. We impose the first two moments analytically and project higher modes from the thermal sky within the retained harmonic space. In the feedback iteration the brightness-derived pressure is normalized with the measured mean intensity 1+4⟨Θ⟩ and the current normal E. The finite initialization test recovers the independent 4×4 thermal stress tensor at β=(0.24,−0.19,0.07).
