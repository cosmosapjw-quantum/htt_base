                                            I.   OBSERVABLE AND PROCESSING

   We compare a fixed polarization morphology statistic in the Planck PR4 Commander full-mission map with the
corresponding released NPIPE/Commander simulations. This is an experiment on processed maps. The related
finite-bandpass calculation uses the 24 real signed coordinates of E and B at multipoles 2 and 3. Here multipole 4
is additionally required by the statistic. The experiment supplies neither a new spectral-rank result nor a measured
bound on foreground moments.
   The observed polarization comes from the NSIDE = 2048 Q and U extension of COM_CMB_IQU-commander_4096_
R4.00_full.fits; the higher-resolution temperature extension is unused. Simulations are the fixed 100 full-mission
products npipe6v20_commander_cmb_2048_mc_0300_005a.fits through ..._0399_005a.fits. Each simulation
identifier contributes one parent-sky realization. Temporal splits and alternative products of the same sky do not add
independent realizations.
   We retain Galactic coordinates and the released COSMO polarization convention. Observed Q and U are in
K_CMB; simulated values are converted from microkelvin to kelvin before analysis. The binary Lollipop mask
retains 1273 of 3072 NSIDE = 16 pixels. Each NESTED parent value is copied to its 16384 NSIDE = 2048 children,
and the same mask is applied to native-resolution Q and U. The masked maps are reordered to RING for spin-two

   For all 100 prescribed realizations, the observed value is 0.2, with 54 inclusive exceedances. The empirical tail frac-
tion is 0.54, and the plus-one rank is 0.544554455446. The 95% binomial interval is [0.437411579866, 0.640156647021],
with half-width 0.101372533577. Figure 1 displays the complete simulation distribution and observed statistic. The
accompanying numerical tables retain every simulation and its signed E/B coefficients.
   Three unit-norm harmonic inputs, E(2,0), a real-field E(3,|m|=1) mode and a real-field B(4,|m|=2) mode, illustrate
the native-resolution mask-and-analysis response in Figure 2. Their outputs retain every E/B component through
multipole 4. They are three selected response columns; they do not reconstruct the full analysis operator or an
upstream NPIPE transfer matrix. The qualified numerical supplement records linearity, masked-pixel, unit, reality,
parity, rotation and retained-coefficient stability checks.
   The comparison quantifies compatibility with this particular processed-map working law. It does not measure
a physical foreground budget, infer radiation stress or matter shear from E, identify a Bianchi family, or establish
unconditional false-positive control.
