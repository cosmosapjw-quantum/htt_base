# R36-02 SAME02 matched-radius sensitivity repair

The independent review found that the original four footprint/method sensitivity summaries compared every subset radius with the **R=5** full-sample block. This affected both comparison fields at R=10 and R=20. The original product and its manifest remain unchanged as the failed candidate and first-falsifier evidence.

This repair reads only the retained `STAGE_C.npz` and `SENSITIVITY_MATRICES.npz` caches. For each of the four subsets and three radii it compares `subset[36r:36(r+1),36r:36(r+1)]` with the **same** block of the full 108-coordinate covariance. It changes eight trace ratios and eight relative Frobenius values. All four R=5 rows, selected counts, traces, ranks, A–X identities, full numerical matrices, conditional mean calculation, Gaussian reference, original figure/table, and R=5 interpretation remain unchanged. No Fourier transform, source contraction, model fit, covariance producer, or old R30 account was run.

The independent counterexample now reads: excluding FP at R=20 h^-1 Mpc gives a subset trace of 16.4553215673 and a matched full-sample trace of 14.0518110353 `(km/s/Mpc)^2`; the ratio is **1.1710463175** and the relative Frobenius change is **0.3561003745**. The old mismatched-radius ratio was 0.9646490579, which reversed the qualitative direction of that sensitivity.

`test_sensitivity_repair.py` checks an identity subset against deliberately distinct covariance blocks at all radii, independently recomputes all twelve actual subset/radius summaries from retained matrices, verifies R=5 parity, and verifies that the result fields outside the sensitivity reference are unchanged. The cache consumer checks the exact original candidate manifest and retained cache hashes before writing. The conditional claim ceiling and empirical `UNKNOWN` fields from the original interpretation remain in force. This corrected candidate is proposed for one targeted independent closeout; acceptance remains false until that review.
