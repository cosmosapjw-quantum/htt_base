## Controlling assumptions and conventions

1. The positive emitter measure mu describes reference intensity in K_CMB at 30 GHz times solid angle. The complex polarization measure eta obeys total-variation domination `|eta| <= mu` in the common Galactic COSMO spin frame. The relevant polarization fraction/phase is frequency independent, or its change has a separate bounded term.
2. For every admitted emitter, intensity is `b0*(nu/nu0)^(-1)*exp(delta*log(nu/nu0))`, with `delta=beta-(-3)` and beta defined in RJ-temperature convention. This is a common, unabsorbed power-law model across the source and target frequencies and epochs, or requires bounded deviations. A far-infrared intensity map does not verify that spectral extrapolation.
3. Each source response has a nonnegative calibrated lower spectral functional, finite strictly positive baseline mass, positive normalization, and finite baseline-weighted mean log frequency. Their means strictly bracket the reference: `a>0`, `b>0`. An aggregate angular/spectral lower domination holds on every consumed emitter atom; alternatively, a direct positive dual inequality dominates the target weights.
4. The source upper bounds are finite and simultaneous under an explicit error model, including relevant gain, offsets, random errors, processing losses and response uncertainty. Source weights are prespecified, or coverage accounts for their selection. Exact beam equality and independent pixel noise are not assumptions.
5. For the displayed target exponential/remainder envelope, every consumed frequency lies in `[-a,b]` in log-frequency coordinates, or additional source domination controls the outside portion. Nonzero unbounded out-of-band response cannot be discarded merely because it is small. The target response and thermodynamic normalization must be included in integration.
6. The stated synchrotron rho formula uses the common retained harmonic operator and existing dimensionless D scales. Applying it to native masked band operators requires their own conservative norm/domination and error bridge. Missing dust/other-emitter rows are not zero.
