# R4: routes for separating the physical mechanisms
At fixed Lie geometry and other inputs define x_0=x(0), x_odd=[x(+e)-x(-e)]/2, x_even=[x(+e)+x(-e)]/2-x_0. Exact identity x(±e)=x_0±x_odd+x_even. x_0 captures the zero-initial-shear trajectory (not necessarily only curvature in arbitrary matter/tilt settings); x_odd contains odd shear response, x_even nonlinear even response, neither automatically a perturbative coefficient. Use actual signed vectors, not differences of norms. At balanced VI with zero shear, curvature sources a nonzero trajectory, and joint N,A contraction makes its initial tracefree curvature O(lambda²). Hypothesis: at sufficiently small lambda, finite-time x_0 is quadratic in lambda; test slopes without declaring global convergence. Balanced VII x_0 is a numerical isotropy control in this closure. At finite k,A0, u changes anisotropic Ricci at linear order; scan around u~10^-3 to resolve near-balanced transition.
