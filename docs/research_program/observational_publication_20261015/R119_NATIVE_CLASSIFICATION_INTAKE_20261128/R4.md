# R4: candidate classification maps
Candidate A: forward deterministic signed sky as finite points, with orientation as a shared SO(3) action per l and channel. Compare x to a U(R)y with a>=0 under explicitly declared metric. This is geometry-only with Euclidean dimensionless inner product, not a likelihood.
Candidate B: per-(l,X) block norms and C_l^{XY} as orbit invariants. Fast necessary comparison; loses signed relative phases between multipoles and channels. Use block-norm bound to bracket A. A third possible extension is orientation-resolved survey response R followed by C^{-1/2}; defer because matched stochastic covariance, masks and priors are not available in this intake.
Minimal case: l=2 STF tensor diag(2,-1,-1) and its negative have equal quadratic norm but different eigenvalue multiset, hence no shared SO(3) rotation maps one into the other. Power-only morphology is incomplete even without a mask. Use named finite representatives, no continuous h interpolation yet.
