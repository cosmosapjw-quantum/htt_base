## R9 mathematical statements (derived, finite domain)

Let `L,H` be the low/high real spin-2 Stokes Q/U design matrices, with the same nonnegative pixel weights `W` for Q and U and nonsingular low Gram matrix. Then `K_W=(L^T W L)^(-1)L^T W H`. For a **single common high-mode source** `h`, the two-mask contrast is `Δy=(K_t-K_c)h=ΔK h`. Under an externally specified coefficient-space ball `||h||<=R`, the sharp bound is `sup||Δy||=R||ΔK||`. For high-E input alone and low-B output it is `0.2564294649638681 R_E` in the reported unit-coefficient normalization. Neither R nor R_E is currently physically constrained.

Under exact `rank(ΔK)=64`, any target `d∈R^64` can be generated by a source in the `90`-dimensional ell6..8 coefficient space, and the unique minimum-norm witness is `h*=ΔK^T(ΔK ΔK^T)^(-1)d`. Consequently no nonzero low contrast `v^T Δy` cancels **all unrestricted** ell6..8 sources. The observed numerical rank 64 does not establish certified exact rank without a justified operator-error upper bound. This is elementary finite linear algebra, NOT a novel physical no-go claim.

For covariance `C_h` of **one same-sky** high-coefficient draw: `Cov(y_c,y_t)=K_c C_h K_t^T` and `Cov(y_t-y_c)=ΔK C_h ΔK^T`. Treating the two masks as independent skies drops cross terms and is not admitted.

## Conditional effective beam adapter (derived)

If the released full-mission SMICA `POL_BEAM` values can be represented by a common axisymmetric, E/B-equal harmonic diagonal `D_L,D_H`, with no zeros at low ell, and `d=L D_L a_L + H D_H a_H`, then ordinary weighted OLS gives `c_hat=D_L a_L + K D_H a_H`; low-deconvolved coefficients have `K_beam=D_L^(-1) K D_H`. For the **same** physical map and two masks, the observed-low response difference is `ΔK D_H`, and low-deconvolved response difference is `D_L^(-1) ΔK D_H`. An exact rational manufactured Wolfram test returned `Identity=True`; sandbox tests separately cover the numerical construction.

This diagonal approximation is NOT the full Planck SMICA transfer: Planck 2018 separately processes polarized E/B, includes frequency-dependent component separation, masks/inpainting and systematics. Actual polarized Q/U sky, ell>=9 tail, anisotropic beams, foreground/noise and matched FFP10 null remain NOT_EVALUATED. In particular, physical high-ell leakage has no certified upper bound from the ell6..8 finite matrix alone.
