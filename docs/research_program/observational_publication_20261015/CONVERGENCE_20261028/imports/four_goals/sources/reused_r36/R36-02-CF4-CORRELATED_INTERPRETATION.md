# R36-02: correlated CF4 response of the exact H03 operator

This is a conditional covariance calculation for the 35,970 selected CF4 source groups and the reviewed 36-coordinate, four-window H03 operator. The observed statistic is the raw fixed-distance, fixed-weight apparent H03 fit. Directions are outward ICRS J2000, redshifts are released `z_CMB` proxies, the metric convention is `(-,+,+,+)`, and `c=299792.458 km/s`. The source radial velocity is in km/s; `A` maps it to km/s/Mpc. The source position is `chi(z_CMB)n` in Mpc in the declared flat matter plus Lambda background. A random observer velocity is absent because the CMB-frame correction is fixed metadata in this calculation. The source and observer constant-velocity responses are opposite, so a common shift is a null direction.

The power spectrum is the source-qualified [Eisenstein–Hu no-wiggle analytic fit](https://background.uchicago.edu/~whu/transfer/transferpage.html) with the retained R30 parameters and sigma8 normalization. The angular radial-velocity kernel follows the linear common-field construction used in [Whitford, Howlett and Davis (2023)](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.11269v2); the local R30 contract supplies the exact finite convention. This is an authored Gaussian-field approximation over only `0.005–0.05 h/Mpc`, split at `0.02 h/Mpc`. It omits power outside these bands, nonlinear velocities, a physical mixed-distance-method response, calibration/PV cross sources, and an empirical CF4 selection law. The same present-day density realization supplies every source, method, epoch, window and smoothing radius.

At each positive Fourier node the response is `g_i (n_i·u) exp(i k u·x_i)`, with no R30 magnitude derivative `j_i`. Its real and imaginary projected columns are multiplied by `sqrt(P0(k) w_k w_Omega/(2pi)^3)` and a shared Gaussian smoother. Their Gram matrices give two positive-semidefinite 108×108 covariances for three radii (`5,10,20 h^-1 Mpc`) and 36 H03 coordinates. The full matrices and projected factors are in the external artifact directory; the figure displays the R=5 cross-coordinate correlation for each band. No block-diagonal window or radius assumption is made.

The inherited H03 working variance is `250^2 + [70 ln(10) d e_dmzp/5]^2` in `(km/s)^2`. It is a weighting law, with no demonstrated split into independent measurement error and PV. The numerical total instead **stipulates** an independent 150 km/s source term, four independent 0.4 km/s/Mpc method *mean-design* offsets, and this restricted PV band component. `K=A[d 1_method]` carries mean offset units; it is not a magnitude zero-point Jacobian. A magnitude calibration law would require the derivative of distance, design, weights and estimator. `CONTROLS.json` varies the two stipulated amplitudes and leaves omitted bands and cross-source covariance `UNKNOWN`. The baseline is shown separately and is not added to or subtracted from the conditional total.

The four refitted subset operators expose sensitivity to catalogue support and method mix. The R=5 PV trace ratios to the full operator are 2.405 for north ICRS (`n_z≥0`, 27,800 groups), 2.580 for south (8,170), 1.173 after excluding FP (9,054 remain), and 0.867 after excluding TF (28,356 remain). Each subset retains the same frozen H03 extraction form and has `A X≈I`. These are changes in operator and footprint under the same Gaussian field; the overlapping CF4 groups are not independent confirmations. The large hemispheric changes make an actual footprint and selection law material to physical interpretation.

Under the one stipulated R=5 total and a *known* Gaussian covariance, three prespecified mean designs were fit to the same 36 H03 coordinates: shared monopole, affine monopole across the four centers, and shared nine-coordinate mean. Minimum `chi2 + rank ln(36)` selects the shared nine-coordinate mean (scores 178.51, 152.33, 112.39 respectively). A deterministic 2,000-draw Gaussian calculation under the fitted shared-monopole reference gives a selected-statistic tail of 0.00050, at its one-count resolution with MC standard error 0.00050. It is a falsifier of that stipulated reference, not an empirical CF4 p-value or Bianchi identification. Different `D`, calibration law, omitted PV, and selection can change this result. For a hypothetical 64-mock estimate at p=36, the inverse factor would be 26/63 and a diagonal covariance element would have 17.8% fractional sampling SD under a Gaussian Wishart law; no selected source mocks are supplied, so actual source-population coverage is `UNKNOWN`.
