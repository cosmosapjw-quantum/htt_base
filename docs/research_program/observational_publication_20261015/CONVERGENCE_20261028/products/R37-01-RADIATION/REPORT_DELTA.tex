\section{R37-01 radiation and STF5 shear
sensitivity}\label{r37-01-radiation-and-stf5-shear-sensitivity}}

The positive synthetic R36 a0.02\_b0.3 temperature sky and saved paired
R36 noise draws determine this conditional methods image. The same sky,
local observer, nuisance, correlated eight-coordinate temperature law,
and constructed central derivative target are used for every computable
adapter. The derivative is an \textbf{assumption}, not a measured
radiation history.

Full direct quadrature and exact (rho, pi, H4) common-carrier response
differ by 8.88e-16 in normalized energy units, below the preregistered
2e-10 tolerance. The full response eigenvalues are {[}0.539469 0.539473
0.637188 0.638056 0.664453{]} and its condition number is 1.2317. The
generic joint gain/shear carrier has rank 23, or 20 at V=0, as reused
from Goal 1. For this fixed screen profile the actual angular kernels
have ranks 15 (full), 11 (H4-dropped with separate rho/pi/P\_star), and
6 (true V=0 Thomson gain M). The screen remains positive with minimum
physical eigenvalue 0.41555.

The H4-dropped closure retains separate rho, pi and P\_star; its central
STF5 bias norm is 4.7577e-05. It is not a gain-only estimator. At fixed
R\_tau and fixed screen profile, two strictly positive brightness fields
have the same true rank-6 gain M to 1.11e-15 yet their true shear
solutions differ in norm by 0.000118028. Thus M alone identifies a set,
not one shear. This deterministic positive fibre witness is separate
from the retained R36 stochastic draw law. GAIN\_IDENTIFIED\_SUPPORT.csv
gives a two-member inner witness of this set and its common-disk
physical sensitivity; the full positive M-fibre may be wider. Omitting
P\_star with the exact intensity response gives full-minus-unpolarized
bias norm 0.0085038, bounded by gamma\_T
\textbar\textbar P\_star\textbar\textbar/(5 lambda\_min) = 0.0087824.

One shared statistical event uses the maximum standardized centered
shear deviation over all 20 mode-coordinate pairs on the retained 8191
calibration draws. Its higher-quantile threshold is 1.27089;
simultaneous centered test coverage on the same 4096 saved draws is
3888/4096 = 0.949219. The earlier five marginal full-response intervals
jointly hit only 3389/4096, retained as a regression diagnostic.
MATCHED\_COVERAGE.csv labels marginal diagnostics separately and reports
joint radii, widths, target coverage and signed bias. The common event
includes saved-draw operator variation because each response is
recomputed on each draw.

The physical sensitivity uses a single two-coordinate unit-disk
primitive z. The same z drives derivative and polarization errors
through the displayed U,V matrices; their effects are summed before one
central inverse map. SHEAR\_SUPPORT.csv gives fixed-operator directional
support over the declared budgets. Its triangle half-width is a
sensitivity layer; combined physical/statistical coverage has not been
calibrated. Structural ranks and five-trial median inference-only
timings on identical precomputed test arrays appear in JSON; timings
exclude carrier extraction.

The figures and tables have owner htt, claim tier
conditional\_\allowbreak{}synthetic\_\allowbreak{}method\_\allowbreak{}experiment, artifact mode
finite\_methods\_image, transfer source none, synthetic null status and
the same R36 correlated noise law. Allowed use is a conditional
response/shear sensitivity image. Empirical shear, native solver
validation and Bianchi family identification are forbidden uses.
Original 42-node acceptance remains 17/42; analytic alternate remains
18/42.
