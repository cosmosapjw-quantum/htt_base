\hypertarget{geometry-and-physical-domain}{%
\subsection{Geometry and physical
domain}\label{geometry-and-physical-domain}}

In the R36 oriented orthonormal hypersurface-normal frame, signature
\texttt{(-,+,+,+)}, \texttt{epsilon\_123=+1}, Ricci has units
\texttt{u\_*\^{}2}. The retained II and unequal-VII0 witnesses are, up
to proper rotations,

\texttt{R\_II\ =\ diag(1/2,-1/2,-1/2)},
\texttt{R\_VII0\ =\ diag(-1/2,3/2,-3/2)}.

Their trace is \texttt{-1/2} in both cases, whereas \texttt{tr(R\^{}2)}
is \texttt{3/4} and \texttt{19/4}. The trace-free quadratic values
differ by the same \texttt{4}. These are actual R36 supplied finite
geometries, not a complete parameterization of either Bianchi type. For
a declared conditional family path, multiplying each source spatial
structure coefficient by the same dimensionless amplitude
\texttt{a\ in\ {[}0,1{]}} gives \texttt{R(a)=a\^{}2\ R(1)} by curvature
homogeneity. The nuisance and noise law are identical between the
competing paths. At each fixed \texttt{a}, each family permits its own
proper \texttt{SO(3)} orientation. The figures sample
\texttt{a=0,1/8,...,1}; the reported separation is analytic over the
full continuous path. No source history, polarization transport, native
solver, or ODE evolution is supplied by this scaling.

For a single symmetric tensor under isotropic Frobenius error,
minimizing over proper rotations is exactly the Euclidean distance
between sorted eigenvalue triples. The source witness therefore has
\texttt{delta(a)=sqrt(2)\ a\^{}2}. This is an \textbf{attained analytic
minimum over orientations}, not a finite-grid upper bound. Its global
infimum over \texttt{a\ in\ {[}0,1{]}} is zero at the common Ricci
origin. The retained I and flat-VII0 controls also give identical flat
local geometry through distinct simply transitive presentations. We
preserve both labels as equivalent presentations; the compatible output
can contain both. Local Ricci spectra alone cannot label a universal
Bianchi presentation. The \texttt{a=0} statement is only a spatial-Ricci
origin overlap; it does not establish a full FLRW spacetime, including
its extrinsic geometry.

The Gauss bridge used by the imported goal-3 analysis requires the
electric Weyl tensor, anisotropic stress, normal-frame \texttt{h} and
\texttt{Sigma}, density, and cosmological term, with a common congruence
and conventions. A shear tensor alone cannot provide \texttt{R\_ij}. The
imported direct-index Gauss check and independent review were read; this
task did not replay their producers.

discarded-space adequacy residual; retaining it permits rejection of
every listed family. If amplitude is jointly fitted rather than

set is \emph{all} family orbits whose profiled distance from the same

\texttt{sqrt(2)a\^{}2-2tau\textgreater{}0}. The plots'
