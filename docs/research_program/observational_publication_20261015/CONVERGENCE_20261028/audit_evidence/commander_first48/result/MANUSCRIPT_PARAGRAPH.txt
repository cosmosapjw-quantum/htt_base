Using the fixed first 48 Commander FULL005a processed simulations (MC IDs 300–347), we estimated the conditional ensemble location of 42 signed-real E/B coefficients at ell=2–4 after the shared Galactic Lollipop-mask/spin-2 operator. The estimate includes any fixed foreground residual and processing/instrument mean and therefore does not isolate either contribution. The sample mean has Euclidean norm 0.0702027 microK_CMB. Under a declared conditional iid Gaussian working law, the finite-sample 95% Hotelling ellipsoid has denominator df=6; its conservative bound on the norm of the total mean is [0, 0.828323] microK_CMB, and the zero-mean model contrast has nominal F-tail 0.00254247. This exploratory analysis was designed after partial exposure, and nonlinear release processing does not establish coefficient Gaussianity or observed/MC exchangeability. The result does not resolve the original physical foreground radius, a foreground-only contamination level, or Bianchi family identification; independent scientific review is pending.
