# C2: source-to-observable route
Current BASS _rustcore/examples/homogeneous_sky.rs consumes --lie-ic as 6 independent STF/shear components and symmetric N, then vector A; initial_lie_node derives positive Omega and required_flux from production Codazzi sign. solve_general selects coupled native harmonic route, recoil, and --recombination matched via REC matched_history. REI current bass_ionization binary takes prescribed HyRec and AX08 mean-clock history, produces eta,xe_per_H and records z<4 conditional continuation. Bind actual source lock and SHA; never treat AX08 donor as a new self-consistent solution for each family.
BASS run.txt gives lie_type_initial/final, h, host/radiation/observer velocities and matched REC delta_xe; diagnostics.tsv gives time-resolved constraint and screen metrics; recombination.tsv gives eta,xe_table,delta_xe,xe; los_modes.tsv/los_cl.tsv are all-m deterministic sky and deterministic pair spectra. Use R119 decode_signed_modes() and profile_bounds() with finite exact labels. For parameter-manifold sensitivity use derivatives of signed mode vector, not C_l ratios alone.
