Central derivative D_e=(x(p+e)-x(p-e))/(2e), O(e²) if smooth; compare e/2 and use same mode band. Projection in real coordinates of complex signed sky against amplitude and the three infinitesimal SO(3) rotation directions uses SVD, reporting nuisance rank and singular values. Divide by ||x|| only for nonzero sky; no observational rank threshold. Separately compare 800 vs 1600 steps for the .02 stencil, holding angular sampling and lmax fixed; also compare angular/multipole cutoffs at selected centers to expose sensitivity rather than conflate errors. Record raw differences, do not silently extrapolate.
