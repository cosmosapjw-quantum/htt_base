# R3: convention and physical admissibility
Signature (-,+,+,+), right-handed normal tetrad, Hubble-normalized N,A (physical structure constants in inverse length multiplied by c/H), Sigma=shear/H. eta=ln(a_bar/a_emit), zbar+1=(1+z_emit)e^-eta, not ray-observed redshift. In BASS geom/lie.rs and kinetic/lie_history.rs: C^k_ij=epsilon_ijl N^lk+A_i delta^k_j-A_j delta^k_i, Jacobi NA=0. K=-c^2 R^(3)/(6 H^2); Omega_E=8 pi G E/(3 c^2 H^2) for normal energy density E in SI. Gauss gives Omega_E=1-Sigma:Sigma/6-K-Omega_Lambda. kinetic/radiation_feedback.rs defines divSigma=-(3 Sigma A+axial(N Sigma)); signed Codazzi residual is divSigma+3 Omega_E q/E, not minus. q denotes normal flux in energy-density units. Required q/E=(3 Sigma A+axial(N Sigma))/(3 Omega_E). Positive Omega_E and |q/E|<1 are necessary; detailed baryon/other stream split and dynamical constraints remain native solver responsibilities. Host observer is not the normal or radiation-energy frame. Signed LOS output uses COSMO E/B, source dimensionless units; no unverified conversion to microkelvin.
