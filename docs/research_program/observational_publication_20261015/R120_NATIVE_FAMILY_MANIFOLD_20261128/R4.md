# R4: constructive chart and competing observables

At fixed k and u, A0->0 gives VI0/VII0; at fixed A0>0 and finite u, k->0 gives V; both vanishing gives I. These are algebra contraction/closure statements, not an exact FLRW-limit claim for every type. On negative branch h=-1 and -1/9 are separately labeled III and VI*, not generic labels; h is undefined on N=0 and not a regular chart at A0=0 (use A0). Baseline k=.01, A0=.005, u=0, common R114 shear six components; finite perturbations in log k, log A0, u and shear scale.
