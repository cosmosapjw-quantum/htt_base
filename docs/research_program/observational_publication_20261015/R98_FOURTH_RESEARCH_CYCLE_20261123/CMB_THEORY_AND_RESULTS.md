### Derivation and premises

At NSIDE 2048, apply the inherited linear T monopole/dipole projection on the relevant intensity mask. In the harmonic basis, P[a] = a − Bᵀ (U Bᵀ)⁻¹ U a, where U extracts the four real low-order moments and B holds the transforms of the four masked real-space basis functions. Thus U P = 0, P² = P and P is linear; E and B already include spin-2 masked map transforms. Use R97's common-mask projection on all three cached streams. The separate split diagnostic uses its own support and OLS basis. Harmonics are thermodynamic µK; angular spectra are µK².

With R94's signed convention define the dimensionless band estimate of a channel by summing G(l,m,M) a(l,m) a(l+2,m−M)* / [C(l)+C(l+2)] and dividing by the band's sum of G². The four lower-l ranges are 2–8, 9–16, 17–32 and 33–46. Retain M=0 real, and √2 times the real and imaginary parts of M=1,2. This gives 20 real coordinates in each auto-channel T, E and B. T uses the published Planck fiducial spectrum. E/B normalization uses the squared pseudo-harmonics of training pairs only, with equal mass per distinct noise group. The five components retain orientations. Hajian & Souradeep derive the SI harmonic covariance and bipolar coefficients and discuss the mask and variance (equation 1 and harmonic estimator); Hanson & Lewis explain realistic mask/noise mean fields and optimal weak-anisotropy weighting. The computation here uses inherited finite-band projections and empirical means. Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0308001 ; https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.80.063004 .

For a training sample of G distinct groups assign each row of group g weight 1/(G n_g). Compute the weighted empirical central moment C, set D=diag(C) and Cα=(1−α)C+αD. Minimize weighted squared centered B prediction error plus 10‖W‖² using standardized T/E predictors. The map (T,E,B) → (T,E,B−WᵀZ−b) is a triangular affine shear with determinant one. For α=0, replacement of C by ACAᵀ leaves both the Mahalanobis statistic and the log determinant unchanged exactly. For α>0, diagonal shrinkage generally fails to commute with a shear, so comparing raw and ridge-corrected scores measures a regularization sensitivity; any score change alone cannot establish physical E→B removal. The diagonal score (α=1) supplies a further fixed baseline. Held-out Gaussian working-model negative log densities include the determinant and use identical feature coordinates within each fold. Their differences are predictive log-score comparisons; estimating finite tails uses the held-out data.

For fixed calibration fold f compare its SMICA score against one predesignated independent-noise representative per held-out group. Cross-fit training sets overlap and reuse a common observed sky. The combined fraction is descriptive with no exact Monte Carlo rank or iid uncertainty.
