- **NB1 law and holdout.** Böhme et al. equations 4, 6–8 give the probability convention and moment prescription used here. With r=exp(η)(1−p)/p, the derivative of the log density with respect to η is r[ψ(y+r)−ψ(r)+log(1−p)]. On distinct fixed cases with p=0.001, 0.12, 0.66, 0.98, direct agreement with scipy.stats.nbinom logpmf was at worst 1.1×10⁻¹⁰. A centered finite difference of the independent exact rising-factorial product agreed with the score to better than 8.2×10⁻⁸ in |Δ|/(1+|score|). The real four-fold moments on all 19 radio flux thresholds lie between 0.06765 and 0.68619, and the mask/patch indices exclude test cells from estimating p. Masks estimated from catalog information remain fixed, so predictive results condition on the existing footprint. https://arxiv.org/html/2509.16732v1 .
- **Repaired optimizer.** For thin X=QR, Z=√n Q and θ=Rβ/√n, Zθ=Xβ follows identically. Whitened gradients correspond by a nonsingular linear transform for full column rank; the model and likelihood are preserved. Reran the 16 actual SUMSS NB1-9 training sets for which the original optimizer encountered a convergence problem. The largest NLL disagreement with a direct scipy.stats.nbinom evaluation was 4.53×10⁻¹⁴ and the largest ordinary or whitened mean gradient component was 7.13×10⁻⁷.
- **Gaia covariance and prediction.** Reconstructed the 160×160 covariance from all 48 synchronized deletion coefficient vectors via (47²/48) times their ordinary sample covariance. Maximum error versus stored matrix 1.78×10⁻¹⁵ in (µas yr⁻¹)². Rank 47; singular values 47 and 48 were approximately 0.03359 and 2.76×10⁻¹⁴. The six separately reconstructed linear contrast matrices agreed within 5.33×10⁻¹⁵. Using the same inverse-noise reference yy, s, F for each view recovered the four reported out-of-patch χ² totals within 10⁻⁸. Every reference coefficient was fitted with its own test patch removed.
- **Coverage.** Actual radio nonempty-deletion supports in the 48 common sky patches are NVSS 42, RACS 40, SPICE-RACS 44, SUMSS 18 and LoTSS 26. A survey with an empty patch retains its fitted full-data statistic on that deletion. This is a diagnostic shared-sky contrast with heterogeneous footprint support and no calibrated cosmological p-value.
