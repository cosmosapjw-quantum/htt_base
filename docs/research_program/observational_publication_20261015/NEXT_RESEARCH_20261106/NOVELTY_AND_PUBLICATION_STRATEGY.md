# Novelty / significance tribunal과 publication strategy

## 현재 판정

현재 시점에 새로운 physical discovery 또는 publication priority를 주장할 수 없다. 기존 연구는 observer-dependent Hubble multipoles, Doppler/aberration separation, redshift-selected kinematic matter dipole, multipole morphology, radio/AGN count dipoles and Bianchi CMB transfer를 이미 다룬다.

가장 강한 후보는 **frame-resolved, selection-aware multi-probe identifiability**다. 즉, 물리적으로 정의된 \(O/U\), \(U/N\), CMB aberration, number-count/flux dipoles, redshift tomography, radial distance와 proper motion을 공통 covariance/nuisance/finite-remainder law 아래 연결하고, 어떤 관측 설계에서 실제 목표량을 식별할 수 있는지 조건과 반례를 제시한다.

## 정리와 적용의 구별

For fixed local experiment \(A=P_NC^{-1/2}R\) and target \(L\theta\), local estimability is equivalent to \(\ker A\subseteq\ker L\), or \(\operatorname{rank}([A;L])=\operatorname{rank}(A)\). This is standard linear estimability, not novelty by itself.

Candidate contribution is the physically correct construction of \(R,N,C,L\) from observer/source Lorentz transforms, observed-redshift migration, radial/transverse congruence response, survey selection, and bounded physical errors. For nonlinear competitors, compare their common-operator model images and preserve the out-of-family residual. Under bounded parameter/remainder sets, positive minimum mean-image distance can establish deterministic mean-set separation. With Gaussian noise, densities still have common support; calibrated power/FPR is required.

## Potential paper sequence

1. Methods/theory: frame-pair theorem, local response quotient, finite-volume stochastic bridge, explicit degeneracies and sufficient multi-probe conditions.
2. Dipole data: DESI DR1 + radio/quasar + CMB aberration anchor, after exact-selection mock covariance.
3. Directional cosmography: CF4/Pantheon+/Gaia radial-transverse response, after source/frame covariance.
4. Low-\u2113/Bianchi: only after matched PR4 T/E/B pipeline/null and validated native Bianchi transfer.

L4 sky classification remains a future-gated branch. The Commander MC100 result is not a discovery. A methods paper may be publishable before empirical family identification, but only with a narrow conditional scope and complete prior-art differentiation.

## Claim tiers

- C0: descriptive feature or processed-map rank statistic.
- C1: conditional mathematical/response result with exact premises.
- C2: source-bound observational result with selected law, covariance, matched nulls and holdout.
- C3: physical inference with identifiable \(O/U,U/N\), validated family/transfer and adequate competitors.
- C4: novelty/publication readiness after independent replication, adversarial review and claim-to-source mapping.

No automatic upward promotion.
