# R99 · Shared angular nuisance, exposure, and source-law boundaries

## R4–R5: a constructive common Gaia field

R98 retained the exact 48-patch sufficient normal matrices F_{ap} for each of ten VSH fits a, with F_a=sum_p F_{ap}. Its first six design columns are three glide and three spin components in the actual source basis. Let E select those columns. Specify a piecewise field on the same patches by six coefficients g_p (microas/year), so the contribution to any source in patch p is the already defined tangent design B_i E g_p. This is a declared coarse angular random-field model, not an estimate of AGIS or AGN covariance.

The coefficient response follows directly from weighted least squares: delta beta_a=sum_p J_ap g_p, J_ap=F_a^{-1} F_ap E. All fits use the SAME g_p. Define a positive-semidefinite spatial covariance on patch centers n_p by K_pq=exp[-||n_p-n_q||^2/(2 ell^2)] (ell dimensionless chord length, with ell=2 sin(theta/2) for a displayed angular scale theta). This restriction of a Euclidean Gaussian kernel to the sphere is PSD. If each of the six coefficient fields has variance s^2 and fields are mutually independent, Cov(g)=s^2 K tensor I6 and C_sys,ab=s^2 J_a(K tensor I6)J_b^T. This has units (microas/year)^2 and is invariant to a common rigid rotation of the Cartesian vector fields and sky.

For a fit contrast, use J_a-J_b before forming covariance. In the constant-field limit K=11^T, sum_p J_ap=E for every nonsingular fit. Thus the common glide/spin shift cancels EXACTLY in the contrast, even if source weights/redshift distributions differ. Independent nuisance draws per fit would destroy that cancellation and overstate the contrast variance. At finite angular scale, different source weights and sky sampling give different J_ap, so an apparent redshift contrast can respond to one shared angular field. This is a calculable source/frame confounder, not a measured cosmological effect.

Apply to the full-source R98 sufficient statistics after checking the cache SHA `70d1bc9c959ccfa0197923fc7d7e950ac790eaa1345ca0aca507ade446464c92`. No source rows are synthesized. Report C_sys alone and shared-versus-independent contrast traces for stated theta and s. The R98 spatial jackknife already contains some spatial variation, so adding C_sys to that covariance would double count unknown components; this cycle must NOT report their sum as a calibrated total covariance or derive a p-value. The unknown independent measurement cross-covariance and source AGN law stay explicit. Independently report the Euclidean component of the 160D observed vector outside the rank-47 patch covariance span; a projected quadratic form cannot test that component.

Validation contracts: constant common-field cancellation, PSD and symmetry, response against direct perturbed normal equations, and a finite correlated random-field draw covariance. Use amplitude as a scenario coefficient, not an externally calibrated Gaia variance. Source evidence: R98 `cross_survey.py::{add_gaia,summarize_gaia,gaia_run}` and `GAIA_FULL_SHARED_PATCH.json`; Gaia-CRF3 primary article https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.12574 confirms source/frame ownership; Gaia EDR3/DR3 astrometry sharing is described in that release. The literature does not justify substituting a parallax covariance formula for proper-motion covariance.

## R4–R5: radio footprint as exposure

The LoTSS official `lotss_dr3.mosaics` TAP table supplies 1,580 coverage polygons, WCS and record identifiers. These are outer mosaic boundaries independent of detected source positions. A union of rasterized polygons yields a reproducible geometric exposure f_p in [0,1] for HEALPix cell p; use a finer fixed NSIDE and aggregate to target NSIDE. Changing fine NSIDE tests boundary discretization. This is an outer support approximation, not the valid-pixel/RMS/weight mask. Image interiors may have holes; source-size and flux-dependent completeness remains unqualified.

For a conditional source-count model with known exposure e_p=Omega_p f_p, expected counts are mu_p=e_p exp(X_p b), so eta_p=log e_p+X_p b. The offset is additive and has no fitted free coefficient. Include observed zero cells inside independent support. Outside support e_p=0, positive observed counts indicate a source/coverage mismatch rather than a finite log intensity. Multiple overlapping mosaics form a union for sky area; summing polygons would count repeated sky. A source-level completeness and component-association law, when supplied, modifies e or the likelihood; geometric coverage alone is not that law. The observed catalogue's own occupied-cell map must not serve as the null's footprint.

The code target is an acquisition manifest plus saved metadata and outer-footprint rasterization, with a direct comparison to the existing LoTSS mask/cell counts. Poisson/NB1/LSS inference beyond this geometrical input remains separate. Official sources: https://vo.astron.nl/lotss_dr3/q/query_mosaics/form, https://vo.astron.nl/tableinfo/lotss_dr3.mosaics, https://lofar-surveys.org/dr3.html . Availability must be read at execution time: a public record saying Online does not override HTTP 202 from its content endpoint. Stable content URLs are retained, not signed redirects.

## R6–R7: independent selection-normalization discriminator

A scalar common field F~N(0,0.7^2), independent m_i|F~N(F,1), and selection Phi(-m_i/0.5) gives conditional alpha(F)=Phi[-F/sqrt(1.25)]. The proper joint inclusion probability is integral p(F)alpha(F)^2 dF = 0.29543408338464494, whereas the product of marginal probabilities is 0.25. Direct one-dimensional quadrature gives E[m_1 | both included]=-1.013467996719508. Integrating per-field normalized likelihoods under the unconditional field gives -0.751555256937466, a different field-selection model. At (m1,m2)=(0.1,-0.2), respective densities are 0.10309602197523607 and 0.19289770026412686. Parent executed scipy.integrate.quad with epsabs=1e-12 on [-9,9], over twelve prior standard deviations. These are fixed demonstrator values, not CF4 measurements. They check the joint normalization derived in THEORY_CF4.md.

P1: corrected magnitude responses and common-field normalization clarify the pushforward law. Native-mark inverse-inclusion bounds concern source populations; they cannot be applied to the 2,048 P1 covariance draws as if those were target counts. Physical anchor and calibrated common law remain required.

P2: keep Commander product release and signal/noise IDs explicit. R98 prepared PR3/FFP10 Commander fullmission signal/noise ID00000 at 603,987,840 bytes each. A noise-file job and its size/header validation are acquisition preparation, not a matched null. Signal, noise, foreground, weight fitting and masks must use the same operator and genealogy. PR3 and PR4/NPIPE are distinct releases; no implicit substitution is made. Existing full CMB cache and observed split conversions are reused, not rerun without a changed law.

P3: THEORY_CF4.md supplies selected-mark likelihood, observable-specific finite-z response, corrected mag covariance, nonlinear source moments and source-support sensitivity. New public SDSS upstream FP fields/randoms/mocks open a reconstructible subset route; they are not a complete CF4 joint calibration posterior.

P4: new independent outer-footprint metadata supplies previously missing geometry, while RMS/weight and LSS/SED remain. Common local/global responses must be compared in one covariance and frame. No per-survey independence is inferred from separate files.

P5: THEORY_OPTICAL.md gives actual distance transport on the same R49 geometry. This is a missing classical optical capability, independent of the external native low-ell Boltzmann solver. R98 one-loop radiation feedback is preserved; self-consistent coupled radiation/chemistry and primordial atlas remain separate model work.

P6: optical distance with emitter/observer Doppler factors and a shared angular nuisance model reduce two distinct forward-model gaps. Proper-motion time drift requires a two-observer-time variation and source motion, not D_A alone. No normal shear or vorticity is inferred from these source-frame coefficients.
