# Observed Gaia angular-pair spin quotient

This is a conditional diagnostic on 4,756 outcome-selected Gaia EDR3 frame sources. The single production traversal covered all 11,307,390 pairs in the nine frozen angle bins. The retained `arrays.npz` contains full unprojected `H` (9×13×9512), `D` (9×13×13), `d` (9×13), `Cd` (117×117), the 13 g/E2/B2 STF response coordinates, source tangent data and covariance, all covariance modes, and the declared working projection. `compute.py` reconstructs the pair kernel from source directions and tangent bases. `d` is an observed response; its working expectation is `D beta`, so it is not a coefficient estimate.

The numerical controls give `max|d-Hy|=2.4e-14`, `max|D-HX|=2.55e-15`, and `max|HB|=7.81e-17`. The common-spin contribution cancels exactly in the analytic pair kernel. The full uncompressed tangent pair kernel has only common rotation in its null space when the source direction matrix has rank 3; the binned operator has at most 117 rows and leaves many tangent directions unmeasured. The working shared-source covariance is `Cd=Σ_i H_i C_i H_i^T`, including cross-bin and cross-component terms. Actual cross-source covariance is unknown.

The fixed, outcome-independent covariance projection retains 88 modes by a declared relative 1e-10 numerical threshold; diagnostic counts are {'1e-08': 71, '1e-10': 88, '1e-12': 107}. All discarded modes remain saved and are positive or ambiguous, never certified exact null. One covariance-only whitening correction reduced the computed spectral residual from 1.15e-07 to 1.69e-13; this is numerical control, not a certified bound. The E1 and full13 representative projected residuals are 87.0026 and 75.0786; their descriptive difference is 11.924. Projector symmetry, idempotence, nesting, singular spectra, fitted profiles and residuals are recorded in `RESULT.json`.
