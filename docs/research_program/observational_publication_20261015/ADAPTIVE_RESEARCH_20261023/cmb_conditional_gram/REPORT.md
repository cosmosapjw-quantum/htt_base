# R25: conditional Gram reference laws for inherited CMB tensor carriers

**Scope and status.** This is a C0 processed-carrier, diagnostic-only result owned by `obsstat`. It uses the 60 ordered `l=2,3` carriers inherited from R24: one observed PR3 sky, five products, three `lmax` settings, and processing windows. The views are dependent. No map, new sky, native low-ell solver, physical depth, or transfer function was generated. The exact R24 result and R25 theory input hashes are checked by `compute.py` before execution.

## Law and mathematical interpretation

Each view is the complete corrected canonical real harmonic vector `(a_l0, sqrt(2) Re a_lm, -sqrt(2) Im a_lm)` in K. R24 already stores this convention in `harmonic12_K_CMB`; the loader consumes those 12 coordinates directly, and inverse decoding is used only for the raw harmonic-to-STF projection. All 60 stored harmonic rows match exactly, and their retained STF components and norms agree within 5.30×10⁻²³ K. The 5×60 and 7×60 matrices are transformed by a **single action per multipole shared over all 60 views**. `H_orient` draws independent Haar `SO(3)` physical rotations for `l=2,3` and acts through their irreducible tensor representations. `H_Gram` draws independent Haar `O(5)` and `O(7)` matrices. The latter is the conditional orientation law implied by independent harmonic-coordinate Gaussian rows with a common arbitrary cross-view covariance, including singular covariance. Qualification of that invariance for masked Planck processing is absent.

For either law, the within-multipole Gram matrices are exactly invariant. Schur orthogonality for the irreducible `SO(3)` actions and orthogonal Haar symmetry give zero means and `E[Y_ell,aj Y_ell,bk] = delta_ab G_ell,jk/(2ell+1)`; independence between multipole actions gives zero cross-multipole covariance. Those statements concern these constructed group laws. The independent 2,048-draw moment control uses five predetermined views, 156 bounded mean/second-moment coordinates, a fixed 0.01 union-error budget, and Hoeffding tolerances. It is a numerical falsifier of the implementation, separate from the inference streams.

The complete 60×12 observed carrier and both 60×60 Gram matrices are in `RESULT.json`. The seeded PCG64 child stream order, source identity, fixed shared actions, and algorithm suffice to regenerate full tensor draws. Four registered shape/orientation projections per view are `(6 tr(A³)², 1.5 C:C, 1.5 (A:C)², v·v)`, where `A=T2/||T2||F`, `B=T3/||T3||F`, `C_ij=B_ikl B_jkl−delta_ij/3`, and `v_i=B_ijk A_jk`. They do not replace the full carrier. Zero tensor norm is explicitly undefined.

The pilot has 4,096 draws per law; evaluation has 16,383 independent draws per law. Pilot weak upper tails include ties with `(1+count)/4097`; the frozen family score is the **single maximum over every view and active coordinate** for each joint draw. `H_Gram` registers all 240 coordinates; the first two shape coordinates of `H_orient` are conditioned away with reference tail one, leaving 120 relative coordinates. They are excluded before ranking, so floating-point variation of invariant contractions cannot affect the rank. Evaluation uses `(1+count(T_eval>=T_obs))/16384`, retaining ties and avoiding zero. The 99% Clopper–Pearson bounds quantify finite Monte Carlo uncertainty for each fixed-pilot conditional reference tail. Strict `Pi` threshold exceedance at seven predetermined thresholds is reported separately with finite Monte Carlo bounds. These are named conditional reference probabilities, with no Planck-calibrated or confirmatory observational p-value.

The `H_orient` intrinsic-shape invariance error is at most 2.45×10⁻¹⁵. Both analytic moment checks pass their fixed bounds. The targeted suite has seven passing tests, including all-60 source/STF parity, a common-versus-relative multipole rotation counterexample, seeded variation of relative statistics with fixed shapes, a cross-view global-rank counterexample, and complete ties.
