# Conditional depth-bias frontier

This exploratory calculation uses the reviewed selected reported-redshift E1/B1 full 24-vector and its complete covariance. It asks how much deterministic coefficient bias, together with a declared isotropic covariance envelope, can reduce a 12-component contrast among three six-vector levels. It does not measure actual Gaia systematic bias or establish physical confidence. The original K03 physical acceptance remains **HOLD**.

For each `tau` and `delta` in `{0, 0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, 10}` microarcsec/year, the calculation minimizes `(d-Ab)^T [A(C+tau²I)A^T]⁻¹(d-Ab)` with `d=A mu` and `||b||₂<=delta`. `full24` permits bias in all 24 coefficients; `slope6` permits it only in the last six slope coefficients. A compact positive-rank SVD retains the orthogonal residual, including zero-rank cases. The complete 24×24 covariance, its cross blocks, and the 18-coordinate `G_F` linear image are retained.

At zero covariance inflation, the full24 bias radius reaching the Gaussian reference threshold `chi2_12(.95)=21.0260698175` is **7.563904** microarcsec/year. The radius for exact zero contrast is **10.178814** microarcsec/year, independent of `tau`; it exceeds the sampled grid maximum of 10. The slope6 bias cannot reach the Gaussian threshold for `tau=0, 0.1, 0.3, 1`, even at unrestricted slope radius: its irreducible squared residual is respectively **232.249, 222.781, 168.070, 44.482**. At `tau=3` and `10`, zero bias already lies below that reference threshold. The moment reference threshold is 240; at `tau=0` its full24 crossing is **1.970579**, while its slope6 crossing is **19.190713** microarcsec/year, outside the displayed grid. `RESULT.json` gives both continuous threshold frontiers and the 36 grid witnesses for each scope.

The Gaussian and moment lines are conditional reference probes of the selected covariance model. Crossing them is neither a calibrated observed p-value nor a physical exclusion. The original empirical 4096-row strict-exceedance `Pi` is retained from the reviewed source; no new null draws were made. `Q` retains its computational reference only; physical `Q` and physical `Pi` remain **UNKNOWN**. `F` is a full-vector support family with a named, uncalibrated radius, and `G_F` uses the full 18×18 transformed covariance. The support examples use `tau=0.3`, `delta=1`, `rho=1` and include attaining bias and covariance witnesses. These are linear support examples, not a measured occupancy.
