## P3 first-pass referee comparator: two 2026 physical interpretations (verified 2026-10-15)

**Stiskalek, Desmond & Lavaux**, *No evidence for local H0 anisotropy from Tully–Fisher or supernova distances*, MNRAS 546(2), staf2048 (published 2026-01-22), https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/staf2048. **Do not incorrectly report their measured zero-point dipole as insignificant.** Their CosmicFlows-4 conditional zero-point dipole is 0.087±0.019 mag (equivalent to an H parameter dipole 4.1±0.9% under the stated conversion), and an intermediate model comparison actually favors the zero-point dipole at odds 877:1. Their stronger radially varying velocity-dipole analysis interprets this as local flow/systematics instead of a physical expansion-rate anisotropy, with a general flow curve consistent with the standard model. This is a key stress case: a significant *data-template* preference is not a cosmological anisotropic expansion detection.

**Duangchan, Valade, Libeskind & Steinmetz**, *Prior-free cosmological parameter estimation of Cosmicflows-4*, A&A 708 A119 (published online 2026-03-31), https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202556645. Here "prior-free" refers to no assumed cosmological PRIOR on reconstructed local velocity-field power, **not** an assumption-free cosmology or likelihood. Their forward model still assumes spatial flatness/flat-LCDM distance relation and a specific radial-flow large-distance behavior. Their 64 survey-like mock catalogues calibrate selection bias. Published H0 inference is 75.9±1(stat) km/s/Mpc, with systematic uncertainties noted separately, and they identify sky selection as enhancing apparent large flows.

**Direct novelty test for P3:** reproducing published dipole/radial-flow statistic or H0 in a different parameterization is insufficient. The submitted paper needs a clearly specified redshift-dependent H_eff(z,n) *observable estimand* and cross-window/method covariance and selection protocol not already addressed by these works, with held-out mocks/observations that distinguish radial local flows from direction-dependent expansion. Negative/null findings and omitted systematic budgets are equally scientifically relevant. Both 2026 papers' original source and dates have been independently looked up; per-formula reading and actual exact model replication remain X02/H05 future tasks.
