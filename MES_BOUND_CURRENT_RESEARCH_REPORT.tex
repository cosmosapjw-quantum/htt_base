\begin{abstract}
This report gives a self-contained statement of the current
Maartens--Ellis--Stoeger (MES) bound framework and all presently admitted
Planck low-multipole results obtained after the statistical-method upgrade.
The theory is a one-way, premise-conditioned ceiling on squared
Hubble-normalized shear and vorticity invariants.  On the registered geodesic,
residual-dipole-free branch, the two ceiling coordinates are deterministic
functions of the measured quadrupole and octupole powers.  Their nonnegative
transformation is invertible and creates neither a sky direction nor independent
information.  In the exact observation-plus-300 paired CMB-plus-noise pool,
$\Sigma^2_{\max}=2.40\times10^{-10}$ and
$W^2_{\max}=3.01\times10^{-13}$ have local ranks $74/301$ each.  The frozen
ten-coordinate scalar MES-plus-morphology family has rank $98/301$ under the
legacy leave-one-out absolute-median reducer and $148/301$ under the
all-two-sided leave-one-out ECDF-midrank reducer.  These are conditional
finite-pool diagnostics, not physical measurements or unconditional
$p$-values.

The external referee reports exposed a consequential representation defect in
the later observer-space lane.  The stored harmonic carrier uses the
orthonormal real convention
$c_{\ell m,c}=\sqrt2\,\Re a_{\ell m}$ and
$c_{\ell m,s}=-\sqrt2\,\Im a_{\ell m}$ for $m>0$, whereas the STF projection
and carrier-injection metric interpreted the same entries as unscaled real and
imaginary parts.  This is not a harmless overall normalization: it treats
$m=0$ and $m>0$ differently and therefore changes rotations and tensor shape.
For example, the same-pass powers require
$q_2=(75/8\pi)C_2=628.05\,\microK^2$ and
$o_2=(245/8\pi)C_3=4698.85\,\microK^2$, not the previously reported
$1187.75$ and $9340.68\,\microK^2$.  Consequently the previously quoted
observer-space $27/301$ result, its 999-row CMB-only comparison, component and
foreground replacements, and the carrier-domain power curves are withdrawn
from the admitted scientific result set pending correction and complete
rerun.  Their exact execution and byte/content provenance remain historical
evidence; those properties do not restore semantic validity.

The review also correctly establishes that an eight-coordinate catalogue
cannot separate the generic nine-dimensional orbit space of a joint
$\ell=2,3$ pair.  It further identifies an overbroad absence rule for a defined
zero determinant on noncyclic Krylov strata.  These findings are recorded as
method defects, not repaired by prose.  No current result identifies a
physical shear or vorticity, a local/global origin, a foreground source, or a
Bianchi family.

\begin{equation}

\Re a_{\ell m}=c_{\ell m,c}/\sqrt2,

\section{Cosmological and MES-bound framework}

\subsection{Kinematics and normalized target invariants}

Let $u^a$ be the registered fundamental-observer four-velocity,
$u^au_a=-1$, and
\[
h_{ab}=g_{ab}+u_au_b
\]
the spatial projector.  With metric signature $(-,+,+,+)$ and
$\epsilon_{123}=+1$, the covariant velocity gradient is decomposed as
\[
\nabla_bu_a=-u_bA_a+\frac{1}{3}\Theta h_{ab}
 +\sigma_{ab}+\omega_{ab},
\]
where $A_a=u^b\nabla_bu_a$ is the acceleration, $\Theta=\nabla_au^a$ is the
expansion, $\sigma_{ab}=\sigma_{\langle ab\rangle}$ is the spatial shear, and
$\omega_{ab}=\omega_{[ab]}$ is the spatial vorticity.  Define $H=\Theta/3$.
The current MES coordinates target the squared dimensionless invariants
\begin{equation}
\Sigma^2\equiv\frac{\sigma_{ab}\sigma^{ab}}{6H^2},
\qquad
W^2\equiv\frac{\omega_{ab}\omega^{ab}}{6H^2}.
\label{eq:physical-invariants}
\end{equation}
These are nonnegative scalar magnitudes.  They are not vectors, principal
axes, eigenframes, or signed tensors.

The MES programme uses the near-isotropy of the cosmic microwave background
to place premise-dependent constraints on departures from an almost-FRW
state~\cite{MaartensEllisStoeger1995,StoegerAraujoGebbie1997}.  The present
version uses the geodesic branch $A_a=0$, the linear almost-EGS regime, and a
registered fundamental-observer frame.  Coefficients involving time or spatial
derivatives of radiation multipoles inherit the corresponding derivative-
hierarchy premises; they are not universal identities of arbitrary spacetimes.

\subsection{Temperature multipole amplitudes}

For a temperature anisotropy field with angular power $C_\ell$, define
\begin{equation}
\epsilon_\ell
=\frac{1}{T_0}

\label{eq:epsilon}

The reported numerical analysis fixes

\[

\]
This is an explicit premise, not a conclusion extracted from the quadrupole or

\subsection{Why a scalar ceiling cannot create direction or STF shape}

Suppose $s$ denotes any collection of rotation-invariant scalars and
$F(s)\in\mathbb R^3$ is $O(3)$-equivariant.  Since $Rs=s$ for every rotation
$R$,
\[
F(s)=F(Rs)=R F(s)\qquad\text{for all }R\in O(3).
\]
The only vector fixed by every rotation is zero.  The same argument in the
irreducible rank-two STF representation shows that an equivariant map from
scalars alone to an STF tensor must also vanish.  Therefore
\[
\text{scalar MES ceiling}\not\Rightarrow
\text{direction, nonzero vector, or nonzero STF tensor}.
\]

Direction-indexed data do permit legal moment maps.  For a full-sky scalar
field $q(\widehat n,z)$,
\begin{align}
V_a(z)&=\frac{3}{4\pi}\int q(\widehat n,z)n_a\,d\Omega,
\label{eq:vector-moment}\\
T_{ab}(z)&=\frac{15}{8\pi}\int q(\widehat n,z)
n_{\langle a}n_{b\rangle}\,d\Omega.
\label{eq:stf-moment}
\end{align}
If $q=v_an^a+S_{ab}n^an^b$ plus harmonics orthogonal to $\ell=1,2$, with
$S_{ab}$ STF, these normalizations recover $V_a=v_a$ and $T_{ab}=S_{ab}$.
For a masked or discretized sky, the corresponding estimator and quadrature
must be bound explicitly.  A single global scalar does not meet this premise.

\subsection{Response-bound states and local/global identifiability}

The current framework distinguishes three semantic objects:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=3.2cm,style=nextline]
  \item[Observable irrep state] Measured or simulated harmonic/STF content

  \item[MES normalizer] A positive, same-channel, one-way ceiling that may

  \item[Response-bound state] An observable state tied to an independently
  specified forward response with compatible channel, frame, basis, units,
  epoch, and normalization.  Its response rank determines point or set

\end{description}

A rank-deficient response yields an identified set, not a point estimate.  A
simple local/global directional model illustrates the requirement:
\begin{equation}
V_i=f_L(z_i)\,\boldsymbol\beta_L
   +f_G(z_i)\,\boldsymbol\beta_G+\varepsilon_i.
\label{eq:local-global}
\end{equation}

depth Gram matrix for $f_L,f_G$ after nuisance projection.  The six-parameter
Fisher matrix is $K\otimes S$, so
\begin{equation}

\label{eq:fisher-factor}
\end{equation}
One shell makes $K$ rank one; proportional depth profiles do the same.  Planck
temperature morphology alone therefore cannot distinguish local boost from

\subsection{Reducer A: leave-one-out absolute median}

Let $X_{ij}$ be coordinate $j$ for row $i$.  For a two-sided coordinate,
\begin{equation}
d_{ij}=\left|X_{ij}-\operatorname{median}(X_{-i,j})\right|.
\label{eq:absmedian}
\end{equation}
For an upper tail use $d_{ij}=X_{ij}$ and for a lower tail use
$d_{ij}=-X_{ij}$.  The observation-inclusive local rank is
\begin{equation}
p_{ij}=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}{\bf1}(d_{kj}\ge d_{ij}).
\label{eq:local-rank}
\end{equation}
Because every row has its own leave-one-out centre, the complete map from all
rows to all scores, not only the observation-row formula, is the statistical
object.  This reducer is invariant under positive affine rescaling but not
under arbitrary monotone reparameterization.

\subsection{Reducer B: leave-one-out ECDF midrank}

For every row and coordinate define
\begin{equation}
u_{ij}=\frac{
\#\{k\ne i:X_{kj}<X_{ij}\}
+\tfrac12\#\{k\ne i:X_{kj}=X_{ij}\}}
{N-1}.
\label{eq:ecdf-midrank}
\end{equation}
The oriented score is $2|u_{ij}-1/2|$ for a two-sided coordinate, $u_{ij}$
for an upper tail, and $1-u_{ij}$ for a lower tail.  Exact integer midrank
numerators preserve mathematical ties.  This reducer is invariant under
strictly increasing coordinate transformations that preserve tie structure.

\subsection{Family scan, ties, and the finite-rank premise}

For a predeclared family $\mathcal F$, every row receives
\begin{equation}
r_i=\min_{j\in\mathcal F}p_{ij},
\qquad S_i=-\log r_i.
\end{equation}
The reported family rank is
\begin{equation}
p_{\mathcal F}
=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}{\bf1}(r_k\le r_0)
=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}{\bf1}(S_k\ge S_0).
\label{eq:family-rank}
\end{equation}
Ties and the observation itself are included, so a zero rank is impossible;
the resolution floors are $1/301$ and $1/1000$~\cite{PhipsonSmyth2010}.

If $(Z_0,\ldots,Z_{N-1})$ are jointly exchangeable under the stated null and
the complete row-scoring map is permutation-equivariant, then the
observation-inclusive family rank is super-uniform, with ties treated
conservatively.  Row-swap and row-permutation checks establish the second
condition.  The first condition is a scientific null-model premise.  If FFP10
does not faithfully represent the observation under the intended null, the
fractions remain exact ranks relative to the frozen pool but do not acquire
unconditional Type-I-error calibration.

The proof is short.  Exchangeability of the input rows and equivariance of the
complete map imply exchangeability of $(S_0,\ldots,S_{N-1})$.  Conditional on
their unordered multiset, the observation label is uniformly distributed over
the $N$ positions.  Counting all scores at least as large as $S_0$, including
ties, can only increase the ordinary randomized rank; hence the reported rank
is super-uniform.

Feature families, coordinate transformations, tails, reducers, missing-row
rules, and the $\epsilon_1$ grid were fixed before observation-row scoring.
No covariance matrix, Gaussian likelihood, posterior, Bayes factor, or
observation-selected feature subset enters Eq.~\eqref{eq:family-rank}.

\subsection{Fixed-bank foreground falsification}

To test a simple projection explanation without fitting after seeing SMICA,
the foreground template bank was fixed in advance.  For every SMICA or paired
null row $i$ and template $t$, define
\[
 A_{it}^{Q}=\frac{\langle Q_i,Q_t\rangle_F^2}
 {\lVert Q_i\rVert_F^2\lVert Q_t\rVert_F^2},\qquad
 A_{it}^{O}=\frac{\langle O_i,O_t\rangle_F^2}
 {\lVert O_i\rVert_F^2\lVert O_t\rVert_F^2},
\]
and the simultaneous single-template score
\[
 S_i^{\rm fg}=\max_{t\in\mathcal T}\min(A_{it}^{Q},A_{it}^{O}).
\]
The upper-tail rank counts every row whose own maximum over the same fixed bank
is at least the observed maximum.  Thus the look-elsewhere choice of template
is included inside the row-symmetric score.

The admitted bank contains Planck PR4 30, 44, 353, 545, and 857 GHz maps plus
synchrotron, dust, AME, and free--free component maps from each of CosmoGlobe
and BeyondPlanck: 13 templates in total.  The best SMICA template is the
CosmoGlobe free--free map, but its simultaneous score is only 0.072797.  The
fixed-bank rank is
\[
   \rankfrac{144}{301}.
\]
The predeclared exceptional-alignment gate was numerator $\leq15$.  Therefore
the necessary morphology expected from a simple single-template projection is
not exceptional in this bank.  This result actively disfavors that narrow
explanation; it does not exclude mixtures, nonlinear leakage, transfer
mismatch, an omitted foreground morphology, or other systematics, and it does
not validate a physical source.

\section{Registered carrier-domain method-power experiment}
\label{sec:power}

\subsection{Question and fixed intervention}

The preceding sections ask how the observed sky ranks in frozen finite pools.
The present experiment asks a different question: if a predeclared algebraic
pattern is added to otherwise held-out simulation carriers, how often does a
specified 200-reference scoring functional reject at level $10/201$?  The
observation is never used.  This is a method-power experiment, not a new
observation test and not an estimate of physical-source sensitivity.

Write each real harmonic block in the registered layout
\[
  \boldsymbol a_\ell=(a_{\ell0},\Re a_{\ell1},\Im a_{\ell1},\ldots,
  \Re a_{\ell\ell},\Im a_{\ell\ell})
\]
with metric $G_\ell=\operatorname{diag}(1,2,2,\ldots,2)$.  The full carrier
has 32 components for $\ell=2,\ldots,5$.  Five integer-seeded templates are
registered: \texttt{Q\_AXIAL}, \texttt{O\_PLANAR}, \texttt{MIX\_AXIAL},
\texttt{MIX\_PLANAR}, and \texttt{MIX\_GENERIC}.  Each is normalized so that
\[
  \boldsymbol t_2^{\mathsf T}G_2\boldsymbol t_2+
  \boldsymbol t_3^{\mathsf T}G_3\boldsymbol t_3=1,
  \qquad \boldsymbol t_4=\boldsymbol t_5=0,
\]
with equal energy in the two active blocks when both are nonzero.  They are
analytic carrier perturbations.  They are expressly not shear, vorticity, or
Bianchi solutions.

Thirty-two deterministic proper rotations are obtained from hash-fixed open
unit-cube points and unit quaternions.  If $D_\ell(R)$ is the metric-preserving
real-harmonic representation, the intervention is
\begin{equation}
 \boldsymbol a'_{2,3}=\boldsymbol a_{2,3}
   +A\,s\sqrt{4\pi}\,D(R)\boldsymbol t_{2,3},
 \qquad
 \boldsymbol a'_{4,5}=\boldsymbol a_{4,5},
 \label{eq:carrier-injection}
\end{equation}
at $A\in\{0,0.25,0.5,1,2,4,8\}$.  The shared scale is fixed from CMB-only
reference rows 00000--00199:
\[
 s=\operatorname{median}_i
 \left[\frac{\boldsymbol a_{2,i}^{\mathsf T}G_2\boldsymbol a_{2,i}+
 \boldsymbol a_{3,i}^{\mathsf T}G_3\boldsymbol a_{3,i}}{4\pi}\right]^{1/2}
 =34.0422165082\,\microK.
\]
Zero amplitude is computed once per arm; repeating it over templates or
orientations is forbidden because that would manufacture trials.

\subsection{Reference, evaluation arms, and exact finite scores}

The reference set is the same 200 CMB-only carriers in both arms.  The paired
arm evaluates IDs 00200--00299, giving 100 independent simulation skies.  The
CMB-only arm evaluates IDs 00200--00999 except 00970, giving 799 skies.  Thus
calibration and evaluation IDs are disjoint, but the two evaluation arms share
100 underlying CMB simulation identities and cannot be treated as independent
replications.  The paired carriers additionally contain noise.  No observed
row, noise map, or raw map is opened by this experiment.

The matched-null exchangeability premise behind the CMB-only finite-rank
interpretation does \emph{not} hold for the paired arm: paired queries are
CMB-plus-noise carriers whereas all 200 references are CMB-only.  Accordingly,
the paired-arm $10/201$ rejection fractions describe performance of the frozen
decision rule under this registered distribution shift.  They are not exact
matched-null $p$ calibrations.  The CMB-only arm is the matched conditional
finite-pool evaluation.

For each injected query and each coordinate, the query is joined to the 200
reference rows.  Both frozen reducers act on this 201-row pool.  If $k_j$ is
the resulting integer local-rank numerator for coordinate $j$, two family
combiners are applied:
\[
  M=\min_j k_j,
  \qquad
  P=\prod_j k_j.
\]
The family numerator is the number of complete-pool rows having its own $M$ or
$P$ no larger than the query value.  The product is compared as an exact
integer; no $\chi^2$ approximation or coordinate-independence assumption is
introduced.  A trial rejects precisely when this family numerator is at most
10.  This fixes the nominal threshold $10/201<0.05$ before the injected
results are read.

Power first averages the 32 paired orientations within each simulation sky,
then averages over skies.  The reported standard error is the standard error
of these sky-level orientation averages, conditional on the fixed 200-row
reference set.  Orientations are not pseudoreplicated as independent skies.
The five templates are separate alternatives, not random draws from a
population; any template average in a figure is only a visual compression.

The complete execution contains 96,000 positive-amplitude injected carriers
in the paired arm and 767,040 in the CMB-only arm.  Both arms completed with
zero typed absences.  All local and family ranks remain exact integers.  The
unaltered $\ell=4,5$ blocks and proper-rotation metric were checked directly.
