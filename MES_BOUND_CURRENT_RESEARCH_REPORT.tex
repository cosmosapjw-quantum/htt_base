\begin{abstract}
This report gives a self-contained statement of the current
Maartens--Ellis--Stoeger (MES) bound framework and all presently admitted
Planck low-multipole results obtained after the statistical-method upgrade.
The theory is formulated as a one-way, premise-conditioned ceiling on the
squared Hubble-normalized shear and vorticity invariants.  On the registered
geodesic, residual-dipole-free branch, the two ceilings are deterministic
functions of the measured quadrupole and octupole powers; the transformation
is invertible on its nonnegative domain and therefore creates neither a sky
direction nor independent information.  Direction and shape enter only through
the actual direction-indexed harmonic field, represented here by real
$\ell=2,3$ symmetric trace-free (STF) tensors.

The statistical analysis treats one Planck PR3 SMICA observation and finite
FFP10 null ensembles symmetrically.  In the exact paired 300-row CMB-plus-noise
pool, the squared MES ceilings are
$\Sigma^2_{\max}=2.4000870\times10^{-10}$ and
$W^2_{\max}=3.0061888\times10^{-13}$, with local ranks
$74/301$ each.  The ten-coordinate MES-plus-morphology family has rank
$98/301$ under the legacy leave-one-out absolute-median reducer; an
all-two-sided factorial audit gives $148/301$ under the leave-one-out
ECDF-midrank reducer, while a separately predeclared physics-oriented tail
registry gives $93/301$.  In the observer-space STF analysis, the
eight-coordinate frame-free family has rank $27/301$ under both reducers in
the paired CMB-plus-noise pool.  The same frozen observation and feature family
give $61/1000$ and $55/1000$ in a separate 999-row CMB-only pool.  This change
is a descriptive null-ensemble sensitivity, not an independent confirmation.
An exact observation-row replacement audit, with the paired 300-row null pool
and every statistic definition held fixed, gives frame-free ranks
$27/301$, $46/301$, $27/301$, and $81/301$ for SMICA, Commander, NILC, and
SEVEM under the absolute-median reducer; the ECDF ranks are $27/301$,
$41/301$, $27/301$, and $40/301$.  WMAP9 ILC, CosmoGlobe, and BeyondPlanck
CMB holdouts were processed by the same operator.  A predeclared 13-template
foreground bank gives a simultaneous $Q/O$ projection rank of $144/301$:
SMICA is not exceptionally aligned with a single admitted template under this
necessary-condition diagnostic.

A separate, observation-free intervention study now measures the method power
of the frozen MES-10 and frame-free-irrep families.  It injects five unit-energy
algebraic $\ell=2,3$ templates, at seven registered amplitudes and 32 fixed
orientations, into held-out harmonic carriers.  Exactly 200 CMB-only rows form
the common reference set; 100 paired CMB-plus-noise rows and 799 CMB-only rows
form two overlapping, non-independent evaluation arms.  The positive-amplitude
study contains 96,000 and 767,040 injected carriers, respectively, with no
typed absences and no observation or raw-map access.  The two arms give nearly
the same cellwise power (median absolute difference $0.00271$), but power is
strongly template-, family-, reducer-, and combiner-dependent.  In particular,
the ECDF/min-local-$p$ MES-10 functional is completely conservative on this
201-row design, whereas the exact integer-product combiner retains substantial
power.  These are powers of the registered 200-reference functionals, not
power calibrations of the original 301- or 1000-row observation procedures and
not sensitivities to physical shear, vorticity, or Bianchi templates.

All fractions are conditional finite-pool ranks.  They are not unconditional
$p$-values, likelihoods, posterior probabilities, measurements of physical
shear or vorticity, local/global attributions, foreground validations, or
Bianchi-family identifications.
\end{abstract}

\section{Cosmological and MES-bound framework}

\subsection{Kinematics and normalized target invariants}

Let $u^a$ be the registered fundamental-observer four-velocity,
$u^au_a=-1$, and
\[
h_{ab}=g_{ab}+u_au_b
\]
the spatial projector.  With metric signature $(-,+,+,+)$ and
$\epsilon_{123}=+1$, the covariant velocity gradient is decomposed as
\[
\nabla_bu_a=-u_bA_a+\frac{1}{3}\Theta h_{ab}
 +\sigma_{ab}+\omega_{ab},
\]
where $A_a=u^b\nabla_bu_a$ is the acceleration, $\Theta=\nabla_au^a$ is the
expansion, $\sigma_{ab}=\sigma_{\langle ab\rangle}$ is the spatial shear, and
$\omega_{ab}=\omega_{[ab]}$ is the spatial vorticity.  Define $H=\Theta/3$.
The current MES coordinates target the squared dimensionless invariants
\begin{equation}
\Sigma^2\equiv\frac{\sigma_{ab}\sigma^{ab}}{6H^2},
\qquad
W^2\equiv\frac{\omega_{ab}\omega^{ab}}{6H^2}.
\label{eq:physical-invariants}
\end{equation}
These are nonnegative scalar magnitudes.  They are not vectors, principal
axes, eigenframes, or signed tensors.

The MES programme uses the near-isotropy of the cosmic microwave background
to place premise-dependent constraints on departures from an almost-FRW
state~\cite{MaartensEllisStoeger1995,StoegerAraujoGebbie1997}.  The present
version uses the geodesic branch $A_a=0$, the linear almost-EGS regime, and a
registered fundamental-observer frame.  Coefficients involving time or spatial
derivatives of radiation multipoles inherit the corresponding derivative-
hierarchy premises; they are not universal identities of arbitrary spacetimes.

\subsection{Temperature multipole amplitudes}

For a temperature anisotropy field with angular power $C_\ell$, define
\begin{equation}
\epsilon_\ell
=\frac{1}{T_0}
\sqrt{\frac{(2\ell+1)C_\ell}{4\pi}},
\label{eq:epsilon}
\end{equation}
where $T_0$ is expressed in the same temperature unit as $\sqrt{C_\ell}$.
The reported numerical analysis fixes
$T_0=2.72548\,\mathrm{K}=2.72548\times10^6\,\microK$.  Thus every
$\epsilon_\ell$ is dimensionless and nonnegative.  The observed
dipole is dominated by observer motion.  In the principal branch used below,
that kinematic contribution is assigned to the observer-motion channel and the
residual cosmological dipole is set to
\[
\epsilon_1=0.
\]
This is an explicit premise, not a conclusion extracted from the quadrupole or
octupole.

\subsection{Why a scalar ceiling cannot create direction or STF shape}

Suppose $s$ denotes any collection of rotation-invariant scalars and
$F(s)\in\mathbb R^3$ is $O(3)$-equivariant.  Since $Rs=s$ for every rotation
$R$,
\[
F(s)=F(Rs)=R F(s)\qquad\text{for all }R\in O(3).
\]
The only vector fixed by every rotation is zero.  The same argument in the
irreducible rank-two STF representation shows that an equivariant map from
scalars alone to an STF tensor must also vanish.  Therefore
\[
\text{scalar MES ceiling}\not\Rightarrow
\text{direction, nonzero vector, or nonzero STF tensor}.
\]

Direction-indexed data do permit legal moment maps.  For a full-sky scalar
field $q(\widehat n,z)$,
\begin{align}
V_a(z)&=\frac{3}{4\pi}\int q(\widehat n,z)n_a\,d\Omega,
\label{eq:vector-moment}\\
T_{ab}(z)&=\frac{15}{8\pi}\int q(\widehat n,z)
n_{\langle a}n_{b\rangle}\,d\Omega.
\label{eq:stf-moment}
\end{align}
If $q=v_an^a+S_{ab}n^an^b$ plus harmonics orthogonal to $\ell=1,2$, with
$S_{ab}$ STF, these normalizations recover $V_a=v_a$ and $T_{ab}=S_{ab}$.
For a masked or discretized sky, the corresponding estimator and quadrature
must be bound explicitly.  A single global scalar does not meet this premise.

\subsection{Response-bound states and local/global identifiability}

\subsection{Reducer A: leave-one-out absolute median}

Let $X_{ij}$ be coordinate $j$ for row $i$.  For a two-sided coordinate,
\begin{equation}
d_{ij}=\left|X_{ij}-\operatorname{median}(X_{-i,j})\right|.
\label{eq:absmedian}
\end{equation}
For an upper tail use $d_{ij}=X_{ij}$ and for a lower tail use
$d_{ij}=-X_{ij}$.  The observation-inclusive local rank is
\begin{equation}
p_{ij}=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}{\bf1}(d_{kj}\ge d_{ij}).
\label{eq:local-rank}
\end{equation}
Because every row has its own leave-one-out centre, the complete map from all
rows to all scores, not only the observation-row formula, is the statistical
object.  This reducer is invariant under positive affine rescaling but not
under arbitrary monotone reparameterization.

\subsection{Reducer B: leave-one-out ECDF midrank}

For every row and coordinate define
\begin{equation}
u_{ij}=\frac{
\#\{k\ne i:X_{kj}<X_{ij}\}
+\tfrac12\#\{k\ne i:X_{kj}=X_{ij}\}}
{N-1}.
\label{eq:ecdf-midrank}
\end{equation}
The oriented score is $2|u_{ij}-1/2|$ for a two-sided coordinate, $u_{ij}$
for an upper tail, and $1-u_{ij}$ for a lower tail.  Exact integer midrank
numerators preserve mathematical ties.  This reducer is invariant under
strictly increasing coordinate transformations that preserve tie structure.

\subsection{Family scan, ties, and the finite-rank premise}

For a predeclared family $\mathcal F$, every row receives
\begin{equation}
r_i=\min_{j\in\mathcal F}p_{ij},
\qquad S_i=-\log r_i.
\end{equation}
The reported family rank is
\begin{equation}
p_{\mathcal F}
=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}{\bf1}(r_k\le r_0)
=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}{\bf1}(S_k\ge S_0).
\label{eq:family-rank}
\end{equation}
Ties and the observation itself are included, so a zero rank is impossible;
the resolution floors are $1/301$ and $1/1000$~\cite{PhipsonSmyth2010}.

If $(Z_0,\ldots,Z_{N-1})$ are jointly exchangeable under the stated null and
the complete row-scoring map is permutation-equivariant, then the
observation-inclusive family rank is super-uniform, with ties treated
conservatively.  Row-swap and row-permutation checks establish the second
condition.  The first condition is a scientific null-model premise.  If FFP10
does not faithfully represent the observation under the intended null, the
fractions remain exact ranks relative to the frozen pool but do not acquire
unconditional Type-I-error calibration.

The proof is short.  Exchangeability of the input rows and equivariance of the
complete map imply exchangeability of $(S_0,\ldots,S_{N-1})$.  Conditional on
their unordered multiset, the observation label is uniformly distributed over
the $N$ positions.  Counting all scores at least as large as $S_0$, including
ties, can only increase the ordinary randomized rank; hence the reported rank
is super-uniform.

Feature families, coordinate transformations, tails, reducers, missing-row
rules, and the $\epsilon_1$ grid were fixed before observation-row scoring.
No covariance matrix, Gaussian likelihood, posterior, Bayes factor, or
observation-selected feature subset enters Eq.~\eqref{eq:family-rank}.

\section{Observer-space irreducible tensors}

\subsection{Harmonic convention and reversible STF bridge}

For a real temperature map the retained convention is
\begin{equation}
T_\ell(\widehat n)=a_{\ell0}Y_{\ell0}(\widehat n)
+2\sum_{m=1}^{\ell}\Re\!\left[a_{\ell m}Y_{\ell m}(\widehat n)\right],
\label{eq:real-harmonics}
\end{equation}
with orthonormal Condon--Shortley harmonics.  The real storage block is
\[
(a_{\ell0},\Re a_{\ell1},\Im a_{\ell1},\ldots,
\Re a_{\ell\ell},\Im a_{\ell\ell}).
\]

The $\ell=2$ block is mapped linearly to a real symmetric trace-free tensor
$Q_{ab}$, and the $\ell=3$ block to a real fully symmetric trace-free tensor
$O_{abc}$~\cite{Thorne1980}.  Five and seven independent Cartesian components
respectively match the dimensions $2\ell+1$.  The explicit normalization is
given in Appendix~\ref{app:bridge}.  Every row passes
\[
a_{\ell m}\longrightarrow(Q,O)\longrightarrow a_{\ell m}
\]
with maximum absolute residual
$1.4210854715\times10^{-14}$ against the predeclared
$\mathrm{atol}=\mathrm{rtol}=5\times10^{-13}$.  This validates the numerical
representation bridge, not a physical source model.

\subsection{Radial, shape, mixed, and parity coordinates}

The radial and quadrupole-shape invariants are
\begin{align}
q_2&=Q_{ab}Q^{ab},&
q_3&=Q_a{}^bQ_b{}^cQ_c{}^a,\\
o_2&=O_{abc}O^{abc},&
J_Q&=\sqrt6\,\frac{q_3}{q_2^{3/2}}.
\end{align}
Here $q_2,o_2$ have units $\microK^2$, $q_3$ has units $\microK^3$, and
$J_Q$ is dimensionless.  For a real symmetric trace-free $3\times3$ tensor,
\[
Q^3-\frac{q_2}{2}Q-\frac{q_3}{3}I=0,
\qquad \frac{q_2^3}{2}-3q_3^2\ge0,
\]
so $J_Q\in[-1,1]$ when $q_2>0$.

When $q_2,o_2>0$, define
\[
\widehat Q=Q/\sqrt{q_2},\qquad
\widehat O=O/\sqrt{o_2},\qquad
v_a=\widehat O_{abc}\widehat Q^{bc},
\]
and
\[
S_{ab}=\stf(\widehat O_{acd}\widehat O_b{}^{cd}).
\]
The registered dimensionless mixed coordinates are
\begin{align}
\widehat R_{v0}&=v_av^a,&
\widehat R_{v1}&=v_a\widehat Q^a{}_bv^b,\\
\widehat R_{v2}&=v_a(\widehat Q^2)^a{}_bv^b,&
\widehat R_{QS}&=\widehat Q_{ab}S^{ab},\\
\widehat K_v&=\det[v,\widehat Qv,\widehat Q^2v].
\end{align}
The first four are reflection-even; $\widehat K_v$ is a pseudoscalar that
changes sign under reflection.  It is therefore an $SO(3)$ invariant with
explicit parity covariance, not an ordinary $O(3)$ scalar.

The frame-free family is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal F_{\rm ff}=
\{q_2,o_2,J_Q,\widehat R_{v0},\widehat R_{v1},
\widehat R_{v2},\widehat R_{QS},\widehat K_v\}.
\label{eq:framefree-family}
\end{equation}
For a separate absolute-frame sensitivity check, let
$z_G=(0,0,1)$ be the Galactic pole and define
\[
P_Q^G=|\widehat Qz_G|^2,
\qquad P_O^G=|\widehat O_{abc}z_G^bz_G^c|^2.
\]
The ten-coordinate companion is
$\mathcal F_G=\mathcal F_{\rm ff}\cup\{P_Q^G,P_O^G\}$.
It is descriptive and is not a physical source-direction estimator.

\subsection{Degenerate strata and typed absence}

At $q_2=0$ the quadrupole shape is undefined; at $o_2=0$ the octupole shape
and mixed coordinates are undefined.  Repeated quadrupole eigenvalues and
rank-deficient Krylov matrices can also make a registered orientation or
partial-domain coordinate unavailable.  Such cases are represented as typed
absence rather than by an invented fallback axis or silent zero filling.  A
missing primary-family value would stop the family calibration.  All 301 rows
of the paired pool and all 1000 rows of the CMB-only analysis are complete on
the registered primary family; every quadrupole spectrum used here is simple.
Global completeness of this coordinate catalogue over all tensor orbits has
not been proven.

\[

 {\lVert Q_i\rVert_F^2\lVert Q_t\rVert_F^2},\qquad
 A_{it}^{O}=\frac{\langle O_i,O_t\rangle_F^2}
 {\lVert O_i\rVert_F^2\lVert O_t\rVert_F^2},

\[
 S_i^{\rm fg}=\max_{t\in\mathcal T}\min(A_{it}^{Q},A_{it}^{O}).
\]

\section{Registered carrier-domain method-power experiment}
\label{sec:power}

\subsection{Question and fixed intervention}

The preceding sections ask how the observed sky ranks in frozen finite pools.
The present experiment asks a different question: if a predeclared algebraic
pattern is added to otherwise held-out simulation carriers, how often does a
specified 200-reference scoring functional reject at level $10/201$?  The
observation is never used.  This is a method-power experiment, not a new
observation test and not an estimate of physical-source sensitivity.

Write each real harmonic block in the registered layout
\[
  \boldsymbol a_\ell=(a_{\ell0},\Re a_{\ell1},\Im a_{\ell1},\ldots,
  \Re a_{\ell\ell},\Im a_{\ell\ell})
\]
with metric $G_\ell=\operatorname{diag}(1,2,2,\ldots,2)$.  The full carrier
has 32 components for $\ell=2,\ldots,5$.  Five integer-seeded templates are
registered: \texttt{Q\_AXIAL}, \texttt{O\_PLANAR}, \texttt{MIX\_AXIAL},
\texttt{MIX\_PLANAR}, and \texttt{MIX\_GENERIC}.  Each is normalized so that
\[
  \boldsymbol t_2^{\mathsf T}G_2\boldsymbol t_2+
  \boldsymbol t_3^{\mathsf T}G_3\boldsymbol t_3=1,
  \qquad \boldsymbol t_4=\boldsymbol t_5=0,
\]
with equal energy in the two active blocks when both are nonzero.  They are
analytic carrier perturbations.  They are expressly not shear, vorticity, or
Bianchi solutions.

Thirty-two deterministic proper rotations are obtained from hash-fixed open
unit-cube points and unit quaternions.  If $D_\ell(R)$ is the metric-preserving
real-harmonic representation, the intervention is
\begin{equation}
 \boldsymbol a'_{2,3}=\boldsymbol a_{2,3}
   +A\,s\sqrt{4\pi}\,D(R)\boldsymbol t_{2,3},
 \qquad
 \boldsymbol a'_{4,5}=\boldsymbol a_{4,5},
 \label{eq:carrier-injection}
\end{equation}
at $A\in\{0,0.25,0.5,1,2,4,8\}$.  The shared scale is fixed from CMB-only

reference rows.  Both frozen reducers act on this 201-row pool.  If $k_j$ is
the resulting integer local-rank numerator for coordinate $j$, two family
combiners are applied:
\[

introduced.  A trial rejects precisely when this family numerator is at most
10.  This fixes the nominal threshold $10/201<0.05$ before the injected
results are read.

then averages over skies.  The reported standard error is the standard error

population; any template average in a figure is only a visual compression.

\section{Synthesis and scientific interpretation}

\subsection{What changed with the latest framework}

The current methodology replaces three potential shortcuts with explicit
separations.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Ceiling versus state.}  $\Sigma^2_{\max}$ and $W^2_{\max}$
  are squared, premise-conditioned upper coordinates.  They are neither the
  physical tensors nor estimates of their directions.
  \item \textbf{Scalar versus shape.}  Directional morphology comes from the
  measured harmonic blocks and their STF representatives, never from scalar
  reconstruction.
  \item \textbf{Exact rank versus null fidelity.}  A finite rank can be
  arithmetically exact relative to a frozen pool while its unconditional
  scientific calibration remains limited by exchangeability and simulation
  fidelity.
  \item \textbf{Observation rank versus method power.}  The observed 301- and
  1000-row ranks are not assigned the power of a different 201-row reference
  design.  The intervention experiment evaluates only its explicitly
  registered functionals and never uses the observation.
\end{enumerate}

The scalar audit shows that the MES ceilings themselves are not locally
unusual in the paired pool: both are $74/301$.  The smallest scalar local rank
is the octupole morphology coordinate $d_{3,0}$ at $16/301$.  The observer-
space upgrade then finds a more localized mixed-shape feature:
$\widehat R_{v0}$ and $\widehat R_{v2}$ are $4/301$ in the paired pool and
$9/1000$, $21/1000$ in the CMB-only pool.  After the predeclared family scan,
the corresponding frame-free ranks are $27/301$ and $61/1000$ or $55/1000$.
This distinction between local and family ranks is essential.

The component-replacement audit adds a different sensitivity axis without
altering that statistic.  SMICA and NILC give the same frame-free family rank
under both reducers; Commander shifts moderately; SEVEM shows the largest
reducer dependence.  WMAP9 ILC transports the same morphology closely, while
CosmoGlobe and BeyondPlanck CMB products move both tensor shape and family
rank.  The fixed foreground bank then fails to show exceptional simultaneous
$Q/O$ alignment ($144/301$).  Together these are evidence of partial, not
universal, cross-product stability and evidence against one narrowly specified
single-template foreground projection.

The carrier intervention adds a prospective sensitivity diagnostic.  It
shows strong response to some algebraic alternatives but also exposes finite-
pool saturation and sharp functional specificity.  The paired and CMB-only
arms closely agree cell by cell, yet are not independent because their CMB
simulation identities overlap.  The result therefore supports the conclusion
that the recorded power curves are reproducible consequences of the frozen
carrier/statistic pipeline; it does not transfer that power to a physical
template, the observation test, or an unconditional null model.

\subsection{What the results do not identify}

The quantities $Q_{ab}$ and $O_{abc}$ are observer-space representations of
CMB harmonics.  They are not the spacetime shear tensor, vorticity tensor,
anisotropic spatial curvature, or a Bianchi template.  No admitted forward
response connects the measured orbit coordinates to those physical states.
No depth-indexed directional channel supplies the rank required by
Eq.~\eqref{eq:fisher-factor}.  Therefore no result here identifies a local
boost, global tilt, physical source, or geometry family.

The present analysis also does not produce the separate diagnostic vocabulary
$(x,Q,\Pi,F,G)$, a likelihood, posterior, evidence ratio, posterior predictive
check, or cross-validation score.  Similar symbols in the STF section---for
example $Q_{ab}$ and $J_Q$---must not be confused with that separate reporting
layer.

\section{Limitations and next evidence requirements}

\begin{warningbox}
\textbf{Central limitation.}
The strongest present result is a conditional observer-space morphology
diagnostic.  Increasing exactness of row identity or numerical replay cannot
substitute for a missing physical response or for null-model fidelity.
\end{warningbox}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Null fidelity.}  The paired pool represents CMB plus admitted
  noise; the 999-row lane represents CMB only.  The component-replacement and
  holdout rows retain the paired Planck pool.  None spans every foreground,
  calibration, beam, mask, or cross-instrument uncertainty.
  \item \textbf{Bounded product coverage.}  PR3 Commander/NILC/SEVEM and three
  external CMB holdouts were executed, but no compatible PR3 split was locally
  admitted.  WMAP low-frequency, QUIJOTE, and CLASS temperature-morphology
  lanes remain typed exclusions for the reasons in the preceding section.
  \item \textbf{Foreground scope.}  The $144/301$ bank diagnostic addresses
  one necessary condition for a simple single-template projection.  It does
  not cover arbitrary linear mixtures, spatially varying spectra, nonlinear
  mapmaking leakage, or omitted components.
  \item \textbf{Finite resolution and dependence.}  Ranks lie on finite grids,
  and coordinates are dependent.  Participation ratios are diagnostics, not
  multiplicity-free physical dimensions.
  \item \textbf{Power-design scope.}  The intervention fixes 200 reference
  carriers, five algebraic templates, 32 orientations, and two family
  combiners.  Its empirical power is conditional on those choices.  Template
  means are visual summaries, 19 of 80 positive-grid curves are nonmonotone,
  and the orientation prefix is not a continuum-convergence certificate.
  \item \textbf{Premise dependence.}  The principal MES numbers use the
  geodesic, linear almost-EGS, registered-frame, residual-$\epsilon_1=0$
  branch.  Other branches require their own recovered coefficients and
  assumptions.
  \item \textbf{No physical response.}  A future claim about shear,
  vorticity, local/global origin, or geometry requires a content-defined
  forward model, compatible conventions, rank analysis, and independent
  observational channels.
  \item \textbf{No family identification.}  A scalar rank, a sky axis, and an
  observer-space STF orbit are each insufficient.  Family identification
  additionally requires a validated physical morphology atlas and null,
  mask, covariance, and equivalence-class gates.
\end{enumerate}

The shortest scientifically meaningful next steps are therefore compatible
split/null-fidelity sensitivity, explicit cross-instrument null construction,
and, separately, a genuine physical response with sufficient directional and
depth rank.  None is silently credited as completed here.

\section{Conclusion}

The current MES-bound framework is now an explicit, typed chain rather than a
scalar-to-physics shortcut.  Low-order temperature powers yield one-way,
premise-conditioned squared ceilings.  Those ceilings can normalize compatible
amplitudes but cannot create direction or independent information.  Actual
directional morphology is carried by the measured harmonic blocks and their
reversible STF representatives.  A physical interpretation remains a separate
inverse problem whose response and rank are not yet available.

On the paired 300-row primary, the scalar MES-squared family has legacy rank
$98/301$ and all-two-sided ECDF rank $148/301$; the physics-oriented ECDF tail
companion is $93/301$, and the two MES anchors are individually $74/301$.
The frame-free observer-space family has $27/301$ under both
reducers, with local minima $4/301$ in two mixed contractions.  In the
separate 999-row CMB-only pool, the frame-free ranks are $61/1000$ and
$55/1000$.  Under exact product replacement, the PR3 component frame-free
absolute-median/ECDF ranks are SMICA $27/27$, Commander $46/41$, NILC $27/27$,
and SEVEM $81/40$, all over 301.  WMAP9 ILC transports the SMICA tensor shape
closely, whereas CosmoGlobe and BeyondPlanck show larger shifts.  A fixed
13-template foreground bank gives $144/301$, so the SMICA $Q/O$ morphology is
not exceptionally aligned with a single admitted foreground template under
the predeclared necessary-condition test.

The registered observation-free intervention evaluates 863,040 positive
carrier injections across two overlapping evaluation arms.  Their cellwise
power is closely stable, while the response varies strongly with template,
family, reducer, and combiner.  The mixed-axial alternative is especially
visible to the frame-free irrep family, whereas the ECDF/min-local-$p$ MES-10
functional is completely conservative at denominator 201.  Exact integer-
product scores recover substantial response in many cells.  These statements
characterize the registered 200-reference method-power design only.

These results establish coordinate-, reducer-, frame-, null-ensemble-, and
component-product sensitivity within precisely specified finite pools.  They
do not establish a detection, complete foreground validation, a physical
kinematic state, a local/global origin, or a Bianchi family.
