\begin{abstract}
\noindent This report summarises the current state of the BASS/HTT
FLRW-departure programme and sets out the plan for the real-data analyses that
can be run \emph{before} a native low-$\ell$ anisotropic Boltzmann solver
(``BASS'') exists. The programme delivers a \emph{diagnostic, provenance-bound}
comparator over four anisotropy sectors $g=(\Sig,\Wsq,\Otilt,\Ok)$, a suite of
conditional EGS-type (Ehlers--Geren--Sachs) theorems, and a growing set of
model-independent real-data descriptors. Since the previous revision it has been
upgraded on two fronts that close a genuine vulnerability of the earlier plan.
\emph{(i)~Axis~C:} a local observer boost leaks an $O(\beta^2)$ kinematic
quadrupole into the shear sector and could fake a $\Sig>0$; a closed-form
\textbf{kinematic deprojection} $\Sigt=\Sig-\alpha\,(\Otilt)^2$ now makes the
low-$\ell$ shear reading provably immune to it (gate- and Wolfram-verified).
\emph{(ii)~Axis~D:} the proposed joint likelihood
$\ln\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{Total}}=\ln\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{PV}}+\ln\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CMB}}$
is critically evaluated and ported to what is honestly runnable now --- a
\textbf{feasible correlated-covariance bulk-flow (tilt) analysis} on real CF4
(low-rank Woodbury, not a dense $38\text{k}\times38\text{k}$ inversion), a
\textbf{real off-diagonal BipoSH statistical-isotropy measurement} on the Planck
SMICA map, and a \textbf{JWST distance-anchor acquisition pipeline} whose prior,
through the Axis-C coupled Fisher, breaks the $\Sig$--$\Otilt$ degeneracy. The
CMB \emph{theory} sector --- the mode-coupled $C_{\ell m,\ell'm'}(g)$ prediction
for a Bianchi $g$ --- is the native solver and is kept \textbf{fail-closed}. The
programme does \emph{not} establish a Bianchi-family identification, an
anisotropic geometry, a native-solver validation, a physical-vorticity
detection, or a globally significant low-$\ell$ anomaly. The Planck-raw E2E
analysis of \S\ref{sec:plan} remains the matched null that would upgrade the K1
morphology statistic; its full pipeline is implemented and validated, awaiting
only the manual $\sim$1\,TB download.
\end{abstract}

\section{Programme and framework}\label{sec:framework}

The programme tests whether the cosmic microwave background (CMB) and
low-redshift peculiar velocities depart from the standard isotropic FLRW
cosmology toward an anisotropic (Bianchi $+$ tilt) one. It deliberately works
\emph{without} a native low-$\ell$ anisotropic Boltzmann solver (which does not
yet exist in-tree), so it makes no family-identification or geometry-detection
claim. The primary object is the \textbf{graded comparator vector}
\begin{equation}
  g = (\Sig,\ \Wsq,\ \Otilt,\ \Ok) \in \mathbb{R}^4 ,
  \qquad x_C = \langle c, g\rangle,\ \ c=(+1,-1,+1,+1),
\end{equation}
where $\Sig$ is the shear, $\Wsq$ the vorticity, $\Otilt$ the tilt, and $\Ok$
the anisotropic-curvature sector; the signed scalar $x_C$ is a \emph{derived}
linear summary, so the per-sector content is preserved and the
sign-cancellation pathology of a single number is avoided. An equivalent
representation casts the comparator as a positive-semidefinite matrix
$M=\mathrm{diag}(g)\succeq 0$ with $x_C=\operatorname{tr}(CM)$ (bit-identical),
whose admissible set is a convex PSD cone and whose reachable eigen-directions
encode identifiability. Two velocity-like sectors --- the observer's local boost
$\beta$ (an $\ell=1$ frame effect, \emph{not} a sector of $g$) and the global
tilt $\Otilt$ (a genuine bulk flow) --- overlap on the sky; keeping them apart is
the subject of Axis~C (\S\ref{sec:axisc}).

\paragraph{Honest claim envelope.} All results are diagnostic-only /
model-independent / conditional-theorem content. No data-fitting headline
exceeds the registry-and-shear-source-validated envelope; the eight Bianchi
families beyond \{FLRW, I, V, IX\} are restricted to axis-aligned mode subsets,
and any family/geometry statement remains gated behind the (unbuilt) native
solver and its full gate bundle. $\Sig$ on the real sky stays \code{partial}.

\section{Local boost vs global tilt: the kinematic deprojection (Axis C)}
\label{sec:axisc}

\paragraph{The vulnerability.} A naive low-$\ell$ estimator that reads all
quadrupole power as shear is not safe once the observer moves. With
$\mu=\hat{\mathbf n}\!\cdot\!\hat{\mathbf v}$, the Doppler/aberration transform of
the monopole $T_0$ carries $\mu^2=\tfrac23P_2(\mu)+\tfrac13$, so a boost
$\beta$ deposits an $O(\beta^2)$ \emph{kinematic quadrupole} aligned with the
boost axis. A bulk-flow-only universe ($\Sig=0$) would then read a false
$\Sig>0$, and the graded-comparator covariance is \emph{not} diagonal:
$F_{\Sig,\Otilt}\propto\beta^2\neq0$.

\paragraph{The fix.} Because the tilt is linear in the velocity
($\Otilt=\kappa_T\beta$), the boost quadrupole equals $\alpha(\Otilt)^2$ at the
quadrupole-amplitude level, with $\alpha$ a $\beta$-independent constant of the
registered response,
\begin{equation}
  \alpha=\frac{4}{9}\,\frac{T_0^2\,N_2}{\kappa_T^2\,R_\Sigma},
  \qquad
  \Sigt=\Sig-\alpha\,(\Otilt)^2 ,
\end{equation}
so $\Sigt\to0$ on a pure-bulk-flow sky and $\Sigt=\Sig$ when $\Otilt=0$ (no
over-subtraction). The physical origin is the $\varepsilon_1^2$ dipole-squared
term of the boost kernel (coefficient $+1$ in the registered
$\varepsilon$-normalisation). This is a \emph{separate diagnostic surface}
(\code{htt/obsstat/egs3\_kinematic\_deprojection.py}): it never touches the
bit-identical comparator $x_C$ or the frozen registered low-$\ell$ statistics,
and adds no new registered statistic. The content is gate-verified (EGS3-C1--C4)
and the closed-form identities Wolfram-verified
(\code{wolfram/egs3\_boost\_tilt\_separation.wls}, 11/11):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Coupled Fisher (C2).} $F_{\Sig,\Otilt}\propto\beta^2$; the $\Sig$
variance inflates by $1/(1-r^2)$ ($r$ the response correlation), returning to
unity as $\beta\to0$.
\item \textbf{Identifiability (C3).} The boost and shear quadrupoles separate
whenever their two $\ell=2$ templates are independent: the Gram determinant
$1-P_2(\cos\angle)^2$ is positive unless the boost axis is aligned with the
shear principal axis (then rank drops and $\Sigt$ over-subtracts).
\item \textbf{Injection-recovery witness (C4).} A deterministic moment-level
experiment (no map, no solver, no real data) shows the naive $\Sig$
false-positive rate rises to $\approx 1$ on a pure-boost sky while the
deprojected $\Sigt$ stays at its nominal level ($\approx 0.05$), and genuine
shear is recovered unbiased and covered.
\end{itemize}
This is a proven \emph{estimator property}, not a detection: $\Sig$ on the real
sky stays \code{partial}, and $\Sigt$ supplies a boost-immune shear reading when
data lands.

\section{BASS-Extended joint PV$+$CMB analysis (Axis D)}\label{sec:axisd}

The proposed joint likelihood
$\ln\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{Total}}=\ln\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{PV}}+\ln\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CMB}}$
pins $\Otilt$ from peculiar velocities so the CMB inversion cannot leak power
into a false $\Sig$. Its CMB \emph{theory} sector is the native low-$\ell$
solver; the rest is ported honestly and runs now.

\subsection{Feasible peculiar-velocity covariance (D1)}
A dense $N\times N$ peculiar-velocity covariance is infeasible for the
$\sim$38k CF4 groups ($N^2\!\sim\!1.4\times10^9$, $O(N^3)$ inversion). The
coherent velocity field is dominated by a few large-scale modes, so
\begin{equation}
  C_{\mathrm{PV}}=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_v^2)+U\Lambda U^{\mathsf T},
  \qquad U\in\mathbb{R}^{N\times K},\ K\ll N
\end{equation}
(the leading bulk$+$shear velocity modes) is inverted by
Sherman--Morrison--Woodbury in $O(NK^2)$
(\code{htt/obsstat/pv\_covariance.py}; verified bit-for-bit against the dense
solve). On the real CF4 catalogue this correlated-covariance bulk-flow GLS
reproduces the K5 amplitude $|\mathbf B|\simeq 341\pm 5\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$
(measurement error; the cosmic-variance budget is separate). A full off-diagonal
$\xi_{ij}$ from a velocity power spectrum or a Wiener-filter field ensemble is a
standing gate (\code{BLOCKED\_MISSING\_FIELD\_REALIZATIONS}); the leading
bulk$+$shear model is the honest, feasible form.

\subsection{JWST distance-anchor prior (D1, forecast)}
A JWST-anchored distance prior --- the CF4 groups cross-matched to published
JWST distances (CCHP TRGB/JAGB, SH0ES Cepheids, TRGB-SBF) --- shrinks the
anchors' distance errors and tightens $\Otilt$. A download pipeline
(\code{dl\_pipeline/scripts/download\_jwst\_anchors.py}, mirroring the CF4
acquisition pattern) fetches the authoritative IOPscience/arXiv tables; a
committed cited seed keeps the analysis runnable offline, and
\code{scripts/jwst\_cf4\_crossmatch.py} matches the anchors to CF4 (9 of 12
seed galaxies matched). Because these are nearby SN-host galaxies --- which
already have small peculiar-velocity errors --- the current
$\sim$10-anchor \emph{global}-tilt gain is modest ($\sim$4\%). This is reported
honestly, as a labelled survey-design \emph{forecast}, with the gain scaling
shown against anchor fraction; it is not a measurement.

\subsection{Coupled-Fisher degeneracy break (D2)}
The tighter $\Otilt$ precision enters the Axis-C coupled Fisher $f_{\Otilt}$
diagonal and \emph{strictly} reduces the $\Sig$ covariance inflation
$1/(1-r^2)$ --- the solver-free, Wolfram-verified realization of ``PV pins
$\Otilt$ so the boost cannot masquerade as shear''
(\code{prior\_precision\_reduces\_inflation}, PASS).

\subsection{Real off-diagonal BipoSH measurement on SMICA (D3)}
The off-diagonal $\langle a_{\ell m}a^{*}_{\ell'm'}\rangle$ signal \emph{is} a
real single-sky observable as the rotationally-invariant Bipolar
Spherical Harmonic power $D^L_{\ell\ell'}$
(\code{htt/obsstat/biposh\_smica.py}; exact Wigner-3j contraction). $L{=}1$ is
the observer-boost/aberration $\ell\!\leftrightarrow\!\ell{+}1$ coupling; $L{=}2$
is the quadrupolar statistical-isotropy (SI) violation a global tilt or shear
would source. Measured on the real Planck SMICA and Commander maps
(\code{scripts/k1\_biposh\_smica.py}) and look-elsewhere-corrected against a
matched isotropic GRF null (500 realizations), the global $p$-values
($\simeq0.68$ SMICA / $0.65$ Commander) are \emph{consistent with isotropy}.
This is the honest sense in which the off-diagonal $C_{\ell m,\ell'm'}$ is
extracted from SMICA: the \emph{data} off-diagonal is measured, while the
\emph{theory} $A^{LM}_{\ell\ell'}(g)$ prediction for a Bianchi $g$ stays
fail-closed. The FFP10/NPIPE E2E null (\S\ref{sec:plan}) is the pending upgrade.

\subsection{The theory sector is fail-closed (D4)}
The mode-coupled $C_{\ell m,\ell'm'}(g)$ / $A^{LM}_{\ell\ell'}(g)$ prediction for
a proposed Bianchi $g$ requires the native low-$\ell$ solver
(\code{AWAITING\_NATIVE\_LOWELL\_SOLVER}). It is \textbf{fail-closed}:
\code{joint\_pv\_cmb\_forecast.anisotropic\_cmb\_covariance} raises
\code{OutOfScopeError} and never fabricates a covariance; the drop-in point is
\code{bass.spectrum.cl\_assembly.off\_diagonal\_biposh}. Accordingly
\code{evaluate\_joint\_loglike} returns a real PV log-likelihood and the real
BipoSH data statistic but \emph{withholds} any $g$-conditioned joint scalar, so
the blocked sector is never silently zeroed. The solver-free substitute for the
CMB side is the data-side BipoSH measurement (D3).

\section{Real-data status}\label{sec:data}

Real-data descriptors are computed on owned inputs; all are model-independent and
carry no family/geometry/anisotropy-evidence claim.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{K5 / D1 (CF4 bulk flow).} A weighted-GLS estimator on the real
Cosmicflows-4 group release (Tully et al.\ 2023, $38{,}049$ groups) gives
$|\mathbf{B}|\simeq 341\pm 102\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$, the error budget
cosmic-variance-dominated --- high but not anomalous. The feasible
correlated-covariance (Woodbury) tilt of \S\ref{sec:axisd} reproduces this
amplitude. Coverage ($0.67$ vs $0.19$ measurement-only) is
geometry-and-error matched and \emph{conditional} on a fixed $\Lambda$CDM
$\sigma_{\mathrm{cv}}=150\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$ per-component prior.
\item \textbf{K6 (CF4 curl/vorticity).} The affine velocity-gradient
decomposition of the real CF4{+}{+} Wiener-filter field returns a vorticity at
most $0.55\%$ of the shear at every radius, with an injected solid-body rotation
recovered to machine precision. This is a WF mean-field curl-suppression
\emph{structural no-go}, not a vorticity measurement.
\item \textbf{K1 (low-$\ell$ CMB morphology).} Stacking six registered low-$\ell$
statistics on the real Planck PR3 maps into a frozen max-scan gives a
look-elsewhere-corrected global $p=0.097$ (SMICA) / $0.121$ (Commander) under an
isotropic $\Lambda$CDM (GRF) null over $2000$ realizations. The $\sim$25\% method
dependence is itself a foreground/cleaning systematic; the two are reported side
by side and \emph{not} averaged. \emph{This is the measurement the Planck-raw
plan of \S\ref{sec:plan} upgrades.}
\item \textbf{D3 (low-$\ell$ CMB off-diagonal BipoSH).} A companion real-data
channel to K1 that reads the \emph{off-diagonal} $\ell$--$\ell'$ structure
directly (\S\ref{sec:axisd}); SMICA/Commander global $p\simeq0.68/0.65$,
consistent with isotropy.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Joint comparator (PR08-006).} Assembling the discharges into $g$ on
real data gives a data rank of two: $\Otilt$ is measured (K5 bulk flow) and
$\Sig$ is partial (K1 look-elsewhere only), while $\Wsq$ and $\Ok$ are
fail-closed (K6 no-go; no leading-order channel) --- never zeroed. No combined
scalar $x_C$ is formed, no MIO certificate is used as odds, and no scalar is
promoted to a family. The rank-2 count is \emph{one fully measured plus one
partial} sector, not two measured sectors.

\section{Planck raw-data analysis plan}\label{sec:plan}

\subsection{Objective and the standing blocker}
The K1 look-elsewhere correction is discharged on the real map under an
idealised isotropic $\Lambda$CDM (GRF) null. What remains
(\code{BLOCKED\_MISSING\_PR4\_E2E\_ACCESS}) is to replace that null with a
\textbf{matched end-to-end (E2E) simulation ensemble} carrying real instrument
noise, residual foregrounds, and the component-separation transfer, so that both
the K1 morphology statistics \emph{and} the D3 BipoSH statistics are reported
with instrument-and-foreground systematics. The ensemble is served only through
the Planck Legacy Archive (PLA) interactive query portal (and
NERSC-authenticated paths), not as a plain-URL download; \code{cobaya} provides
the Blackwell--Rao $C_\ell$-level low-$\ell$ likelihood, not the $a_{\ell m}$ map
ensemble the morphology and BipoSH statistics require. The acquisition is
therefore manual; everything downstream is implemented.

\subsection{Data products and acquisition}
The recommended route (matching the SMICA observed map) is the FFP10
component-separated simulation library:
\begin{itemize}
\item \code{dx12\_v3\_smica\_cmb\_mc\_<00000..00999>\_raw.fits} --- nominally 1000 CMB
(signal) MC, of which \textbf{999 are usable}: realization \code{00970} is a known
missing/corrupt file on the PLA (ESA/PLA confirmation pending);
\item \code{dx12\_v3\_smica\_noise\_mc\_<00000..00299>\_raw.fits} --- 300 noise/systematics MC.
\end{itemize}
The E2E null is CMB$+$noise, paired \emph{by parsed MC id},
$\mathrm{cmb}[\mathrm{id}]+\mathrm{noise}[\mathrm{id}\bmod n_{\mathrm{noise}}]$;
id-based pairing is used so the missing \code{00970} leaves a single absent
realization rather than misaligning every later pairing. The ensemble is
therefore the \textbf{PLA-available} set (999 usable CMB MC $+$ 300 noise MC),
not all 1000. At full IQU $N_{\mathrm{side}}=2048$ this is $\sim$1\,TB; a
dedicated 2\,TB nvme accommodates the full set. Raw maps stay outside version
control; only compact summaries and provenance are committed.

\subsection{Pipeline and runner modes}
The frozen pipeline is identical to the observed-map path: read each simulation
(temperature only), downgrade-on-read to the analysis $N_{\mathrm{side}}$, apply
the matched low-$\ell$ mask, compute the six registered morphology statistics
(and, for the D3 channel, the $L{=}1,2$ BipoSH powers), and feed the observed
vector and the simulation matrix to the look-elsewhere max-scan, which returns
the $+1$-corrected global rank $p$. Processing is streaming (one map at a time),
so disk and wall-time, not RAM, are the constraints. Three modes in
\code{scripts/k1\_global\_maxscan.py} each write a \emph{separate} artifact so
the canonical GRF result is untouched (Route~4 noise-only; Route~A full E2E, the
exit gate; and a re-registered v2 precision set adding proc-$N_{\mathrm{side}}$,
$\ell_{\max}$, and a mask$+$inpaint). The BipoSH channel
(\code{scripts/k1\_biposh\_smica.py}) reuses the same GRF-now / FFP10-pending
null path and the same frozen statistic set.

\subsection{Higher-precision (v2) statistic set}
On a 12-core/64\,GB workstation the analysis is I/O-bound, so precision can be
raised at essentially the same wall-time. The v2 set
(\code{htt/obsstat/lowell\_precision.py}) is a deliberately re-registered
statistic set with three levers: processing $N_{\mathrm{side}}$ (default 64; the
ceiling for $\ell\le 8$, where $N_{\mathrm{side}}=16$ is Nyquist-sufficient and
the only gain is removing the pixel-window suppression, Table~\ref{tab:pixwin});
$\ell_{\max}$ (default 30; the $\ell$-summed statistics gain genuine information,
the quadrupole--octupole alignments are intrinsically $\ell=2,3$); and a galactic
mask $+$ diffuse inpainting applied \emph{identically} to the observed map and
every simulation, so the mask response is absorbed into the null.

\subsection{Compute budget}
Parallelism (\code{--jobs}) fans the per-simulation loop over processes
(\code{spawn} context, one thread per worker, avoiding the fork-after-OpenMP
deadlock); the noise set is pre-downgraded once and cached. The measured per-map
cost at v2 is $\approx 3$\,s; the PLA-available $999{+}300$ ensemble is I/O-bound
(a $\sim$3.5\,min nvme read floor), giving $\sim$5--7\,min wall-time on a Ryzen
5900X with \code{--jobs 12}. Peak memory is $\approx 1$\,GB per worker, so 12
workers use $\approx 12$\,GB --- well within 64\,GB. No additional RAM is
required.

\subsection{Exit gate and kill switches}
K1 re-points \code{measured\_partial}$\to$\code{measured} only through the ledger
generator, with a global rank $p$ under the \emph{matched} E2E ensemble; a
configuration hash bound to \{statistic list, mask, beam, $\ell$-range, method\};
an ensemble provenance manifest; and both component-separation methods reported
side by side. The run stops if the map/mask/beam path differs between observed
and simulations, if methods are mixed without labels, if fewer simulations are
available than declared, if the statistic set is unfrozen, or if any text would
claim E2E global significance without the matched provenance.

\subsection{Co-evolution with the native low-$\ell$ solver}
The v2 analysis uses $\ell=2$--$30$; for internal consistency the future native
low-$\ell$ atlas should predict the \emph{same} statistics over the same range

separate (PR10) work and grants no detection authority by itself; the theory-$g$

\section{Claim envelope}\label{sec:envelope}
The defensible envelope is a diagnostic methods-and-calibration framework: a
rank-aware, provenance-bound sector comparator; a boost-immune shear estimator
with a proven false-positive-rate and a coupled-Fisher error model; conditional

bulk-flow analysis; and real, null-calibrated low-$\ell$ CMB descriptors

significant low-$\ell$ anomaly, an anisotropic geometry, a Bianchi-family
identification, a native-solver validation, or a physical-vorticity detection;
no diagnostic is used as posterior odds, no scalar is promoted to a family, the
theory-$g$ CMB likelihood is fail-closed, the JWST prior is a labelled forecast,
and $\Sig$ stays \code{partial}. The Planck-raw E2E analysis narrows the K1/D3
systematics gap; it does not change these boundaries.
