\begin{abstract}
\noindent This report summarises the current state of the BASS/HTT
FLRW-departure programme and sets out, in detail, the plan for analysing the
Planck raw-data (end-to-end simulation) ensemble that closes the one remaining
real-data gating measurement (K1). The programme delivers a \emph{diagnostic,
provenance-bound} comparator over four anisotropy sectors
$g=(\Sig,\Wsq,\Otilt,\Ok)$, a suite of conditional EGS-type
(Ehlers--Geren--Sachs) theorems, and three model-independent real-data
descriptors (CF4 bulk flow, CF4 curl no-go, low-$\ell$ CMB morphology). It does
\emph{not} establish a Bianchi-family identification, an anisotropic geometry, a
native low-$\ell$ solver validation, a physical-vorticity detection, or a
globally significant low-$\ell$ anomaly. The Planck-raw analysis described in
\S\ref{sec:plan} is the matched end-to-end (E2E) null that would upgrade the K1
low-$\ell$ morphology statistic from a $\Lambda$CDM-null look-elsewhere result to
an instrument-and-foreground-calibrated one; the full pipeline, three runner
modes, and a higher-precision (v2) statistic set are implemented and validated,
awaiting only the manual download of the $\sim$1\,TB simulation set.
\end{abstract}

\section{Programme and framework}\label{sec:framework}

The programme tests whether the cosmic microwave background (CMB) and
low-redshift peculiar velocities depart from the standard isotropic FLRW
cosmology toward an anisotropic (Bianchi $+$ tilt) one. It deliberately works
\emph{without} a native low-$\ell$ anisotropic Boltzmann solver (which does not
yet exist in-tree), so it makes no family-identification or geometry-detection
claim. Instead, the primary object is the \textbf{graded comparator vector}
\begin{equation}
  g = (\Sig,\ \Wsq,\ \Otilt,\ \Ok) \in \mathbb{R}^4 ,
  \qquad x_C = \langle c, g\rangle,\ \ c=(+1,-1,+1,+1),
\end{equation}
where $\Sig$ is the shear, $\Wsq$ the vorticity, $\Otilt$ the tilt, and $\Ok$
the anisotropic-curvature sector; the signed scalar $x_C$ is a \emph{derived}
linear summary, so the per-sector content is preserved and the
sign-cancellation pathology of a single number is avoided. An equivalent
representation casts the comparator as a positive-semidefinite matrix
$M=\mathrm{diag}(g)\succeq 0$ with $x_C=\operatorname{tr}(CM)$ (bit-identical),
whose admissible set is a convex PSD cone and whose reachable eigen-directions
encode identifiability.

\paragraph{Honest claim envelope.} All results are diagnostic-only /
model-independent / conditional-theorem content. No data-fitting headline
exceeds the registry-and-shear-source-validated envelope; the eight Bianchi
families beyond \{FLRW, I, V, IX\} are restricted to axis-aligned mode subsets,
and any family/geometry statement remains gated behind the (unbuilt) native
solver and its full gate bundle.

\paragraph{Sector-null precision (a key refinement).} The two unreachable
sectors are \emph{not} the same kind of null. $\Wsq$ is a genuine,
order-independent \emph{structural} null: radial peculiar velocities satisfy

\emph{beyond} leading order (higher-order ISW, lensing, the native transfer)
within the same channels.

\section{Real-data status}\label{sec:data}

Three real-data descriptors are computed on owned inputs; all are
model-independent and carry no family/geometry/anisotropy-evidence claim.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{K5 (CF4 bulk flow).} A weighted-GLS estimator on the real
Cosmicflows-4 group release (Tully et al.\ 2023, $38{,}049$ groups) gives a
bulk-flow amplitude $|\mathbf{B}|\simeq 341\pm 102\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$, the
error budget cosmic-variance-dominated; this is consistent with the
$\Lambda$CDM expectation ($\sim$150--250\,$\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$ at the effective
depth), i.e.\ high but not anomalous. The cosmic-variance-inclusive coverage
($0.67$ vs $0.19$ measurement-only) is computed with geometry-and-error matched
Gaussian mock mechanics and is \emph{conditional} on a fixed $\Lambda$CDM
$\sigma_{\mathrm{cv}}=150\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$ per-component prior; full
selection/Malmquist/grouping/correlated-field mocks remain a standing gate.
\item \textbf{K6 (CF4 curl/vorticity).} The affine velocity-gradient
decomposition of the real CF4{+}{+} Wiener-filter field returns a vorticity at
most $0.55\%$ of the shear at every radius, while an injected solid-body
rotation is recovered to machine precision. This is a WF mean-field
curl-suppression \emph{structural no-go} (the field is curl-suppressed by the
Wiener-filter prior across modes), not a vorticity measurement; a true
constrained-realization posterior remains a standing gate.
\item \textbf{K1 (low-$\ell$ CMB morphology).} Stacking six registered low-$\ell$
statistics on the real Planck PR3 maps into a frozen max-scan gives a
look-elsewhere-corrected global $p=0.097$ (SMICA) / $0.121$ (Commander) under an
isotropic $\Lambda$CDM (GRF) null over $2000$ realizations. The
$\sim$25\% method dependence is itself a foreground/cleaning systematic; the two
are reported side by side and \emph{not} averaged, and neither is
E2E-calibrated. The local single-statistic $p$ are: parity asymmetry $0.021$,
parity even/odd $0.021$, planarity $0.045$, $S_{1/2}$ $0.060$, Q--O alignment
$0.67$, axis-to-dipole $0.71$. \emph{This is the measurement the Planck-raw plan
of \S\ref{sec:plan} upgrades.}
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Joint comparator (PR08-006).} Assembling the discharges into $g$ on
real data gives a data rank of two: $\Otilt$ is measured (K5 bulk flow) and
$\Sig$ is partial (K1 look-elsewhere only), while $\Wsq$ and $\Ok$ are
fail-closed (K6 no-go; no leading-order channel) --- never zeroed. No combined
scalar $x_C$ is formed (two sectors are blind), no MIO certificate is used as
odds, and no scalar is promoted to a family. The rank-2 count is therefore
\emph{one fully measured plus one partial} sector, not two measured sectors.

\section{Planck raw-data analysis plan}\label{sec:plan}

\subsection{Objective and the standing blocker}
The K1 look-elsewhere correction is discharged on the real map under an
idealised isotropic $\Lambda$CDM (GRF) null. What remains
(\code{BLOCKED\_MISSING\_PR4\_E2E\_ACCESS}) is to replace that null with a
\textbf{matched end-to-end (E2E) simulation ensemble} carrying real instrument
noise, residual foregrounds, and the component-separation transfer, so that the
global $p$ is reported with instrument-and-foreground systematics. The
ensemble is served only through the Planck Legacy Archive (PLA) interactive
query portal (and NERSC-authenticated paths), not as a plain-URL download;
\code{cobaya} was checked and provides the Blackwell--Rao $C_\ell$-level low-$\ell$
likelihood, not the $a_{\ell m}$ map ensemble the morphology statistics require.
The acquisition is therefore manual; everything downstream is implemented.

\subsection{Data products and acquisition}
The recommended route (matching the SMICA observed map) is the FFP10
component-separated simulation library:
\begin{itemize}
\item \code{dx12\_v3\_smica\_cmb\_mc\_<00000..00999>\_raw.fits} --- nominally 1000 CMB
(signal) MC, of which \textbf{999 are usable}: realization \code{00970} is a known
missing/corrupt file on the PLA (ESA/PLA confirmation pending);
\item \code{dx12\_v3\_smica\_noise\_mc\_<00000..00299>\_raw.fits} --- 300 noise/systematics MC.
\end{itemize}
The E2E null is CMB$+$noise, paired \emph{by parsed MC id},
$\mathrm{cmb}[\mathrm{id}]+\mathrm{noise}[\mathrm{id}\bmod n_{\mathrm{noise}}]$
(the 300 noise MC cycled across the CMB set, matching the Planck-2018 permutation
scheme). Id-based pairing is used precisely so the missing \code{00970} leaves a
single absent realization rather than misaligning every later pairing. The
ensemble is therefore the \textbf{PLA-available} set (999 usable CMB MC $+$ 300
noise MC), not all 1000. At full IQU $N_{\mathrm{side}}=2048$ this is
$\sim$1\,TB; a dedicated 2\,TB nvme accommodates the full set. The observed maps and common temperature mask are already local.
Raw maps stay outside version control; only compact summaries and provenance are
committed. A per-simulation manifest records map IDs, URLs, SHA-256, beam, input
$N_{\mathrm{side}}$, analysis $N_{\mathrm{side}}$, mask, and method.

\subsection{Pipeline}
The frozen pipeline is identical to the observed-map path: read each simulation
(temperature field only), downgrade-on-read to the analysis $N_{\mathrm{side}}$,
apply the matched low-$\ell$ mask, compute the six registered statistics
(\code{s\_one\_half}, \code{parity\_even\_over\_odd\_ratio},
\code{parity\_asymmetry}, \code{planarity\_mean}, \code{qo\_axis\_alignment\_deg},
\code{axis\_to\_cmb\_dipole\_deg}), and feed the observed 6-vector and the
simulation $N\times 6$ matrix to the look-elsewhere max-scan, which returns the
$+1$-corrected global rank $p$-value (global $\ge$ min-local). Processing is
streaming: peak memory is one map at a time, so disk and wall-time --- not
RAM --- are the constraints.

\subsection{Higher-precision (v2) statistic set}
On a 12-core/64\,GB workstation the analysis is I/O-bound, so precision can be
raised at essentially the same wall-time. The v2 set
(\code{htt/obsstat/lowell\_precision.py}) is a deliberately \emph{re-registered}
statistic set (own configuration hash and \code{statistic\_set} tag) with three
levers:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Processing $N_{\mathrm{side}}$ (default 64).} Raising
$N_{\mathrm{side}}$ does \emph{not} add information at $\ell\le 8$
($N_{\mathrm{side}}=16$ is Nyquist-sufficient); the only lever there is removing
the pixel-window suppression (Table~\ref{tab:pixwin}). $N_{\mathrm{side}}=64$ is
the ceiling for $\ell\le 8$; higher values help only if $\ell_{\max}$ is raised.
\item \textbf{$\ell_{\max}$ (default 30).} The $\ell$-summed statistics
($S_{1/2}$, parity, planarity) gain genuine information from more multipoles;
the quadrupole--octupole \emph{alignment} statistics are intrinsically
$\ell=2,3$ and are unchanged.
\item \textbf{Galactic mask $+$ diffuse inpainting.} The common temperature mask
is applied and the masked region diffuse-inpainted before the spherical-harmonic
transform, \emph{identically} on the observed map and every simulation, so the
mask response is absorbed into the null and the look-elsewhere $p$-value stays
valid (no per-statistic deconvolution is claimed). The observed vector is taken
from the full-resolution observed map, processed identically.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Compute budget}
Parallelism (\code{--jobs}) fans the per-simulation loop over processes
(\code{spawn} context with one thread per worker, avoiding the
fork-after-OpenMP deadlock); the noise set is pre-downgraded once and cached.
The measured per-map cost at the v2 configuration is $\approx 3$\,s; the
PLA-available 999$+$300 ensemble is I/O-bound (a $\sim$3.5\,min nvme read floor), giving the
wall-times of Table~\ref{tab:walltime}. Peak memory is $\approx 1$\,GB per
worker, so 12 workers use $\approx 12$\,GB and 24 use $\approx 24$\,GB --- well
within 64\,GB. No additional RAM is required.

\subsection{Exit gate and kill switches}
K1 re-points \code{measured\_partial}$\to$\code{measured} only through the
ledger generator, with: a global rank $p$ under the \emph{matched} E2E ensemble;
a configuration hash bound to \{statistic list, mask, beam, $\ell$-range,
method\}; an ensemble provenance manifest; and both component-separation methods
reported side by side. The run stops if the map/mask/beam path differs between
observed and simulations, if methods are mixed without labels, if fewer
simulations are available than declared, if the statistic set is unfrozen, or if
any text would claim E2E global significance without the matched provenance.

\subsection{Co-evolution with the native low-$\ell$ solver}
The v2 data analysis uses $\ell=2$--$30$. For internal consistency, the future
native low-$\ell$ atlas should predict the \emph{same} statistics over the same
range: an atlas restricted to $\ell\le 6$ cannot be scored against an
$\ell=2$--$30$ morphology statistic, and Bianchi families (notably the
$\mathrm{VII}_h$ spiral) diverge most at intermediate $\ell$, where the
discriminating power lives. The shear Fisher floor also keeps improving to
$\ell\sim 20$ before saturating. The recommended atlas ceiling is therefore
$\ell=2$--$30$ (staged: $\ell\le 20$ for $\sim$90\% of the discrimination and
floor gain, then $\ell\le 30$ for full atlas--data parity), conditioned on the
exact-FLRW-first gate and per-$\ell$ convergence. This remains separate
(PR10) work and does not, by itself, grant any detection authority.

\section{Claim envelope}\label{sec:envelope}
The defensible envelope is a diagnostic methods-and-calibration framework: a
rank-aware, provenance-bound sector comparator; conditional EGS-type theorems
under stated hypotheses; and three model-independent real-data descriptors. It
does \emph{not} establish a globally significant low-$\ell$ anomaly, an
anisotropic geometry, a Bianchi-family identification, a native-solver
validation, or a physical-vorticity detection; no diagnostic is used as
posterior odds and no scalar is promoted to a family. The Planck-raw E2E
analysis narrows the K1 systematics gap; it does not change these boundaries.
