# Negative and limiting results

1. At nonzero tilt, free beta timejet yields unbounded acceleration/shear and some expansion/vorticity directions in the admitted geometric jet family. Joint nuisance-free combinations survive.
2. Unrestricted dipole/quadrupole coefficient timejets span all eight retained radiation residuals. Static multipole values plus those equations alone do not supply an added kinematic bound in that algebraic model.
3. Deterministic feasible set does not determine a probability law or Pi tail.
4. Physical compatibility and actual observed MES fraction remain unresolved.
5. MGE Eq71 contains a sign inconsistent with its Eq70 integration and Eq89 shear term. The adopted R5 results avoid that term.
